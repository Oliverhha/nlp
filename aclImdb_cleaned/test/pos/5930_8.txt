was now up to cena & batista. first after reversing an f-u, batista went for the batista bomb but suddenly both men crumbled on the outside at the same time. after a controversial decision & mr. mcmahon making his way to the ring to settle the matter. the match was restarted again as batista & cena battled it out, but batista got the better out of cena, nailed a spine-buster & threw cena out
