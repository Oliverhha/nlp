man, was i disappointed. 1) adam arkin is more whiny than ross geller from 'friends' 2) a great cast is wasted (kenneth mars, alan arkin, ed mcmahon, pat morita, louis nye) with this amateurish script. 3) the movie suffers from horrible pacing. it jumps around through in a jumbled, confusing manner. 4) the story doesn't even make sense. why does he want to break the football streak? what about the stupid violin music? none of it is explained. 5) it's not even funny. it's like a bunch of accountants trying to do improv, saying "lookit me! lookit me i'm being funny!" this was a bad attempt at making another "love at first bite". i like larry cohen movies, but man he failed here. i couldn't wait for the credits to roll. horribly disappointed.
