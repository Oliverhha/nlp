one of the very worst slasher films ever made; a ski-masked killer is doing away with vacuous coeds for a reason that never really becomes clear. not even a spark of originality and every girl who dies struts around naked despite the obvious presence of the maniac in the house / woods / room. the only notable thing is a marginal appearance by kevin costner as a boyfriend who everyone thinks is the killer (but probably isn't if you've seen a few of these). suspiciously, his name is elided completely from the film itself, and the way the end credits roll has to be seen to be believed. awful.
