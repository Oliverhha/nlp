i gave this a one because imdb doesn't offer a zero-option. less than zero would be closer to the truth. in what at first at least appears to be a bad rip-off of the saw movies, which weren't that great to start with, we find four women in the situation of being forced together with no memories and a lot of unpleasantness ahead of them. from there it goes downhill. zero plot for most of the movie, completely forgettable and unlikeable characters, a horrific soundtrack that had me reducing the volume to barely audible, and nearly complete predictability. no surprises at all at the end. thinking of watching this one? go watch paint dry while pondering your navel. the time spent will be more productive.
