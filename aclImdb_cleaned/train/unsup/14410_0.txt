this is my first cassavetes film (as a director) and i red all the reviews warning people not to start with this one, but i must say that i'm quite satisfied that i did. this is truly a unique piece of film making and a nice character study showed through, what appears to be a special 70's crime movie style. i love american genre films of the 70's so that is mainly why i started with this one, and it scored. i think that cassavetes explored depths that were unexplored in american cinema of that time, and it perfectly shows in this film, how you can make an art/crime picture. i watched truncated 104 minutes, version, but id doesn't appear to be missing anything, either in plot or character development.definitely recommended even if you're not a fan of john cassavetes's style.
