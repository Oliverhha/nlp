very enjoyable film, mcconaughey is your average ott american military man (always chewing a cigar etc), bale's cockney accent is cringeworthy at best but they are two minor faults to an otherwise great film. the locations are great, the dragons are superb, the acting is ok but its a great film and one that i will be definitely be watching again and would recommend anyone else to do the same. special note goes to the special effects man, some of the best fire effects i have seen in any film.
