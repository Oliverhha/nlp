this show appeals to a certain type of audience - it's not particularly clever but it's enjoyable to watch if not taken so seriously. this is one of those shows that will age well given time - it feels like the actors/writers haven't quite settled into the show yet. buffy the vampire slayer had a disastrous first season with terrible, cheesy writing, low brow plot lines and terrible acting... yet it went on to have a huge cult following and seven seasons. i think this series deserves a chance. the characters are endearing and it has a clear direction that i'm interested in. i hope we see a second season.
