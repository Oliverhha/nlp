i have a pretty high tolerance for slow period pieces, but this one pushed me to the limit. the first 5 minutes, and the last 10 minutes were okay. everything else in between was a snore fest. if you don't mind watching annoying, boring children try to act for over an hour straight then i suppose this might be the movie for you. personally though, i spent most of the movie hoping all those kids would fall in a well. the plot was contrived and relied completely on massive coincidences to drive itself, the children had way too much screen time and were annoying, and i got about ten times as emotionally attached to a puppy that was on screen for about 30 seconds than i was to any of the main characters. three thumbs down!
