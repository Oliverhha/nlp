if you're looking for a fun movie that will make you laugh, this is it. michael riley gives a great performance as an ordinary guy who works at two hum-drum jobs and lives a pretty boring life. robbie coltrane comes into his life and changes it for the creative better. as always, coltrane is over-the-top with his portrayal of a failed, but always hopeful restauranteur. the dialogue is witty especially from kristina nichol, who has a minor, but standout role. eugene lipinski, who also wrote the original story and co-wrote the screenplay, gives a hilarious performance as the jealous, slightly-psycho 'hopeless.' kenneth welsh is equally funny as the obsessed-to-win hockey coach and boss. the list of memorable performances goes on. the cinematography involves unique camera shots that are creative beyond what i've ever seen. definitely, a must see. i give it a 10 and a spot on my favorite movies of all time list.
