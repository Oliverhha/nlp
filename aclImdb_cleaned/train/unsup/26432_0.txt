this is such a powerful movie. without revealing any of the plot details, i will just say that at the "preclimactic" moment i thought it was anyone's guess as to which way the chips would fall... i was literally holding my breath in anticiaption. the movie was beautifully shot, artfully written and skillfully acted. i can't shake the image of the characters as each realizes the life-altering consequences that their behavior had on themselves and each other. i saw it at the stony brook festival (july 2005) and hope it will be released nationally although i'm not so sure it's a film that will appeal to a wide audience. i admire campbell scott and his production company for making the film. i hope at the very least that it successfully plays the "art house" theaters and has a big dvd distribution. i think that word-of-mouth will take this film far among true movie lovers.
