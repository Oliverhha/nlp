the budget for mars was about $1 million us. that wasn't much money, and almost all of it went to the film's paltry cgi effects, and one explosion scene. the film was shot by a first time director in an abandoned shopping mall. most of the places you see in the film are converted retail stores like gap or foot locker. the surface of mars is a truckload of sand that someone dumped in the food court and died red. if you look closely at the costumes, you can see that the futuristic breathing equipment is made from pool toys that were spray-painted black. you just can't make this stuff up. sharon belafonte tries to make a comeback in this film... and fails. she looks good for her age though. grunier does a good terminator impression, as in, shows little or no emotion for the whole film.
