this is one of the few, probably the only film in the urban youth genre, a la 'boyz in the hood' and 'menace 2 society' that i can truly appreciate. this film is as gritty and true to the life as the other films mentioned, but the story and acting in this film rises it above the rest. tupac shakur was excellent in his role as bishop....he came through with a very intense and harrowing performance. if you need any convincing as to his talent, this film will show that. this was arguably omar epps' best performance, he was very convincing as 'q'. where a movie like 'menace 2 society' was just a showcase of the gang life, 'juice' mixes that with an interesting and true-to-life story. it shows the struggle of black youth in a whole new light; i can't help but feel for q and his friends because that is something that could happen to anyone. a classic for fans of hip-hop culture. *** 1/2 out of **** stars.
