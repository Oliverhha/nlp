an action film at heart. the story of gun smuggling is just a backing just big enough to support the relentless action scenes. motorcycles exploding in mid-air, cars blowing up, shotguns, pistols, submachine guns, bombs...it's all here. they all make for some of the best action sequences ever filmed. a good mix of the gritty and realistic with the improbable and totally fake. though i think that the ending is bit too much. but what can you expect from woo aside from a molotov cocktail of intense explosive action and violence? action and cult movie fans are advised to watch this film, however others might be turned off by it. 7/10 rated r: intense strong violence
