this film is a very average 80's action/thriller. there really isn't a lot of action until the end, but i can't imagine it being categorized as anything else because the plot was so transparent and shallow that it couldn't be considered a drama or anything. this one has it all: cheesy lines, the aforementioned bad plot, horrid and forced acting and really bad special effects. the movie's upside, which most people already pointed out, is the cast. what were these people thinking when they agreed to be in this stinker? there's tons of big names in this film from when they were very young. ben stiller as a mobster, liam neeson as a 'hillbilly', helen hunt as the girlfriend...adam baldwin, bill paxton, and obviously patrick swayze. speaking of swayze, it's movies like this that really stopped his career from going from 15 minutes of fame to possibly 15 or more years of fame. bad accents, forced acting, out of place casting and everything else mentioned above made this movie a below average film even for its time. unless you're a swayze nut, stay away from this movie.
