i watched a few episodes of this programme and increasingly found that by the end of the episode i wished i'd never bothered. when i first watched it i felt that the characters had promise, which is why i watched a few more episodes. also there isn't too much on australian tv on a tuesday night! i soon realised that nothing was going to improve. the programme just has nothing innovative about it. i think the characters are wasted with very pointless clichéd story lines that wind up far too quickly and conveniently at the end. i don't think the writers are even trying. how did this programme have 4 series?! don't bother watching it!
