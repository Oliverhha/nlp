the movie diverges sharply from the short story by henry kuttner, and falls prey to every single et-based cliché, complete with floating objects and overzealous fbi agents, ancient tibetan prophecies, etc. the original story was more akin to "childhood's end", whereas the writers decided that every movie must have good guys and bad guys, and a crisis and a phony car chase. this results in a story that feels forced and fake, without any flow or sense. at many times i felt that items were "pasted on" in failed attempts to create humor or tension, but they were silly. give this one a miss.
