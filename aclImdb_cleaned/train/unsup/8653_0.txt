the all star cast delivers all star performances in this first rate drama. i won't alliterate the story line, anymore than i have in the summary, but this work was very compelling, demonstrating a realism seldom found in these types of works. do not be surprised to find yourself emotionally invested in these characters and their plight. while i do not watch it often as the subject matter is quite serious, i do consider it one of my favorites of this genre. the story is great, sadly so, but great. and as i said before, the performances are about the best to be found. it rates an 8.8/10 from... the fiend :.
