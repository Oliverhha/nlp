german produced horror. directed and written by anthony hickox. not exactly interesting, so it really takes an effort to sit through this. some preppy students are lured into a neighborhood wax museum. some veteran actors lend their talents, half heartedly i would imagine. this is just not intense enough for me. if you like bland horror...relax and enjoy. cast members: zach galligan, deborah foreman, michelle johnson, patric macnee, john rhys-davies and miles o'keeffe. kudos to the set designers that provide at least an anxious atmosphere.
