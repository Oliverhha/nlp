this move is quite fun. no award winner but really enjoyable. kids will love this film but i think most adults will too. sean hayes as the evil cat was excellent and quite funny. yes it was a little hokey but hey, i wasn't expecting anything different. jeffrey goldblum's performance left a little to be desired but considering the role, it was allright. overall i was quite pleased with all the animal characters and laughed quite a bit throughout the film. i definitely recommend this film but try to see it on a matinee.
