i don't know what people have against this movie. okay, so some of the special effects aren't great, but to be honest i prefer films like this which have interesting plots, solid characters and imaginative scenery to the kind of effects-dependent trash hollywood is currently churning out without care or interest. the story involves scientists intending to explore under the ocean with the use of a diving-bell. however, after they find a gold statue they, together with most of the crew of their ship, are abducted by a giant octopus and taken to the lost undersea world of atlantis. this is a classic and highly entertaining film with good performances from the cast and atmospheric settings with appropriately haunting music.
