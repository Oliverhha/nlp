don't bother with this movie. from a talented but wildly inconsistent boorman and a good cast comes a poorly written, directed, acted, unrealistic, predictable and sappy movie. no matter what bad things happen to this middle class family in a london suburb at the start of wwii, they always come out smiling and happy. to be honest, there are hints at some depth to the story, but they never come to fruition: a teenage daughter's coming of age and related escapades; an unhappy marriage; etc. if you're interested in some fluff about wwii england and don't want realism in any form, you might enjoy this slice of life from the view of a young boy. otherwise, don't bother.
