this is the most enjoyable movie i have ever seen: hilarious situations, unforgettable lines, car chases, characters you care for, and even a dramatic scene that makes me cry every time. a real american classic: call it a road movie, action comedy, buddy film, whatever---it's a beautiful script, professionally executed. what a country, huh? what a country! my friends and i rented this movie to celebrate a reunion after 12 years, and we were rolling with laughter in five minutes. don't watch the tv version if it's got the f-words edited out---they are very important to the experience. i can't believe: (a) this movie didn't get an oscar, (b) it's not well known even in the u.s., and (c) there's no dvd version available any more. i want to buy it! why don't they want to sell it???
