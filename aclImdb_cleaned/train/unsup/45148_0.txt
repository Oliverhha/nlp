i loved the book by phillipa gregory. i was so excited when i heard they were making it into a movie - and with such wonderful actors... well, i can't even begin to describe how disappointed i was. the movie is absolutely nothing like the book. the whole tone of the story is changed! the actors weren't bad. natalie portman actually gave a wonderfully commanding performance. but i could not get beyond the comparison of movie vs. book. and the movie was just simply awful. it was extremely boring at times and left out parts of the story - like what happened with mary's husband? i know from the book that he died of illness, but that was never addressed in the movie. bottom line: this movie should not even share the same title as the book. if you read the book you will be disappointed in the movie.
