it's official. this is the worst movie i've seen this year. i find this rather unfortunate because i had been anticipating shoot 'em up for quite some time and had heard many good things about it. the action was so redundant i was bored out of my mind. the dialogue was god awful and corny. it also failed as a comedy because i think there was only one (maybe two) parts where i laughed. i wasn't expecting a ton of plot or characterization going in but i was expecting an entertaining movie which this failed terribly at being. by all rights it feels like it was written by a high school student. so many plot holes are present and the director expects the audience to have way too many suspensions of disbelief. i understand something being over the top but when a filmmaker chooses to completely disregard reality, common sense, and psychics it's not only lazy but insulting. i was expecting something clever but instead got something dumb and trite. rather than being a parody of the action genre it merely managed to sink lower than the worst case scenarios of bad movie making and become a prime example of a genre at its absolute worst. i'm honestly amazed at the amount of positive feedback this movie is receiving.
