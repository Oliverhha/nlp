yesterday i bought this used vhs in a rental without any previous reference first because i am a great fan of horror movies. the very beautiful art of the cover also attracted me. last but not the least, the reasonable price convinced me to buy it. when i arrived home and saw that matthias hues was the lead 'actor', i recognized that i had not made a good deal and i surely believed that this flick would not be good. indeed it is worse than that, it is a junk! the storyline is horribly plagiarized from 'from dusk till dawn' and is also divided in two parts: the first one tells the 'saga' of two morons hitchhiking and being kidnapped by a psychotic killer. the second one is about a group of persons, including the two morons, trapped in a bar, surrounded by the legion of the dead leaded by togaio. i believe this flick was supposed to be funny, but it is not. the viewer can only laugh how imbecile a producer can be sponsoring such a crap. my vote is two. title (brazil): 'legião dos mortos' ('legion of the dead')
