olivier dahan tries to build on the success of the first 'rivieres pourpres' and brings back jean reno in a second series which has little in common with the first film, unfortunately. the story is one of a series of murders on persons that emulate the disciples of christ. what could have been the promising premises of a religious mystery story is combined with a quick pace filming and fights inspired from the far east ninja movies. all these are mixed with a post world war ii story, and the result looks like a strange pot with differently colored and flavored potions that do not really mix or combine well together. jean reno is a wonderful actor, but he cannot save the film alone. it's a quite a weak film by all scales, like the director did not decide what genre it would belong and took a little but of everything. disappointeing.
