this movie is meant to relax the tensions built up in the dutch society between the 'natives' and the immigrants, in this case marrocans. the story revolves around a marrocan family which tries to start over their lives in the nethelands with all cultural differences and prejudices depicted with a comical note to it. this film was well received for both audiences, dutch and immigrants. the intro movie was hilarious, but the more the movie advanced, so did the seriousness of the story. i liked this one, but i still miss the artisticness that some of the french or japanese movies have. it seems that every movie to come out of holland must have a comic tone to it to make it more acceptable for the masses.
