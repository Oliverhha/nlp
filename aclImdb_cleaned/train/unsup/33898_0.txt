how many movies are made that involve police corruption, where the corruption always runs higher than expected? how many crime rings run by corrupt police officers have been thwarted by civilians (in the movies of course)? todo el poder has your typical corrupt cop scenario, and plays out as expected. i have seen this movie around a dozen times, all under different titles, and wasn't very impressed by this one. the plot is the problem: there are no unexpected turns or developments. even though the film looks decent enough, and the acting is fair, the plot isn't intriguing enough to make you want to continue watching it. i'm not sure if it was funny, either. i realized there were humorous parts, but i didn't laugh. this, most likely, can be blamed on the fact that i had to read subtitles. overall, if you are not tired of the old scenario, and don't mind reading subtitles, you may find this one enjoyable.
