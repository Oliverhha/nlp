madame satã (2002) is more or less a biography of a brazilian criminal, pimp, and abuser of both men and women. i think we are to understand that his actions are driven by his ambition to be a transvestite star in the rio de janeiro slums. i don't know if the performances we see are meant to be evidence of talent--i can't judge what a brazilian audience would enjoy in 1932. i do know that the protagonist's behavior was consistently cruel and explosively violent. nothing i saw in this film impressed me. it was shown at the rochester gay and lesbian film festival. is this the best film the committee could find about cross-dressing?
