i picked this up at the rare video store on vhs since it seems it's not very well known or even on dvd. it's a surprisingly refreshing supernatural horror film with elements of the 'giallo' mixed in. well directed, even creepy at times it is very interesting. the plot deals with a professor who uncovers a sinister plot. filled with good camera-work, colorful elaborate lighting and scenes in old city centers of europe, it is enticing for the horror fan. if you liked this, i'd recommend: zeder (1983)(aka "zeder - voices from beyond" or "revenge of the dead"), the house of the laughing windows (1976), suspiria (1977) and one dark night (1983).
