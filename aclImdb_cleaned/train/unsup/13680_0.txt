how meryl streep beat out jessica lange for the best actress in 1982 is beyond me. once again, streep pulls out one of her phony-baloney accents with the emotional range of a stuffed tortoise. what do people see in this claptrap? stingo was well-cast, but as usual kevin kline was annoying, loud, pretentious, while eating the scenery. what is with this guy? the fact that this was even nominated shows how bad the academy had slipped by the early 80's. junk is junk. streep and kline are portrayed as intellectual fun-loving free spirits, when they're really just a couple of neurotic con artists. forget this one! i gave this a 3 because of the cinematography and stingo.
