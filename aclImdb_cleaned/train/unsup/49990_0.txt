a group of five friends travel to ireland during their school break vacation in order to do some shrooms in the wilderness along with their irish friend/ mushroom guide. when tara (lindsey haun) takes a particularly bad mushroom, she fills her mind with horrific visions of the past and of her friends' future. a future that will bring death and destruction. it's also a future that will hold boredom, and a wasted hour and a half for those who choose to watch this uninvolving, predictable, agonizingly slow horror flick. the acting is all right, but the scares are just sadly non-existent. also the ending (which anyone with half a brain can figure out early in the movie) is pretty much a direct rip-off of another modern horror film (no spoilers) my grade: d+
