(warning: i'm not fully bilingual, so please forgive me for my poor english vocabulary) this one was awful from start to finish! there was no notable action: the main characters were in a big investigation, full of dull dialogues, and the creature was just wandering around in the sewers doing some cheap kills once in a while, nothing too original, entertaining or gory. not absolutely painful, just plain boring. if you want to see something a little better with the same creature, try syngenor instead. i'm a big fan of the genre, especially the "so bad it's good" sub-genre, but this one is definitely not in that league. avoid.
