they did not change what had actually occurred (though i guess fight club did change events to an extent). my theory is this. the real daughter julia actually stayed with her, but the movie was representing her imagination of a more attractive and delinquent daughter named julie. at the end sarah says to her publisher that there were things he kept from her, and that was one of the reasons she decided to go to another publisher;
