i am one of the world's biggest fans of the original phantasm. but i have to admit, that the sequels really do nothing for me. number four is the closest thing to the original. it lives the same world, but isn't distracted by pop culture phenomena of the time the way 2 & 3 seemed to be influenced/commentating on 'evil dead' and 'home alone'. as much as i like seeing the tall man, reggie and mike back in action, jody still seems forced into the series. i really wish that coscarelli had just left him dead and moved on. the whole 'living ball' thing is plain silly.
