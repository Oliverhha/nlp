i really wish i could give this movie lower than one star. it is an insulting follow-up to one of the best modern films noir ever made. i was initially intrigued when i saw the title, and i expected an ample follow-up to bridget gregory's antics. i was sorely disappointed to see none of the original cast in the credits, but persevered nonetheless; maybe it still had merit, i hoped. my disappointment spiraled downward to mild anger that anyone would waste time making such an offensively inferior "sequel" as this one. it really was soft-core porn; i fully expected the two women with larger roles to contrive some way to get it on. ugh. i wonder if the actors in this one can salvage their careers?
