the first "bring it on" is no masterpiece, but it has charm and manages to be very entertaining. in this direct-to-video sequel, the talented cast from the original is replaced by second-rate actors and the script is brooding with everything i hate about teen movies in general. maybe i'm biased because i'm from new jersey, but i've never heard any of my peers use the phrase "shut up!" give me some characters and dialogue i can relate to for god's sake! enough of these barbie and ken dolls! aside from the ultra-lame dialogue, almost every gag falls flat. and the stupid plot involves a couple of cheerleading outcasts training a ragtag team of students (who know nothing about cheerleading) in a limited amount of time! i'm not going to give away the outcome, but the premise alone makes you want to puke, right? bree turner is a hot actress, but she doesn't match up to gabrielle union. and that reese witherspoon lookalike who plays the lead definitely doesn't match up to kirsten dunst! don't see this movie! just another lifeless sequel to serve as fodder for the video shelves. my score: 3 (out of 10)
