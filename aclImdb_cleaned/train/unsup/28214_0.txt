this mack sennet produced comedy starred w. c. fields and it was deliberately constructed like an old fashioned stage melodrama--complete with the same type music, sets and entrances. the concept was kind of funny, but after a while the single joke got kind of thin. however, there was an eventual payoff, as the ending of the film was terrific. the story begins with fields singing an annoying and bad song about the evils of strong drink(?!). this is where the title of the film was derived, but about 80% of the short occurred after this and was unrelated to the song, other than the fact it was all told as a giant morality tale against vice. not bad, but certainly not up to the standards of such fields full-length classics as it's a gift and the bank dick.
