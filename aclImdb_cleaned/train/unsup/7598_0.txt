the cinematography on this film is outstanding; it really showcases ottawa beautifully. the director was excellent. although the female lead actor was not always believable, most of the other actors were. the plot of this film was a balance between the predictable and the unexpected. the ending is great. all in all, i was very impressed with this low-budget canadian-made film. the producers should applaud themselves. you do us canadians proud. we need more good canadian films. the leading boy in the film was great -- very cute, adorable, and a good actor too. the plot had interesting undertones and provided a glimpse into new canadians' lives. i would recommend it for families, the young and the young at heart. go with minimal expectations and you will be delightfully surprised.
