the essence of a good spy flick is the plot. today repetitive spy movies, like james bond, have easy and most of the time boring plots with a bad guy who wants to rule the world. add to this unbelievable stunts and special effects... if you like these, don't watch this movie. on the other hand, if you like real spy flick like this 1985 movie, with a twisted plot and betrayal this one is for you. the action is believable, the storyline is intriguing and the ending is surprising... a must see for people who like real spy movies. 3.5 / 5 stars.
