i know the book by heart, and this mini-series is incredibly close, it's practically word-for-word; they've omitted several small non-essential scenes, made a couple beautiful, logical additions, and otherwise changed perhaps a dozen words or so. almost all the scenes are exactly as i had always imagined them; casting is incredible (except for the awful, miserable pilate - regret of a small, cowardly man is never as tragic as that of a strong man). joshua is beyond words - seeing... him gives you shivers, never before has there been such a portrayal of jesus in cinema. woland is breathtaking, down to the voice. the final scene of woland contemplating the city from above is like a goya painting.
