michael keaton is one of my all-time favorite actors, his style & natural performances in almost all of his films have made me a proud fan. 'the merry gentlemen', which marks the debut of the legendary actor's directorial debut is a disappointment. 'the merry gentlemen' is a dull fare, the film just doesn't hold. keaton's direction is neat, but the screenplay isn't. the film has no pace, it starts with a dull tone and ends the same. the climax also looks artificial & abrupt. talking on performances, keaton is okay. this ranks amongst his weak works. kelly macdonald is sincere. on the whole... one expected much more from keaton.
