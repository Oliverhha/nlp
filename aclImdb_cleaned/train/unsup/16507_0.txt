the star of this show is the hotel, it is a awesome and atmospheric set piece. the acting is fairly average, which is fine for a movie like this. the direction is strong and the camera work good. the story is a remake of an older movie that i have not seen.basic story: a being/man is killing people to feed off of them in some evil ritual that keeps him alive. the death seems are a blast, reasonable gory and full of jumps. i have not had this many jumps in a movie in a long time, makes these fun to watch. all in all a great bargain bin movie or 2.00 rental.
