perhaps we have come back to where we started as jet li takes up the mantle of the great oriental martial arts heroes. li is excellent in his role as the police officer who has come to france to assist the police in paris with a conspiracy. things turn sour for him, and, along with the excellent fonda [who gets better by the film] he has to take control and investigate for himself or lose his own life. well directed and with some brilliantly choreographed action scenes, this is definitely one to see.
