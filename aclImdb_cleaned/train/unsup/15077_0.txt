this was one of those films where the premise made me groan. i pictured a tedious slightly cheese-ball dated experience. boy was i wrong. this movie was so well-done, so well-written and directed that i didn't want it to end. one setting is all that exists and hitch certainly knew how to stage this and make it work. hitch is truly the master at 1 set movies, from rope to rear window to this. intense! i consider it one of his greatest films alongside such classics as shadow of a doubt and the afore-mentioned rope and the dark comedy the trouble with harry. of proof of hitch's genius, look for his cameo. classic! this is a must-see!
