crown prince karl heinrich (ramon novarro) hates being a prince. he can't go out and mingle or meet other people because of his station. he goes to heidelberg to study. there he meets beautiful bar maid kathi (norma shearer) and falls madly in love. she loves him too..but he can't marry beneath his station in life... just stunning silent film. you watch the screen mesmerized by how beautiful it is. it's wonderfully directed by ernst lubitsch with some truly incredible images but it's novarro and shearer who carry this film. he's unbearably handsome--she's just incredibly beautiful. together they look like perfection and both give superb performances - i believed they were in love completely! there's a just incredible sequence on a starlit night in a field of flowers. all kidding aside, it was so beautiful you could almost cry! it all leads to a tragic but realistic ending. just simply a must-see movie. don't miss this one!
