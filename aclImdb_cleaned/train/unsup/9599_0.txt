i had heard superlative comments on this film and it does not disappoint. paris is the backdrop and what a paris, a paris of la boheme and the merry widow. audrey is brilliant in the role of amelie, projecting a true joie de vivre. the film is full of surprises in both plot and characters. i left the theatre feeling lighter. it is truly one of a kind, eccentric, unusual and uplifting. i will not say more on it as it would spoil the fun. i gave it a 10 out of 10 for something so completely out of the ordinary and so very unhollywood.
