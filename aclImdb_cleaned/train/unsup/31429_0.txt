"evil dead ii" is one of the most hilarious, over-the-top, gory, and tasteless comedies of all time. naturally, that means it's a great film. sam raimiis a surprisingly energetic director, and puts a brutally quick pace on the movie. bruce campbell gets the role that made him into a star, and with good reason. he is great fun to watch - energetic, and dashing. the fact that the plot is basically non-existent (and what is there consists of ridiculous horror clichés) only serves to make the film more amusing. sam raimi's and scott spiegel's script is brilliant with it's bizarre jokes, and insane situations. the ending is particularly clever. any fan of black comedy will find "evil dead ii" hard to resist. 7.9 out of 10
