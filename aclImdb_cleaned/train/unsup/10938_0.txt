kevin bacon is "footloose" in this 1984 film which also stars john lithgow, dianne wiest, lori singer, and chris penn. bacon is ren, the new kid in a town that doesn't allow dancing, and he sets out to change that. that's actually about all there is to the plot, except that ren gets involved with pretty ariel, the daughter of the preacher (john lithgow). ariel likes to play chicken with things like trains and cars. in fact, all these kids have hormones, impulses, and energy that are in desperate need of an outlet. "footloose" has one of the best soundtracks ever, and some wonderful dancing. despite its serious moments, it has a relentlessly upbeat and exuberant feel to it. herb ross directed; originally, it was supposed to be michael cimino. it certainly would have been a different film with him directing. take "footloose" for what it is, don't ask for "citizen kane," and you'll love it.
