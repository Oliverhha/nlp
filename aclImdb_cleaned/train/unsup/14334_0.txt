this was yet another example, when it came out in 2000, of the latest strides being made in animation, computer-or-otherwise. at the time, i thought to myself, "man, this just gets better and better all the time." six years later, i am still saying that but with newer animated efforts. some of the scenes in here look so real you wouldn't know they were drawings. the colors are particularly good in the opening minutes. they are just stunning at times. the story is pretty interesting, too. it's not great, but not boring, either, and not too kid-oriented. adults can enjoy this. in fact, i was surprised at the amount of violence. that, and some of the jokes actually make this more for adults than kids in many spots. i recommend the movie for its story and innovative techniques.
