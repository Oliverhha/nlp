this movie probably would give an impression of coming from the mind of someone like ed wood - completely idealistic and with no redeeming qualities whatsoever. this really is the ultimate turkey if you ask me. aune sand would probably be best described as norway's answer to ed wood, a young idealistic filmmaker who thinks his movies are a great work of art. no matter what anyone else says. and ironically enough this movie has been one of the biggest domestic commercial successes for a norwegian film ever. about the plot and characters there's not really much to say except: terrible, dreadful, boring and involuntarily funny. see this movie at your own risk...
