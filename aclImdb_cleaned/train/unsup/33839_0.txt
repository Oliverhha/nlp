at first, when i saw black, i was really impressed with the movie and the way sanjay leela bhansali made it. the slick camera movements, the camera angles, the snow... everything was impressive. a few months later, i happened to see the same movie at the american international school in lesotho except that this was not made by sanjay leela bhansali, and it was an animated movie. at first i thought the smart boys in hollywood remade bhansali's film. only later did i realize (when i got hold of the dvd cover) that it was made much before black even started production. why did bhansali make this movie? its a frame by frame copy of helen keller's story (often shown in international primary schools to growing kids). sanjay leela bhansali or anyone associated with the script of black should feel ashamed claiming credit for black's script. they haven't even thanked the original screen writers of the original movie in the opening/closing credits of black.
