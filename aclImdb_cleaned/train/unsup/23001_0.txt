"vic reeves big night out" is stupid humour for the very juvenile mind. there is more intelligent humour to be found in a children's day care center than in this show. i like surreal humour as much as the next person, but i swear i could feel my brain cells dying off while viewing this waste of time. fortunately, vic reeves and bob mortimer redeemed themselves later with the enjoyable and stimulating randall and hopkirk(deceased). if you have not yet purchased this show on dvd, keep "vic reeves big night out" out of your dvd collection. if this is what a big night out is like, i'd rather stay in.
