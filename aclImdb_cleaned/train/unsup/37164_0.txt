man, seeing the previews of the movie hyped it up, but when i clicked on it to watch it it was painful to watch. first off, the guys who produced it were too cheap to get a real aircraft or even use cgi for the plane, instead they used a poorly made model of it. then, without warning after the crash, it went all down hill from there. the raptors, which looked to be cgi, had like really poor intelligence. they stood there being shot, and didn't even move to kill the person shooting them. instead they stood over the body, which they killed in a tyrannosaurus or big hunter sort of way(never once did they use their terrible claws). the only good cgi dino was that tyrannosaurus look alike, which looked like a amalgam of t-rex and carnotaurus. the acting, if you can call it that, was just plain horrible. huge plot lines in the story, you could drive the titanic through them. even the location was wrong for a dinosaur flick. this movie was just as bad as the carnosaurs trilogy, and that is saying a lot. jurassic park iii was a low point in the jurassic park series, but no dino flick is as bad as raptor island in my book. 3/10, stay far, far away from this movie.
