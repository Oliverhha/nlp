this is what grind house movies are all about. they lack most of the things that one would think, make up a good movie (i.e.- no budget, no name actors, poor art direction, rough cinematography, etc.). but this is exactly the type of movie that you expect nothing from, and it gives you an unbelievable amount of entertainment. if there is any way you can get a hold of a print of this film, or have a chance to see it at a revival house, i suggest you do. it is much better to watch on film than it is on dvd. i believe this film is a sequel (one of many) to the film "zombie a.k.a: dawn of the dead" originally directed by george a romero. the second zombie film was done by italian director lucio fulci and was entitled simply, "zombie" here in the u.s. truth be told, i expected little from this italian import campy horror flick. but as the film progressed, i realized that it is just the kind of nostalgic grind house film that select demographics will enjoy for years to come.
