saw sleepy time gal last night at the cleveland film festival. in short, i found the film very powerful and surprisingly well done. jacqueline bisset is just wonderful. although a bit predictable in places, the plot has a few twists...definitely not a big studio release. i cannot understand why this film isn't in wider distribution. although the movie has nothing in common with the oscar-nominated "you can count on me" other than the fact that both movies are small, intimate portraits of families, i have to say that sleepy time gal is a much more nuanced, ambitious film with a lot more depth than yccom. i'm very sorry more filmgoers won't get a chance to see sleepy time gal.
