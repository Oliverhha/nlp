"bingo" is exactly the word! this film hits all the right spots and its not all about joan crawford, who simply sparkles. i fell totally in love with her character! it is not by accident that imdb lists 107 films by director jack conway between 1912 and 1948. this was his 72nd film according to that chronology and his expertise shows. i found it thoroughly engrossing and continuously entertaining. i laughed aloud often -- some really great gags and a fine early sit-com. i don't know much about the writer charles e. scoggins, but co-writer sivia thalberg is credited with 15 films and is the sister of irving thalberg. that ought to tell you this film deserves close attention. don't allow yourself to think of it as "cornball" and just engross yourself in it. you won't be disappointed.
