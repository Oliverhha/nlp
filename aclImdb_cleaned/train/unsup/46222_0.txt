in rotterdam, when kevin burke (skeet ulrich) is promoted to chief of security of a multinational investment bank, he goes to a hotel with his girlfriend, the executive of the bank rosalind bremmond (katherine lang), to celebrate and propose her to marry him. however, rosalind is murdered in the room, and blind of rage, kevin looks for revenge following the guidance of his chief and mentor karl jorgensen (derek de lint). "soul assassin" is a horrible movie, indeed a festival of clichés. the story is totally unbelievable and amazingly ridiculous; the characters are not well-developed and the viewer does not know who they are and their motives; the camera seems to be shooting a mtv video-clip; the choreography of the fights is awful, with many closes and cuts. i have seen few dutch movies, but this one is certainly the worst. my vote is three. title (brazil): "alma assassina" ("assassin soul")
