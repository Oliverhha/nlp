probably one of the most memorable tom and jerry animated shorts, has tom the cat hopelessly smitten with his love interest at a day at the beach. jerry the mouse, with the help of a crab (in the crab's defense he hates jerry as well as tom), plot to of course turn tom's good time into a day of pain, degradation and humiliation, often with hilarious results (the clam sandwich, the sand tea, the fake wooden lady, the spit-take, and of course the umbrella spring to mind) viva tom and jerry!! this hilariously great cartoon can be found on disc one of the spotlight collection dvd of "tom & jerry" my grade: a
