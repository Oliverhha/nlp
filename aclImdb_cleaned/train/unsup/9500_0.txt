another gem from kim-ki-duk, very complex movie in terms of concept and screenplay. even hard to digest, what is right, what is wrong...lines are blurred. you'd like to hate the character of han-ki very much but you'll be forced to like him. evoked a mixture of emotions in me...its brutal in its sense, sensual in its sense...a totally different grammar for emotions... once again, kim-ki-duk beautifully used silence in the movie and the main character (as usual??) is silent for major part of the movie. this is not everyone's cup-of-tea, so beware if you are thinking of watching it...you may find it bad
