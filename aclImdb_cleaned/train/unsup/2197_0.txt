creative and original horror comedy about an eloping teen's girlfriend who is abducted by a cop from hell---literally. the late richard farnsworth plays mentor to the teen (chad lowe), and soon the boy is off to the netherworld to save his bride-to-be. only mildly suspenseful, but works better as a self-parody, with dashes of loopy humor and supporting characters the like. the hell cop (played by friday the 13th part 6's c.j. graham) is particularly amusing. **1/2 out of ****. mpaa: rated r for violence and brief nudity.
