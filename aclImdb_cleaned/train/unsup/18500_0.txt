coming across like the big six, the man of the century left me grungy for a better day. the writing and direction hit on all sixes. the music was dandy. and when johnnie and samantha danced the charleston beautifully, i was hooked like a mackerel on joy juice. the smiles were strong, the music keen, and the photography in stellar black-and-white. the actors all played it straight, which made the film work perfectly. where did adam abraham, the director, go? he only made a few films, won a slew of awards for this one, and then nada! the next time this film is on ifc, sit your keester down, give it five, and you will see what a keen flicker it really is. it simply slayed me!
