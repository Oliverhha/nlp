i'm all for a good b horror film, but this was the most horribly done, worst acted, most retardedly written thing i have ever seen. the sets were laughable. the gore was stupid. i kept wondering when it would be over. i have an unfortunate need to always watch the whole movie, no matter how bad, just to give it a chance to get better. this one never did. june 2008 ~ after reading some of the positive reviews, i reevaluated my opinion. with every film i watch, i try to find something positive. i am an artist myself and i know that a lot of effort and love goes into any production of art. i try to see it from the writer's, director's, even the editor's point of view. i try. really, i do. but, unfortunately, i have to stand by my original review. sorry guys.
