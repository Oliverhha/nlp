peter jackson has a big heart, i think. even for all the gore and grossness of his films (i.e.; dead alive aka braindead) he always has a very heartfelt moment of sentiment. in this film, it's the loving way mckenzie is treated and the seriousness of his death scene, captured on film. while much of the film has very tongue in cheek chuckles, this part is played very seriously. also, as someone who works every day with "forgotten silver", i admire his treatment of the whole subject. for, while mckenzie and his films may be bogus, the plight of old movies is not. i'm amazed at how much footage shows up in attics and basements and garden sheds every year. in just the eight months so far of 1999, we have received at least 30 "new" films; rare and unique items all. and we have films done by people very much like colin mckenzie. private, personal projects, most of which never saw the light of day or vanished quickly after their initial release. so much "forgotten silver."
