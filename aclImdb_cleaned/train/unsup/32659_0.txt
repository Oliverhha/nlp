after making a great supporting role in van wilder and being pretty damn funny in harold and kumar, i had kind of high hopes for kal penn, but oh god does this movie suck. short of changing the actor who plays the lead character for a sequel, the worst thing you can do when making a sequel is get rid of the main character who is in this case also the title character (how can it be called van wilder 2 if van wilder isn't freaking in it?). a brief synopsis of the story is that taj goes to england and starts trying to do van's thing in a posh college, he also somehow gets involved in a dog show. sounds pretty lame, doesn't it? well, yeah it is. not only is it not funny at all, but again there's the completely stupid british stereotypes flying around as if people even found them funny the first time round. don't waste your time
