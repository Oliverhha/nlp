this movie is a real disaster: insulting, with awfully stereotypical acting and an incredibly poor script that one wonders why this movie has been made for. if i had seen that movie as a closeted gay teen, i would have been shocked and ashamed. fortunately i have seen it first as a (by then openly gay) movie-addict in my twenties, so i could dismiss it as only an embarrassingly bad film. either you just forget about this miserable movie or you take it as a source for filmhistory, namely how bad, how ridiculous, how homophobic so-called comedies sometimes could be in the sixties (alas, there are not that many of this kind...). i'm afraid there's nothing more to say about it.
