after getting panned by reviewers and critics alike, i went along to the local two dollar theater with little expectation. what i saw was a very funny movie, with some excellent dialogue and set at a great pace. the story is silly and some of the special effects look rough around the edges, but who cares? the story and many memorable quotes make this a great film for kids and adults alike. i may get this as a dvd when it's released! quite the sleeper hit.
