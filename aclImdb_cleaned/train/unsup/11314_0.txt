operation amsterdam is a pleasant surprise. it has both a strong story and some unusual cinematic touches to keep the viewer interested. the story of the british secret service agents who are sent to amsterdam to recover industrial diamonds before the german invasion is a familiar one, and their eventual safe escape is predictable enough. what interested me was the atmosphere of fear and bleakness that the producers manage to convey. the empty streets, in bright sunlight; the columns of fleeing people; the confusion of not knowing who are enemies or friends, makes for a better than average effects. added to this a score made only with drums, and some very abrupt editing that is almost painful to watch, makes this a worthwhile watch.
