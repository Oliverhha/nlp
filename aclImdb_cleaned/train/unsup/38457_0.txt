considering the all-star cast and the fairly large budget (this is no indie film), there's no reason why this film should be as bad as it was. ian mccellan gives a stellar performance, but otherwise this film is rendered almost unwatchable because of the performances of aaron eckhardt and britney murphy, and most of all, because of the insufferable/clichéd writing, which makes every effort to pull at our heartstrings. don't let the allure of the cast (in addition to mckellan, eckhardt, and murphy, it also features william hurt, nick nolte, alan cumming, jessica lang, and more) draw you in -- stay away from neverwas!
