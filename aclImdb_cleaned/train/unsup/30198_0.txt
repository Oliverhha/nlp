there is no better movie on that period in history. it shows the african spirit not only as passive and accepting or merely as a vehicle to be brutalised by an antagonist who is given all the lines but here the africans are allowed to be people. we see a real portrayal of people with feelings, emotions and lives. it touches on some of the aspects of mental slavery that other movies avoid, such as the biracial son of the master and a slave being indoctrinated with the church to hate his black mother and her spirituality. the scenes with him gazing longingly at white madonna and child imagery in a church being attended to by a white priest telling about his wicked heathen mother really ring home. from start to finish this is definitely the black perspective on slavery. amistad is nothing to this. amistad was about a court case, did nothing to the actualities of slavery or the deeper issues as sankofa did with panache and feeling.
