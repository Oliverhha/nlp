three stories (all bland and emotionless) unfold in red shoe diaries 2: double dare, a terrible anthology filled with loser episodes from the series. the first stars steven bauer and joan severence and follows their turbulent sex-only relationship. the second is laughably, horribly bad starring a very unattractive actress as a cop who wants to get kinky with a guy she never met before. and the third is a so-so story of a game of double dare played between a man and woman who work across the street from each other. if you liked the original, like me, you will hate the lack of emotion these stupid anthology stories give. one star out of four.
