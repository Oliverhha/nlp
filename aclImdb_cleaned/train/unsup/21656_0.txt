an interesting movie and a worthy part of the sherlock holmes tradition. the most interesting part for was the use of the count down notes as the members of the organization died. it even included a red herring note. if the viewer is paying attention, it is a clue to the actual murderer. agatha christie, the well known english mystery writer who also has had many books made into movies, used a similar motif in her book, ten little indians. the most fascinating part, though, is that this movie was made in 1933 and ten little indians was published in 1939. christie may have borrowed from this movie for her plot.
