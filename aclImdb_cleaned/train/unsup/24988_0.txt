this is definately one of my favourite movies and in my opinion, mike myers' best movie ever made. i just about know every line off by heart now thanks to dvd. this movie doesn't rely on bodily function jokes (ie austin powers ), but on a very clever script. the casting was great and i loved and laughed at alan arkin, michael richards, phil hartman and charles grodin in their small, but effective roles. i found it funny and enjoyed the twist at the end. do yourself a favour, please rent this one out.
