in 'kinky boots' joel edgerton plays charlie price, a young man with big dreams outside of northampton. suddenly, the death of his father forces him to leave london and return home. he has to resume his father's role and take over the family business, something he has dreaded most of his life. when he discovers that the business is in turmoil, he has to come up with a radical idea that will save everyone job. with the help of a popular drag queen (chiwetel ejiofor), charlie decides to switch gears and start making women's shoes. women's shoes that are strong enough for a man. this movie was a little disappointing; i thought it would be funnier. it does have its moments. joel edgerton does an amusing job as the businessman without a clue. i liked chiwetel ejiofor as the brave lola/simon drag queen. however, when he sings, i feel someone else would have been more suited for the role. good story, but not enough comedy.
