i saw this film when it was first released and with its all-star cast, the lines were a block long at the 41st street sheridan theater on miami beach. the acting was out-standing, but the story-plot seemed sort of unrealistic. the film however was stolen by tom courtenay as a reluctant war-hero. courtenay gives a brooding, emotional, sole-searching performance of a simple man caught up in the madness and terror of world war ii. the story line just goes off in two many different directions: serial killer, murder mystery, plot-to-kill-hitler, madness, love story, the horrors of war, hero worship, and survival. if you're a fan of tom courtenay this is a must-see. if you're not, rent another film.
