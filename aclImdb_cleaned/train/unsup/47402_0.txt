characters are supposed to change as stories progress. the main character here changed from a selfish pompous dork into a selfish pompous reborn "guru". nolte evidently has had a lobotomy. this was a film about wisdom seemingly made by high schoolers who had read a comic book and become "enlightened". a dash of karate kid, a little rocky and a whole lot of ishtar. the best that can be said is that this was a low budget film and not a lot of money was wasted. unless it was a tongue-in-cheek satire, it was one of the worst films ever made. you need to run your head into a brick wall a few times before you watch this and, if you like it, you need to return to the wall and hit it much harder.
