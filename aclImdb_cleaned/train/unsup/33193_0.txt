simply put, the worst film i've ever seen. i've seen better acting from junior school dramatics, and been more thrilled by noddy books. the 'plot', for lack of a better word, revolves around a sexy teen on her way with stereotyped friends (geek, jock, bitch, clown) to a concert, which they never reach (!)... and the group fall into the hands of the dumbest hillbilly psychopath in the world. "my god, this place is so creepy.... quick, let's make out!" the actual horror content is not even comedic - filmed on a cheap 'shakey' cam, tired clichés of 'he's behind you!', and 'the body has vanished!' are over-used, and by the end, if you actually have the stamina for it, you're praying they get cut up and hung up on hooks. i've had more enjoyable experiences picking my nose.
