the title may sound like this would be a satirical comedy spoof,it sure is not, the director takashi miike normally has directed horror film, here he attempts a western, with a decided japanese flavor. .the dialogue is all in spoken (not dubbed)english,giving the stylized film a sense of reality. combine all the qualities of a japanese samaurii film & a classic shoot out western ala the italian spaghetti westerns of leone films of the sixties. the film is very well acted & the production values are first rate. the various stunts & effects are all real i doubt if any cgi was used. the film is quite violent with an ending almost tragic. it is not a great film,but is very interesting & well made. the dvd includes excellent extras including 54 minute 'making of' short subject. ratings *** (out of 4) 87 points (out of 100) imdb 8 (out of 10) ** ** this rating would be about the same on most good american westerns of years back.
