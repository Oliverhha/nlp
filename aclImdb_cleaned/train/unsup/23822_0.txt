this film has to be one of the most enjoyable films i have ever seen. the sincerity and wittiness of jane austen combined with the ridicule of a shakespearean play, cold comfort farm tells a clever, little story about a young, determined london-girl who is set on lifting the curse of the starkadder family. kate beckinsale's flora poste is as unshaken and well-spoken as austen's emma (who beckinsale has also starred as) but in a more cheerful and less hypocritical manner. the names in the film do their best to describe the utter depravity of the starkadder's: the horse viper, the cows aimless, senseless, heedless and desireless and aunt ada doom. the acting in the film is flawless and stephen fry is just the icing on the cake.
