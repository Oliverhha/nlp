if you are going to steal ideas, at least give credit to the original. also, if you are going to copy, take your time and try to improve upon the original. both, i guess, are too much to expect from typical bollywood productions. as some of the other folks have already mentioned. this is a remake of bruce almighty with the typical bollywood masala. overacting (come on, we are talking about salman), weak script, weaker directing. just to give you an example, i usually like jaspal yadav in movies. no matter how small his role is, he manages to make it funny. not in this one. in an attempt in increase the running time of the movie, the director/script-writer added several additional characters which truly haven't added anything to this poor movie. thank god for priyanka though. god, tussi seriously great ho. final verdict, if you love bollywood movies no matter how bad they are, you will probably enjoy it. but, if you have any taste or care for quality, save your money and time.
