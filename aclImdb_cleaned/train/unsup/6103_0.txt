i saw this on television recently. i remember when i saw it first in the early nineties that it got bad reviews at the time. i think the critics just had it in for polanski. 'frantic' was dismissed as a poor man's hitchcock, though i thought it was a pretty good stab at doing a hitchcock, suspenseful enough. a devastating chronicle of the disintegration of a relationship. though the role reversal development was done better in bunuel's tristana, i thought. i was struck at the time by hugh grant's hilarious portrayal of the twit of an englishman. this was an act that soon palled over the decade, however.
