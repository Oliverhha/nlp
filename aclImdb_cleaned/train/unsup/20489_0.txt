admittedly, the premise of doc hollywood is more of a sentimental ideal than an actuality: high-powered big city surgeon falls for the charms of a small town full of colourful characters and in need of an old-time country doctor. but michael j. fox is perfect for his part. he can be manic and money-oriented, or compassionate and charming. whether he's keeping a beribboned sow on a tight leash, spreading his scent through the woods, or floundering around with fresh fish, he's right at home in this film, down home. when i first saw doc hollywood, i didn't expect to fall under the spell of this mayberry of squash either, but the locals really grow on you. like kudzu. michael j. fox winds up as happy as a pig in silage and so will you.
