i went into this expecting a film on par with road trip or old school...nothing brilliant, just a solid hilarious movie littered with quotable lines and "remember-that-part-when...?" moments. well, none found here. this movie was just stupid, and i don't mean stupid-funny slapstick style, i mean just plain dumb. the really funny moments, and they were few and far between, were utterly predictable and played out. the story line was also ridiculously clichéd, lacking any real original twists. just a rehash of a million other similar movies. overall i was really just waiting for it to end. i give it a 4 out of 10, not worth buying a ticket for or renting, but decent enough to catch on hbo if there's nothing else on.
