this is the soap opera version of the raid on entebbe. about half of this movie is poorly written, a real shame. i couldn't believe that this all-star cast would sign up to make a movie of this low caliber. i have watched all 3 entebbe movies. as others have commented, the "raid on entebbe" is strongly on point of the story at hand (the rescue of 101 israelis held by terrorists). "operation thunderbolt" is also on-point, and has many of the actual players playing themselves, but it is largely unfocused. so, for the people who want to get lost in the background, "victory" is the pick. i wish they could have taken this cast, and had them perform the raid on entebbe script ... it would be incredible.
