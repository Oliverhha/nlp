this is a bad movie. everything is wrong with it. just, everything. technically the director constantly "crosses the directorial line" and the cinematography is often not carefully composed and the scenes are poorly light. the dialogue is terrible. its as if the script was written by a student. because the dialogue is so terrible, the performances are just as bad. the two leads have no chemistry. and the plot, apparently based on a shakesperean play, is ridiculous in a modern setting. that said, i did like the premise of the film. the film is a satire of bollywood films, which in itself is an interesting ideas, but unfortunately it is not executed well.
