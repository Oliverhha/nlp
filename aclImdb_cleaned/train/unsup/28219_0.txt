i was driving to visit my cousin recently, & passed very close to the birthplace of w c fields, a native philadelphian like myself. it reminded me that w c has somehow gotten out of vogue. why?? back in the 1960's & 1970's there was a huge revival of interest in his anti-establishment ways. we used to have birthday parties for him every jan. 29th (i won the child-insulting contest one year). his films were on tv constantly. it's time to get old william claude back where he belongs! he was the greatest, & we need more of his brand of humor now. (plus, he inspired me to learn how to juggle - a hobby i've now enjoyed for almost 40 years) best short: fatal glass of beer best feature: it's a gift
