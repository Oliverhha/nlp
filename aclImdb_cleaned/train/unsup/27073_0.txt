the reporter malcolm anderson (kurt russell) is contacted by a serial killer, alan delour (richard jordan), who seeks promotion of his acts in the news. the only leads for the police force, commanded by detectives ray martinez (andy garcia) and phil wilson (richard bradford), are the contacts of the criminal through malcolm and the bodies of his victims. when malcolm becomes more important for the media than alan, the killer becomes jealous and kidnaps malcolm's girlfriend, christine (mariel hemingway), looking for revenge and more attention for his crimes. "the mean season" is an efficient thriller, mostly supported by the great performance of kurt russell, in excellent physical shape. the story has minor flaws and some clichés, but hooks the attention of the viewer until the last scene. watching it again in 2005, we can see a not famous andy garcia in the beginning of his brilliant career. my vote is seven. title (brazil): "temporada sangrenta" ("bloody season")
