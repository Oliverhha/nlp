william wellman was really a helluva director. anyone that can do a movie like this, and make "the ox-bow incident" too, must have been born to direct. coming in at a breezy 75 minutes, "nothing sacred" is still very funny on several levels, for several different reasons. plot does not matter as much as execution, and how you deliver a line matters more than the line itself. frederic march and carole lombard are perfect, and the supporting cast is just as good, especially the actor who played 'oliver stone', march's frustrated boss. wellman does unconventional things like make the actors faces be hidden by a tree branch, practically unheard of in that day and age. but the fact of the matter is, that sometimes people are not perfectly framed in life, so maybe they shouldn't be in the movies - at least not as a rule. the first time you get a good look at lombard, she has shaving cream on her face from kissing a man who is shaving - also not the normal star-moment you might expect. just terrific. 9/10.
