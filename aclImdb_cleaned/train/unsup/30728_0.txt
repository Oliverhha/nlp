i first saw this film just after i left the navy in '73. back then, it seemed like an interesting film. i'd been a long-time fan of rock hudson, especially his film roles in "man's favorite sport" and "ice station zebra." and, i was a faithful watcher of his popular television series, "mcmillan and wife." but, i guess i'm just an old fuddy-duddy now. after re-watching it, i honestly can't see the appeal of a film that even impliedly champions teacher pedophilia, murder to cover up the pedophilia, and a supportive spouse who flies off with her sick husband to south america at the end of the film ... allowing him to get away with all his crimes scot-free. i will not be watching it again ... ever.
