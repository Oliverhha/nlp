inuyasha is an excellent anime series with everything a good tv series needs. its got action, suspense, comedy and love. inuyasha is about the adventures of a young half-demon from futile japan and a human girl from modern times. they team up with a monk and a demon slayer to find sacred jewel shards and destroy an evil demon. the animation is really good. what you have to remember is this is a tv series and not a movie. tv series never look as good as movies. i've seen some bad animation and this is not one of them. the music is great and the voice actors fit the characters perfectly. all-around : great show with few (if any) flaws. any anime fan who loves fantasy will adore this show.
