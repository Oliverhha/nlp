terrible, terrible, terrible. i can appreciate a good spoof, but this movie had me wincing all the way through. i'm 20 years old and i've done more professional stuff than this. out-of-focus shots, too dark at times, terrible sound in some scenes and a really annoying shifting white-balance (which means the image will change tones from one shot angle to another). the whole thing was filmed by handycam and looked like a porno, from the costumes, to the actresses, to the plot. scream bloody murder is a porn movie minus the actual porn! the story was ridiculous and the ending downright stupid and nonsensical. i usually can appreciate a stupid-fun movie, but this is just way too horrible. thank god i got to rent this for free. 2 out of 10! and that's on the strength of the uniforms alone ;). daniel
