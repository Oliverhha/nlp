i am not sure quite how this film claims to be based on faulkes's novel charlotte gray. the story vaguely follows a similar chain of events, but much of the emotion in the book, is lost in the film. the producers/screenplay writers have added small details which do not aid the story, but just take it further and further away from the original story. i also think a scottish person should have played charlotte - i did not find the accent of cate blanchett at all convincing. in all, the film is not too bad to watch, but compared to the book it is a dead loss and a waste of time.
