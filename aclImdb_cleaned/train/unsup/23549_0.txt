basically, the movie is about a family (parents and three daughters) in the 1960's as they deal with issues from the past and the present. there is also a secret that is eventually revealed, and sort of explains why things are the way they are. the casting was good, everyone played their character well (mostly), except i sort of agree with the other reviewer when they said that you really don't 'dislike' the father, even though his acts can sometimes be inappropriate. there are some scenes i liked with some of the individual characters, and the cinematography is nice. i especially liked the opening and the closing scenes. overall, i didn't like some of the story. but if you like any of the actors or are just somewhat interested, it is probably worth seeing.
