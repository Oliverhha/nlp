if you're like me and drool like an over-hormonal teenager when that unlikely sex-symbol james nesbitt smiles his cheeky-charming smile then you must go and see this film. even if you don't, i'd highly recommend it. as with the director's previous brit-hit `the full monty' this provides plenty of laughs, many colourful characters and some poignant moments. they also managed to fit a love-interest into a prison movie (hard to do and not particularly convincing but fun to watch nonetheless). perhaps not as belly-laughingly funny as the full monty, but still loads of laugh-out-loud moments and an all-round enjoyable film.
