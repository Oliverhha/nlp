this is one of the few times that i have been badly mislead by the reviews on imdb! i avoided this film in the past because of this, but thought it would be a good "soft" film to watch on video with with a friend and his wife (she doesn't like difficult films). what a revelation! i understand that people who have read the book may be disappointed, but as an uneducated viewer this is a fantastic film! novel situation, novel location (beautiful images, even though the beach was re-modeled), novel plot, novel characters (all of whom are wildly different yet possible to sympathise with - except maybe for bugs)... this film has everything in it that i find so desperately lacking in the average hollywood pulp i'm forced to watch with friends. and i *liked* the fact that the film made a few surprising changes in style... i hate knowing the end of a story at least an hour before it finally grinds its inevitable way there (see any "action" film).
