some of these reviews must have been from a friend of someone connected to the movie. the box cover is the most professional part of the movie. the writing is unoriginal and the acting is just bad. it starts off in a slow time warp and then the cement starts to set. i spent most of the movie wondering how much time was left. i kept waiting for it to get better and live up to the box cover. half way through felt like too long for a feature film. most of the time i just felt bad for the actors having to recite the horrible dialog and the rest of the time feeling mad at those who decided to release it. do not watch this movie if you value your time! the most frightening part will be that you didn't have anything better to watch.
