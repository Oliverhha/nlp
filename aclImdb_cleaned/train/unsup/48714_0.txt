i have seen many versions of the play in my life, both on stage and screen. for that matter, i actually played the role of puck as a child. and this is unquestionably the worst version i've ever seen. the actors are stiff -- probably mostly due to the director, as they never, ever move. the camera angle, however, leaps whimsically and frequently. characters, too, and not just the fairies, appear suddenly before freezing in place to say their lines. it reminds me of the confuse-a-cat sketch from monty python's flying circus. regardless of the high-powered cast, only ian holm as puck and relative nobody michael jayston as demetrius show any energy at all ever. i'd also like to concur with regards to what others have said about how dirty the lovers get, the strangeness of the costumes, and a number of other points. the overall effect is that of a remarkably boring two hour long drug trip, best appreciated through open mocking. on a side note, i would not allow any daughter of mine on screen naked but for green paint.
