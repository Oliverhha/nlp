"gloomy sunday" was based on a real song that was actually thought to be responsible for an outbreak of suicides. the music is quite haunting and lovely, although i still feel quite strongly that "life is good" and i am not struck with a desire to end it all. i'm not sure how much of the rest of the film is 'true,' but i found it quite enjoyable. it screened here in austin as part of the local jewish film festival, and i found it as interesting as anything i saw a couple of weeks ago during sxsw film. it almost reminded me of one of my favorite films: "1900." not quite, but it might have been better to mimic that film's beginning and ending just a bit more closely.
