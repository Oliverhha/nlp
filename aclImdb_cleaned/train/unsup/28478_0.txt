donna deitch has done a great job of conveying highly pertinent real life aspects of gay and lesbian recent history and modern life. with very tight scripts and damned fine acting from all players, it would be hard to go wrong, but donna makes the whole film cohesive and attractive. i also really enjoy the stereo soundtrack. i've seen common ground several times, enough to be inspired to import the ntsc tape - and the only problem with it is that the left and right channels are reversed (it's worth swapping your cables around temporarily). even though i know my emotional buttons are being pushed, i still enjoy every word and aspect of this film. it offers inspiration for younger and older gays and lesbians - and food for thought for our family and friends. this is high quality, gimmick-free and utterly honest film-making.
