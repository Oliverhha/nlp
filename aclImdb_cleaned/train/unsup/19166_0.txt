"dead poets society" talks about the lives of the students in the "welton academy", an elite prep-school preparing the students to ivy league school. these students were brought up in a strict and mainstream families. all unexpectedly, the students are exposed to mr. keating, an english teacher whose will change their lives through "free-will" thinking. one of the best film of all time!
