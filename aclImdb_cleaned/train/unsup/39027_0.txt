great on location photography in budapest hungary, and surprise at the end (don't blink or you'll miss it) can't save this otherwise boring movie about a group of people, seemingly chosen at random, attending the reopening of an old hungarian castle which has been closed for half a millennium, being stalked by an unseen killer. the killer turns out to be a werewolf. or, maybe it was just a great big hairy dog. i couldn't tell, the killer was on screen so briefly. this one isn't as awful as the previous three sequels, but that doesn't mean that it's very good either ... *1/2 out of ****
