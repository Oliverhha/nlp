i guess i have seen three or four hundred 'first films' of young american directors in the last couple of decades. none has given me such a sense of euphoria that metropolitan did. it stands in a very high position in that much neglected and un-nameable genre that presents us with a bunch of totally unlovable characters and then proceeds to make us love them. that may have started with la regle du jeu, but stillman handles this so well, he should not blush at the reference.
