if you like action movies, this film is for you. if you like gentle comedy, this film is also for you. if you've only seen chow yun-fat playing suave, "hard boiled" killers this film is *definitely* one you should see. breathtaking action sequences pepper a sweet plot about the triumph of innocence over arrogance and the power of love and friendship. plus you get to see chow yun-fat walking around with mickey mouse balloons. 5 stars out of 5. one of my favorite hk comedies.
