merbabies was a truly enchanting short. the animation is stunning, with beautiful underwater backgrounds, and fluid movements, and the music was a delight to the ears and was strongly reminiscent of a tchaikovsky ballet score. the characters, especially the adorable merbabies, were completely likable and there are some truly entertaining bits, like the part when the seahorse tries to get through the hoop. i will admit, merbabies is thin in terms of story, and i was saddened when the merbabies turned to sea foam at the end. still, it is very beautiful, and i would definitely watch it again. 9/10 bethany cox
