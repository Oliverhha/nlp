although i like the actors in this movie, i found it to be boring. i chuckled a few times but they were few and far between. the previews made this movie look like a funny romantic comedy but i found myself nodding off toward the end. the movie does not fit as a romantic comedy. the chemistry between the main characters is lacking and as far as the comedy goes...well there wasn't much. so if you are looking for a romantic comedy, i say look elsewhere. if you want a movie you can watch and step away from every now and then while doing your chores or other errands, this is the one to do it with.
