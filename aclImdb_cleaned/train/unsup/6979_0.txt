when i was about to watch this film, i didn't really know what to expect from it. i didn't really know what it was all about, and i wasn't familiar with french avant-garde movies or the work of marc caro and jean-pierre jeunet. but it was an instant delight. it's not at all gruesome even if the plot is about cannibalism, and it's so comprehensible. it's such a good movie that i don't know how to explain why. it's so funny, well-directed, lovable, whatever. it's just a real delightful movie. and i love the 2 leads, julie and louison, especially when they played music together. speaking of music, i even remember just how good the score was, too.
