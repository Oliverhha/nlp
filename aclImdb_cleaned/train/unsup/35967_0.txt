two idiots (ted danson, howie mandel) plan to bet on a horse in a race that's fixed to win. but they have to deal with the mob and the mob's head wife who is sleeping around. wow--is this bad! i saw it in a theatre in 1986 and remember hating it and being really amazed at how lousy this was. i figured with ted danson in it (who was popular from 'cheers' at the time) it couldn't be bad. well, i was wrong! he tries but the material is hopeless. mandel was just annoying. the rest of the cast are just there. purportedly danson shot nude scenes for this movie but they weren't in the final cut. also there were sequences shot with danson and mandel imitating old laurel and hardy routines (hence the title) but those were cut too. i heard the studio was horrified by the final result (no shock there) and just released it very quietly and it died a quick deserved death. easily one of the worst comedies of its time. just painful. avoid.
