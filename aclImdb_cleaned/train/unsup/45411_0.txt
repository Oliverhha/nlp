with the latest revival in horror flicks, you would at least expect decent movies, since they make a lot of money these days. but 'abominable" proofs that turkeys still exist in the genre. everything is wrong: -the acting is terrible, especially the female lead is painfully to watch. -the dialog is cheesy: remember when they talk about their past right after a murder with the beast out there ? oh god... -the lame story rips off hitchcock's rear window and even... mimic 3. go figure.. -the creature is something to laugh at. never scary or anything. -no frights, no suspense, murders appear mostly off-screen. you can mix horror with humor, like lake placid, which worked out great. but this one is not meant to be funny. avoid at all cost.
