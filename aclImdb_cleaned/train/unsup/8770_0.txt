when the movie was first released, i saw woodstock in a theater with my great uncle. i was in 8th grade. he took me to see it because we both played guitar and shared a love of music. it was quite an experience - especially some of the "free love" scenes, but we loved it and he was especially awed by jimi hendrix. i had a chance to see the director's cut in a theater this year. it was such a gift to go back in time and recapture the feeling of that time. while four hours is a long time, the extra footage of jimi hendrix and the dreamy scenes of janis joplin near the end are worth it. yes, woodstock is essential viewing for any rock fan and for anyone who wants to capture the real sense of what it was like to feel part of a generational "love' movement. it seems so unreal in retrospect, but those of us who lived it - remember it. see woodstock and enjoy.
