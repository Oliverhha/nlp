well-made with excellent performances, but not at all what was promised. i had hoped to see a self destructive man who was changed by his circumstances and especially by a wise woman. while that happened, it only happened because he was facing death! what a cop out! anyone would embrace strong spiritual guidance when forced to cope with a very early end to their life. i thought the movie really missed an opportunity to make its point. resorting to such a time worn cliché made the writer/director seem lazy. if i'd known the whole film was a promo for buddaism, i would have skipped it. i'm surprised so many writers on this site were so impressed. yes, it's not the typical hollywood fare, but that doesn't automatically make it a great movie.
