for all the big names in this film, it is truly pathetic. shallow, cartoonish bad guys, and one-dimensional characters throughout. i can't believe this one was ever released theatrically, nor why it is being shown on tbs now. back in 1989 some of the now-big name actors like ben stiller were just getting started, but others should have turned this one down, like liam neeson. his casting as a backwoods hillbilly out for vengeance in the big city of chicago may be the worst casting i can remember. patrick swayze does his usual pathetic job of trying to be tough and sensitive at the same time, and again i am unimpressed.
