i simply can't see why this film has scored such a high rating. i'm generally in agreement with a lot of people's opinions on films, so when i was recommended this as being a simply awesome film i had to watch it. what i experienced was film with a plot line so thin it was painful, characters with no real 'character' about them (which may well have been the point) and a bizarre use of flitting back and forth throughout the plot. i mean what is this film about? retribution? friendship? perhaps, but it seemed to go on for a very long time with me wondering exactly if we were going to get to a point... all in all, worth a watch if you're someone who appreciates obscure films that seem to strain a non-existent theme. hey, perhaps i missed the point but thats the beauty of opinion.
