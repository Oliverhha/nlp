i first got hooked on starcraft from my friend. the day after, i ran out and bought it. this game has a great story, great cinematics, and great units from all the races; terran (human), protoss (alien), and zerg (bug-like critters). if you like starcraft, then i suggest getting the expansion (brood war). i would be grateful if they came out with a second expansion. i reccomend, again, that anyone who likes good graphics, real-time combat, great story lines, and sci-fi action get this game!
