an independent film, "american desi" accurately captures the abcd/fob experience. the title is a play on the phrase "american born confused desi", used by indian immigrants as a disparaging term for first-generation indian-american (desi is hindi for "someone from our land", meaning india). it's a good mix of cross-cultural issues, with an ensemble cast that also covers the diverse nature of india itself: characters include hindus, muslims and sikhs. engaging & fun, this film is full of indian & western music, offering a good blend of cultures. smartly produced, it's a well-written story that is reminiscent of american teen films from the 80's.
