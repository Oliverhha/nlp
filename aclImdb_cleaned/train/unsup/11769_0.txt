in 1974, two american agents are sent to hungary by his superior miller (barry bostwick) to get werewolf blood for a secret project for the creation of an invincible warrior. one of the men dies and the other decides to inject the blood in himself, being killed by miller. there is a shutdown of the project. twenty years later, the scientist anne de carlo (kim delaney) is carrying out a research under the command of miller, and the body of the dead agent is delivered for the experiment. the agent is transformed in a kind of killing metallic werewolf. the screenplay of this low budget movie has no originality. i do not know how many films i have seen with this storyline. my vote is four. title (brazil): `metalbeast'
