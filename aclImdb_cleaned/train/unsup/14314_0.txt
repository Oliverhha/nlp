as far as adults are concerned, the first 20 minutes is pretty amazing from a visual point of view. the movie looks incredible. after that, the "dialogue" is bad, the scenery is weak, no real music, plot is a copy of land before time (with littlefoot). as far as kids are concerned, my 6 year old loved it and thought it was the coolest thing ever. my 5 year old was scared to death. there are some relatively graphic, violent scenes for a kids movie. i think for kids over a certain age (maybe 5!), it's no big deal, but i think it is too scary for very little kids. so go see the first 20 min, then leave and catch another movie that will challenge your brain a little (like road trip!) :)
