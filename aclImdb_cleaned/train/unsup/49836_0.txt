it would be hard to make a film as witless as king ralph without really trying. it's a shame, because the set-up (gauche, unclassy american takes over the stuffy british aristocracy) has good comic possibilities and goodman is generally a fun actor to watch. most of the problems are directly due to the feeble script and weak direction. there are no surprises and none of the comedy set-pieces are handled with any wit or skill. avoid this film like the plague.
