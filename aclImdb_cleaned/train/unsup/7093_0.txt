few series have more ably depicted the complicated communication between two people from different backgrounds who are trying to forge a relationship. mike and laura start out associating by accident and then by design. laura is prickly and easily offended. mike is well-intentioned but inarticulate. laura is an intellectual with strong family ties (especially to her long-suffering sister). mike is a landscaper with no family to speak of. they both feel like outsiders looking in most of the time. this series details their mutual yearnings and blunderings in a warm and humorous manner. by the end, i wanted terribly for these two awkward people to get together. a sign of a successful series. good acting, good writing, and good supporting characters. highly recommended.
