definitely one of the worst films of 1998, if not the worst, "the godson" lacks one fundamental element of comedy: humor. the cast, including rodney dangerfield, dom deluise (who spoofs don corleone), and kevin "kids in the hall" mcdonald, would make you think this film a riot. however, it is quite the contary. the jokes all fall flat, including the excrutiatingly unfunny "bank" skit, that just went on and on until i was squirming out of my chair - not from boredom (well, not only because of boredom) - but because like many of the "jokes," it outstays its welcome to a point of discomfort. overall, this movie is one of those films that should be seen only if you're in the mood for it. which should be never. 1/5 stars - john ulmer
