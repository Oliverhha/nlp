"gremlins" is a strange, disgusting, twisted, demented, bizarre, funny, and violence-packed comedy. see what they can fit into an hour and a half? "gremlins" is a down-right genuine classic. it's all about an inventor dad who buys a fuzzy-wuzzy creature for his son that will make you say, "can i have one?". but after the first half-hour, you say, "i never want one of those things!" why? (warning: possible spoiler!) because the three rules that were supposed to be followed are accidentally broken, and more fuzzy creatures pop up and then later turn into despicable and gruesome monsters. you'll get more laughs than you expected were in such a violent movie. i hope that you love this movie as much as i did and still do. 10/10
