only this and the bourne identity have a car chase that i rate this high, the fact that it was filmed in paris actually added to the authenticity of the chase. i thoroughly enjoyed this film, and aside from the car chase, i still get 'tingles' when watching the fight scene with the tall and short guys in the office, the soundtrack playing during this scene works very well with it. this is jet li at his best, without too much fighting, which (although i love it) can sometimes become a bit tiresome if you just get fight scene after fight scene. excellent use of the soundtrack, blistering stuff, which i can't rate less than 10!
