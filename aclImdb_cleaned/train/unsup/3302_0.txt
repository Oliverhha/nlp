gripping movie surrounding a world of religious beliefs, generational betrayals, and blackmail .throughout, the movie chimes a powerful message as voiced by the main character adam: "how many people have to die for you to make your point?" follow adam as he is guided through the darker sides of human nature while he tries to save his family, his unborn child, and his conviction. the writers were outstanding in the backdrop motivations for the characters  even down to a little boy thief. they do a good job of helping the viewer wrap realism around the "why". the "voice" on the phone of the terrorist was mesmerizing as he instructed adam on his journey through the philippine squatter camps and towns. it was a good choice to not put a face with the voice of adam's nemesis. the only real problem with the story is the "girlfriend" back story. the conversation between adam and the girl about her not wanting a "muslim" baby made no sense and was out of place considering their first conversation on the phone. it should have been left at that. over all, it was worth the watch so don't say i didn't tell ya!
