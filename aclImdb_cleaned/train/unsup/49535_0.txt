the lighting was bad, the dialog was stiff, unnatural and awkward, the camera work was jumpy and distracting, the sound was in and out, and the acting was terrible. there was nothing redeeming about this movie. given the cheesy blue screen work (a montage p-town scenes behind an actor as he marches/struts in place instead of actual film of the actor walking down a street), i should not have expected much, but this disappointed even my limited hope. low budget films have been done in the past - and done well (clerks), but this one feels and looks schlocky. after watching it, you feel that your 8 year old niece or nephew could have done better with the family camcorder. what amazes me is that miramax picked it up and released it.
