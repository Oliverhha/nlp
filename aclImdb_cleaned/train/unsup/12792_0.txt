where do i even start with this movie... this is probably the goofiest film i've ever seen in my life. no... scratch that. this is the goofiest film i've ever seen in my life. i've heard "hausu" is a cult horror classic in japan. fine. i admit, i can see where it would be a cult classic just because it is so strange. and if you really legitimately like it, that's fine. but i saw this with a bunch entire time. and what really bothers me is that in the post-screening discussion... even though no one could accurately explain or make sense of what they just watched... even though half the audience was making fun of it the entire time... even though it was an absolute goofy mess, not one person said it was bad. now, this may just be me, but i'm of the opinion that if you can't watch a movie
