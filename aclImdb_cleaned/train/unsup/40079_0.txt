possibly the most tedious war melodrama ever made. long, empty scenes. from a long, empty script. cate blanchett is an interesting looking woman, no doubt. but i think about half the running time of the film was composed of close-ups featuring her amazingly limited acting range: from nervous all the way to tremulous. after about twenty minutes, i just grew tired of looking at her. and in this film, she's about all there is. not one moment of this whole tortured mess felt genuine. but, hey, the scenery was great. so one has to give france and the second unit its props.
