i just watched this film for the first time in many years (thanks tcm), and it's still very funny. it's also touching, as the portrayal of early-adolescent personalities is often quite moving, between gut-busting guffaws. the stereotyped "little-league parents" are drawn very realistically as well, and joyce vanpatten's turn as the bitch of an equipment manager is superb. having been unathletic as a child, i still get damp around the eyes every time timmy lupus catches that fly ball. even though i would've hated him if i'd known him in school, i now admire the spunk and tenacity of tanner. there are lots of great lines in this script, and i look forward to seeing the film many more times. bears rule!
