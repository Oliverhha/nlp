angus is an overweight high school student who is great at science, has a crush on a popular girl named melissa and is bullied by rick. as a cruel joke, rick rigs the ballot box so that angus is nominated for king of the winter ball. angus also has a side kick little friend named troy. rick makes angus life a living hell but there is a great scene where angus gives him the tough lecture on "who is normal?" i thought that it was great because for once, angus was fighting back. a part that i didn't like was when rick and his friends hurt troy. that just broke my heart but other than that, i liked this movie a lot. so, if you are not normal, this would be a great movie to see.
