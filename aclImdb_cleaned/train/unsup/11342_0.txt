ok, firstly if your not a fan of rpg's or dungeons & dragons the game, you probably should stay away from this film. as a film its fairly average , with undistinguished, but not awful acting, low budget effects, and basic plot outline. that all said as a fantasy film its got all the requirements you want, villains, monsters, a party of heroes, inter-party conflict, and high magic. i really enjoyed this film, it contained many classic elements from the d&d game: from a vorpal sword, walls of fire, fiendish traps, strange monsters, and classic character classes. it was far more the film that the first outing should have been, which was truly awful. it is a shame it didn't have a larger budget. that said it sits high up in the ranks of fantasy movies.
