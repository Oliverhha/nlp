this is one of the most monumentally pointless movies i've ever seen. many have said that this is a dark look at youth, or that it is a daring indie film, and similar diagnoses. however, it's really just a movie for all the beavis and butthead fans out there, for whom plenty of sex and a bit of gunplay makes a film. the utter trashiness of this movie is found in the whole 666 gag. whenever the protagonists purchase something at a store, the bill totals $6.66. that basically sums up this movie's ambition to be daring and rebellious, but exposes how pathetic these attempts are. everyone who has branded this movie as dark and daring probably just read the tagline. maybe that's what makes this movie so 'daring.' i suppose that, in a way, making a pathetically bad film is daring, especially when you put your name on it. simply put, watching this movie is a painful experience. hopefully it will claim it's rightful place on the bottom 100 before long. however, maybe that's too good for it.
