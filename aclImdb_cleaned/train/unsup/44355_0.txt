an easy-going, boil-in-the-bag romcom. it relies a little too much on slapstick to fill out its 90 mins but, given its simple plot it's got enough wit and clean charm to make a go of it. i've always been a courtney cox fan and she definitely gives us something in addition to regurgitating monica gellerisms. david arquette is a sweet, chubby version of adam sandler. this slightly off-kilter duo, a real life couple, have a first-class straight 'guy' in viola davis (one of the better coloured actresses in hollywood, doing the day job stoically). there's nothing to it but it's not too sickly for a wet afternoon on the sofa. 3/10
