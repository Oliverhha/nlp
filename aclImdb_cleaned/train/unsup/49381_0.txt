i've seen all the other st trinians films, and the superb (and superior) forerunner the happiest days of their lives, so i felt that i should watch the 1980 film, even though i'd read somewhere that it was poor. poor is an understatement. the script is awful. the acting is dreadful - doubtless the cast would blame the script but really they are equally at fault. this is probably the worst british film that i've ever seen - certainly it's the worst portion of a franchise. if you think that some of the 1970s bond films are bad - try watching the wildcats of st trinians. not one for a blu-ray release...
