'attack the gas station' is a marvellous movie, and uncommonly difficult to describe. it's what would result if you gave quentin tarantino a sense of humour and a sense of good taste. and improved his ability to direct. and gave him good actors to work with. and an impeccable writer. the english subtitles are odd. at times their errors are amusing, and add something to the movie; at other times there has evidently been painstaking care taken to make the subtitles at least try to make sense - the attempt at translating a vocalised word-game is, while not perfect, a surprisingly good try. i've never before seen a movie constructed of a montage of separate scenes that came together so well as a whole. if only it weren't so hard to find this one.
