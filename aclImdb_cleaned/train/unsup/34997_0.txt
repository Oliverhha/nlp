balls of fury wasn't the total disaster, but when that's the highest praise i can offer, well, that's a problem. think of it as a lesser version of dodgeball. ping-pong just isn't that funny, or exciting, or anything really - just like the film. the idea of underground ping-pong never really goes anywhere. the jokes are okay at times, but there are no big laughs and certainly no surprises. unless you are a 10-14 year-old boy, who given the film's humor and scantily clad actresses is obviously the target market, you will probably be disappointed by paying money for it. wait a couple of years and watch it free some night on comedy central and spend your ten bucks somewhere else.
