i guess maybe i'd have been interested in this film if i knew anything about skateboarding or the history it documents. however, i didn't, and i was bored. this movie did nothing to encourage my interest; i didn't care about the characters or anything that was happening. furthermore, the stunts weren't even at all impressive (except a few shots of modern trick skateboarders at the very end, and even that was just impressive by comparison). don't go see this film unless you know you're already interested. at least it's short.
