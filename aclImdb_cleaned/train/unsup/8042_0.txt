this drive-in rarity is perhaps best enjoyed in the same style as mystery science theatre 3000. plot: alternately silly and confusing. a doctor suffers a minor car accident, then, strangely enough, a young lady has a car accident right next to him. he goes for help at the conveniently-nearby spooky castle. strangeness ensues. dialog: originally in italian. on the dvd version, you get your choice of dubbing and subtitles. both are bizarre and occasionally howlingly funny. special effects: mostly performed by shaking the camera. a few smokepots. a monster that is about as lame as ed wood's giant octopus from "bride of the monster." music: you got the synthesizer and the bongos, but no wocka-wocka guitars or plinked wires. thankfully, no moaning. vampiric lesbianism? of course. it's required in movies like this. female nudity? yes, since the plot *absolutely*required* that the female lead wander about the castle and its exterior in either a towel wrap, a negligee, or nothing at all. male nudity? a short special-guest appearance by the half-red-and-half-blue guys from star trek (or their poor cousins) in thong loincloths. sex? eh, barely as racy as your typical r rated motel fare. gross? hardly. funny? often,
