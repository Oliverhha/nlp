funny how opinions can be different. i perfectly respect that many people found this movie a masterpiece, but i found it pretentious, dishonest, an external exhibition of "misery" full of stereotypes (and quite a conservative undertone). the fallen angel girl that goes through resurrection, the madonna mother, bad (really really b a d) father, abusive and violent self-centered boyfriend, etc. all too calculated, staged for my taste - there are so many other films that i find more honest in their portray of poverty and misery (but with dignity). and then above all the fairy-tale end. it seemed so much like an imagined poverty by someone who has never experienced it. the only thing that i really enjoyed of that film was the erotic electricity between two older characters that is a rare thing to see in a movie. in my opinion, that gives an extra start to this movie.
