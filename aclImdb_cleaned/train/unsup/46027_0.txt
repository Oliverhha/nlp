i still love this movie. more because of the music than the film itself. i think it's interesting to follow actors' careers over the years. victor garber (jesus) has been a very prolific actor over the years. his most recent is that of jack bristow on tv's alias. one of the other actors is lynne thigpen who later went on to play the police captain on "where in the world is carmen sandiego?" and most recently as ellie mae farmer in "the district" before her untimely death. she had a beautiful singing voice. in one of the scenes notice that they are dancing on top of the world trade center as it is still being built. there are several scenes when you can see it in the background about 3/4 finished. this is an important film for its historical content. the jesus movement was big around 1973-74. at the time, many of the hippie set were trying to make jesus out to be nothing more than a guru. this film was a solid attempt to reach people with the gospel of jesus while still portraying him as the messiah rather than just a spiritual leader.
