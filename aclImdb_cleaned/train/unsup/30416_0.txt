a perfect little gem of a film, which the other posters here have attested to quite well. peter o'toole fans and those who can remember that time in our history would do themselves a great service in seeing this movie. it gets shown on tcm once and a while (good on them), this is where i had re-stumbled upon it after many, many years. i am also delighted to hear that it was released on dvd a couple of years ago, and i am eager to check it out. this is one of my favorite movies. reason being, i remember seeing this as a kid with my grandfather, who was very much a part of that era. i've never seen anyone laugh harder at a movie, ever. seriously. though he passed away a long time ago, it's one of my favorite memories, of him just laughing out load in uproarious delight. that's what movies should do.
