silly horror movie. possible spoilers!!!! a mad doctor runs an insane asylum (how original--heavy sarcasm). for reasons not worth mentioning he needs the blood of inmates to stay alive. that's it. no vampires at all, but the film does open with a pretty bloody (and totally unnecessary) staking. the film looks great--beautiful sets, nice color and some fairly strong violence (for 1958). but that's about it. the plot is stupid, the pace is slow and it gets pretty dull. donald wolfit, as the doctor, really tries to give a good performance but he's stuck in some lousy makeup that makes him look like bela lugosi (why?)! also check out the even worse makeup on his deformed assistant carl (victor maddern). vincent ball (horrible actor but handsome) and barbara sheeley (beautiful as always but given nothing to do) are the obligatory nice, innocent couple. not unwatchable but of very little interest either.
