a parasite that's "a combination of aphrodisiac and venereal disease, and will hopefully turn the world into one beautiful, mindless orgy" is unleashed in a luxurious canadian hotel. despite director/writer david cronenberg's script, "shivers" isn't any great shakes as a bloody erotic horror. mr. cronenberg shows promise, however; and, there are at least three sexy scenes. first, you have luscious lynn lowry (as forsythe) sexily undressing. also, still beautiful barbara steele (as betts) takes a bath; and, later, engages susan petrie (as janine tudor) in a seductive lesbian kiss. other than that, the movie features more bad hair than anything else. cronenberg could have gotten off more steam by setting the maniacal sex parasites loose in a college. paul hampton (as roger st luc) emerges as the last man standing. imaginatively re-titled "they came from within" and "the parasite murders". **** shivers (10/10/75) david cronenberg ~ paul hampton, lynn lowry, barbara steele, joe silver
