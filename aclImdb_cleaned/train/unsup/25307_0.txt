this delicious, light, romantic comedy is full of surprises and subtleties. witfully written and superbly acted, it shows why juliette binoche is such a great asset to modern cinema. outstanding chemistry with reno makes this theatrical play made into a movie a rewarding experience to be treasured. juliette binoche shows her talent in a totally different role to most of the ones she has played before, she is a common girl with many problems and yet her nobility and class shine through. if you like binoche you will love jet lag. don't miss it.
