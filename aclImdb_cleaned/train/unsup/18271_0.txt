approach with this material is truthful, and to not be exploitive in the sense of manipulative characters (situations are another thing). there's really not much in way of it going through anything specific in being stylized. if anything, as i recall, malle's style here is to try and not seem apparent much, if that's possible, with a very controlled sense of pacing and how scenes should move.
