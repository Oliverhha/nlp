this was a fun movie. it's not to be taken seriously, and anyone who does will be disappointed. it's supposed to be a humorous take on the trabbi and east/west germany. lighten up, folks. if you really know what's going on, you'll appreciate the subtle jokes more. i'd much rather watch this again than eurotrip, which was just a blatant take on american's view of europe. driving me crazy was refreshing and silly with mild historical undertones. please, give this film a chance. i really enjoyed it and the people i showed it to did as well. this is a good movie to show a german class for a fun movie day or something.
