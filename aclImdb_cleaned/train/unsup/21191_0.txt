cary grant's got a real problem on his hands. he thinks his wife was killed in a plane crash seven years ago. so he goes to court with gail patrick who he now intends to marry after getting the judge to declare irene dunne legally dead. wouldn't you know it, dunne turns up the day of the honeymoon and puts cary in an awful pickle. he's a lawyer and he's busy trying to work out all the ramifications of what's happened. i've thought about it for a while and i came to the conclusion that it's grant's professional training that prevents him from just confessing to one and all what's happened. where a lot of the laughs come in is grant trying to avoid marital consummation with patrick until he can work it out. another factor comes into play when grant learns that dunne spent several years on the desert island with hunky randolph scott. grant starts to feel a little less guilty then. the whole mess is dumped on the judge who originally declared dunne legally dead, granville bates. his role as the judge gives him some of the best lines in my favorite wife. i do feel sorry for gail patrick though. usually she plays a lot of bad girls and other women in movies. but she really is the wronged party here. dunne and grant worked well together in another marital comedy, the awful truth and they were just as bright in my favorite wife as they were in the first film. one interesting footnote it was a remake of this film something's gotta give that marilyn monroe did not complete. eventually it was made over with doris day and james garner. that one was good, but this one is great.
