i had the misfortune to sit through the full 102 minutes of what, in my opinion, is this shockingly bad film. it fails on pretty much all levels; the cast is awful, the acting - ham at best and the plot lacks any depth, leaving me feeling violently apathetic as to the outcome of any of the convoluted story lines. plan b has none of the charm this genre has the scope to convey and i found myself physically cringing at the various points where the script makes its regular misjudged meanderings anywhere towards the region of comedy. a bona fide saccharine coated turd of a movie.
