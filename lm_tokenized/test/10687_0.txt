hoping for another academy nod , jean - xavier de lestrade unsuccessfully tries to duplicate the gritty , grainy realism that won maha productions its first oscar . de lestrade's earlier cinematic critique of the us justice system , " un coupable ideal , " won the 2002 academy award for best documentary feature . unfortunately , maha's second movie about a murder on a sunday morning fails to capture any of the reality of the north carolina murder case , and ends up being nothing short of fantasy . the filmmaker has bent over backward to create the impression that the accused killer , michael peterson , is the innocent victim of a wide - spread conspiracy . jean - xavier de lestrade's strangely biased movie masquerading as a real - life documentary represents a tragic fall from the honesty of his other work .
