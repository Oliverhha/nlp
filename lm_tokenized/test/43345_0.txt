rubbish is actually an understatement . its hard to even begin to describe how terrible this movie is . i understand the fact that some movies require you to suspend your belief , but this is just pushing it too far . the story makes absolutely no sense , the plots are too damn confusing and the acting is just horrifying . that said , it can be somewhat entertaining if you don't wear your thinking caps . paresh rawal and johnny lever do give you some giggles but the rest of them do no justice to their acting talents . sharmila tagore and om puri are some of the finest actors around in bollywood and it is sad to see them do roles such as these . the music is crappy , the special effects are crappier and chunkey pandey is the crappiest of them all ! avoid watching this movie as it is just not worth it .
