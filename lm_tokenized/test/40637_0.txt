' as for comparing it to gta , i can see how people can think that . yet something is off with the surroundings . you see , the gta series has found a way to make their cities have a different look for different areas . in spiderman 2 , everything has the same ' blah ' quality to it , throughout the city . it almost feels like you're web - slinging through a map of cubes rather than buildings . also , the graphics look like they were made for the original playstation as opposed to ps2 . i wouldn't recommend this game , in my opinion , the original spider - man video game for the ps1 was much more fun than this one . at least it didn't get so old so quick . this game leaves you wanting more . but not in a good way .
