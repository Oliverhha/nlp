this is a big , loud , colorful tragicomic and articulate satire on human society , media , politics and the most inner desire and organic need of man to destroy . there has been criticism on the high content of violence , and has been categorized as instigating , but it's actually quite obvious that it's just a satiric view of contemporary " personal success " driven society . all characters are natural born killers , not only the ones doing the actual killing . they are all shown as predators with no morals , who will do anything to achieve their goals . the movie will take you flying , smash your brains and feast your eyes . . . , it will feed your senses , it will make you love it and hate it . combining the brilliant early writing skills of tarantino , oliver stone's addiction to violence with the brilliant performance of woody harrelson , juliette lewis , and all the supporting cast ( rodney dangerfield , robert downey jr , tom sizemore and tommy lee jones ) . fantastic soundtrack and graphics . it's the eye opener . the ultimate 90's movie . it's a ride !
