i loved this movie . it was suspenseful and kept my interest throughout . al pacino and ellen barkin were perfect in their roles . both were playing characters of a type that they had played before - - so perhaps not too original . but the film was well made and carried the viewer along . although some of it was obvious , i for one had not guessed the ending . overall , this movie interested me much more than did a top - 20 box office film i saw this past weekend at the theater ( which shall remain anonymous here ) . pacino played his role very well ; his performance harkened back to his prime in the 1970s when he could be absolutely riveting on screen . ellen barkin in this film is playing the sexy kind of role that she played all during the 1980s . she is very good in this one . john goodman was also very good as the police partner of pacino . his sort of light easy attitude ( even when he was very serious ) played very well against pacino's dark and brooding persona .
