i had no idea gony was set in the 1800's , and so the film came as quite a shock to me . i had expected mafia - type action but instead i was treated with a fantastic yet dark world of betrayal and blood and an excellent black comedy . the only complaint is the fact that cameron diaz can't act ! she barely keeps her olde american accent consistent , and looks way too thin to be authentic . and the lines they give her are laughable at best , sounding more like lines from an essay than an actual person .
