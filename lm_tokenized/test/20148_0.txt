i saw this as a special showing in memphis , tn . i know the owner of the publishing company and therefore have read this book many times and have recommended it to countless people . this movie was exceptional . abigail breslin was just as charming and entertaining as " little miss sunshine " and drew fuller did an an amazing job making you believe he truly went through this inspiring story . i left the theatre choked up and feeling warm and relishing in the emotions the movie took me through . i plan on taking everyone i know to see this when it comes out in march .
