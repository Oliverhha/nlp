" home alone 2 : lost in new york " is young macaulay culkin's second greatest hour in the series as kevin mccallister , who has to learn to be a man again on the mean streets of the big apple after being separated from his family once more . he checks into a swank manhattan hotel ( where donald trump is apparently staying at too ) . he's pursued again by comic bad guys harry and marv ( joe pesci and daniel stern ) , and director chris columbus and screenwriter john hughes keep the action and laughs coming . tim curry also appears as a concierge who has to prove that kevin is on his own , and brenda fricker is the pigeon lady who watches over kevin's little shoulder . there is another tricks & traps showdown at the end , which keeps the slapstick and comic violence at an all - time high . 8 / 10
