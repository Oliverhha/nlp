this has got to be one of the funniest films i have ever seen ! nick ( cary grant ) was married to ellen ( irene dunne ) for 4 years before she was shipwrecked , and presumed to be dead . 7 long years later , he remarries ( to gail patrick ) - just as ellen has finally been rescued . she turns up at his place only to find that he's on his honeymoon , so she hightails it to the hotel to go surprise him . nick gets himself into an awful pickle trying to find the right time to tell his new wife ( whom he has been married to for only a few hours ) what has happened , and also telling the children that the new lady that they are enjoying the company of is actually their mother . the plot can only thicken when nick finds out that ellen wasn't alone on that island for 7 years , but she had company in the shape of a very athletic ( and very single ! ) man . . funnier than grant and dunne's previous comedy , the awful truth , and just as sweet as penny serenade ; this is a definite 10 out of 10 !
