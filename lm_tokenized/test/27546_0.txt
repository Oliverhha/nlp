like all the haters , i to thought flash gordon was pretty bad in the beginning . the " hawkmen " episode just about lost me , but i have a high tolerance for cheese . then something unexpected happened - - the show got better ! the writers figured out who the characters were and came up with some plot lines that are actually interesting . now the characters are fun to watch and i am genuinely interested in how the story progresses . they've struck the right balance between humor , camp , and drama as to be reminiscent of earlier seasons of sg - 1 . flash gordon has moved from a show i watched because nothing else was on to a show i look forward to every week . what a difference a few episodes can make ( specifically the " til death " episode ) . if you haven't seen it since the first few episodes , take a look at it now . i hope this one gets a second season .
