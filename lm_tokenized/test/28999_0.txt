* this comment may contain spoilers * who wants to be a superhero is a reality show that seems to be far from reality . i saw a few ads for the show and was mildly interested but never actually got around to watch it . then while being stuck and bored in my hotel i decided to give it a try . i loved it . i like superheroes so i was interested of course , but you don't have to to like the show . stan challenges the type of person they are on the inside . sometimes it can be very goofy and funny and other times you feel for the characters . it surprised me , and i couldn't stop talking about it . the eliminations are based off of what happens and not on appearance or popularity , it's real , unlike many other reality shows . my mother watched one episode with me after i told her about it and now she can't wait for the new episode . it may look weird but it's a good show .
