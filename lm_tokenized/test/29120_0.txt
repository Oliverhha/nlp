i must say , for a first time director / actor / writer . . . mr . cherot has many skills . he acts without pretension , the dialogue is snappy and realistic , and the plot revolves around the characters , instead of some contrived formula . my only question is with some of the acting , but damn . . . for a low budget , first time debut - - - this movie impressed the hell out of me . this movie happens like real life , like a seinfeld episode . . . it's quirky and blunt . and i give mad props for the ending , which playfully satirized the movie and genre itself . . . and it confirms the courage and honesty i liked so much from the beginning . . . enjoy this one without your ego , just watch it and be absorbed !
