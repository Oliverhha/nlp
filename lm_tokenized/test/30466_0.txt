i think ' mind game ' is one of those movies that you either really like or really hate , but never in the middle . i spent first 20 minutes itching to turn it off since the animation style didn't seem all that pleasant , but then things just kept happening and before i knew it i was really immersed in it . ' mind game ' has everything : irony , action , humour , even romance ; and it touches practically every aspect of human life , going from relationships to domestic problems . all that covered in psychedelic imagery leaves a great impression . you might experience various emotions watching this movie but one thing for sure , you won't feel like you've wasted your time when it's over .
