this romantic and somewhat humorous drama fails to arouse much interest . i really enjoyed the rain scenes , otherwise it seems the effort was put together at the last minute . a hard luck woman ( robin wright penn ) tries to make for a better life while her volatile husband ( sean penn ) spends ten years in the ' looney bin ' . there is a lot of wasted talent assembled here : james gandolfini , harry dean stanton and john travolta . this movie seems to take too long to deliver its message . not a very attractive story and the strong language needed for realism gets old very quick .
