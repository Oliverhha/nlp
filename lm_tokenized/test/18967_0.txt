( you should also check him out in young tom edison for another example of this ) i actually think the 80s tele - movie with ricky shroder and alec guiness worked slightly better than this version does ; the characters are a lot more casual in their delivery , and the story flows better . but this is a pretty good version on its own terms any rate .
