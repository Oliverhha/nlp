continuation of the events in the original " evil dead " movie . after recapping the events of the first movie ( telling the story somewhat differently , even eliminating three of the characters ) , this movie has poor , tormented ash ( bruce campbell ) constantly being harassed and tortured by sadistic demons - including , in the movie's most memorable sequence , his own possessed hand . absolutely hilarious ; this effective mix of hardcore horror and slapstick comedy provides a lot of laughs , impressive effects , and the very game campbell putting up with a lot as the film - makers set out to abuse his character as much as possible . " evil dead 2 " manages to be even more entertaining than the first movie , a goofy and fun horror / comedy with a chaotic finale that i would recommend to any fan of this genre . what is also funny is that the character of ash is already showing signs of the super - obnoxious moron he would become in " army of darkness " . i loved " evil dead 2 " . 9 / 10
