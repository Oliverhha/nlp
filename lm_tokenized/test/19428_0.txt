it's always the short ( < 30 episodes ) anime series that get me , and elfen lied is no exception . at a mere 14 ( 13 + 1 extra ova ) episodes , i was kind of doubtful it was gonna be any good when my friend recommended it to me . i was wrong . best , most intense , most depressingly emotional show i have ever watched . this thing is a masterpiece . instantly became my favourite anime ( and show ) of all time , beating out death note and the like . highly recommended ! in fact , i pity those who have not watched this . some people are turned off instantly by the gore and nudity , but these people are ignorant pussies ( to put it bluntly ) . ps : don't forget to read the manga to find out the real ending . yes , the manga is better than the anime , and that's saying something .
