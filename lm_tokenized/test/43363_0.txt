i had been looking forward to seeing this film for about a month after i saw an interview with ben kingsley talking about it . i was very disappointed . i found it slow , pretentious and really flat . i thought the two main characters were vain , shallow and unlikeable . penelope cruz's performance was far better than ben kingsley's but was nowhere near her usual standards , such as volver . she has much greater ability than was displayed here . the background music was laboured and intrusive . all in all i found it horridly contrived and i had to keep looking at my watch to see how much longer i had to suffer it before it ended .
