firstly , i want to say i went into this film not expecting a boring drama . what i got was completely the opposite . an exciting , powerful , drama . it was excellent . the acting was great , the direction was great , and the soundtrack was absoutley the best score i have heared in a long time . if i do have any problems with the film , it would have been the length . it was a little too long , and one or two of the scenes that made it long were not really needed , but the rest of it was great . it was also a challenge to watch in some places . like a book is considered a hard read , this was a hard watch in some places , and you really have to concentrate to enjoy it , but the rewards of doing so are huge . definitley , if you are a fan of good cinema , see it ! if you don't really like these films , then it still may be worth a look ! highly recommended ! 5 out of 5 .
