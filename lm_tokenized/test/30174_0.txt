i love short story films , especially when , of one particular theme , they are connected to satisfy a feature length running time . like taking any subway / underground / metro train , with subway stories , if you don't like one particular story , wait a few minutes and a better one will come along . of course everyone will have their particular favourites , but for me fern's heart of darkness , sax cantor riff , manhattan miracle , love on the a train , and underground were simply original and wonderful . i'm english and lived in london for four years so these types of stories are not exclusive to new york . where there are people , there are possibilities .
