not since alex cox's " straight to hell " have i had such a shoddy , amateurish work put before me by a director whose work i otherwise like . i'm a believer that there are no bad concepts , just bad execution , so while it may have been possible to make a good movie using jarmusch's structure and theme , this is not that movie . two minutes ' worth of concept was regularly dragged out to ten minutes . the dialogue was half - considered , if that . vignettes ground painfully away towards some ill - conceived , inane " punchline " . the performers , even granted that they had little to work with , were visibly floundering ; given that they were for the most part playing themselves , they never came across as people who actually existed , or that they had any reason to talk to the people playing opposite them ; most often , they couldn't even convince me that they had ever actually drank coffee before . only cate blanchett walks away from this car - wreck with any dignity intact . i'm normally quite generous in my ratings , but this movie was simply insultingly bad . jarmusch should have kept the only print locked away in a vault , with instructions that it be burnt following his death . 2 / 10 , and that's only because i smiled when bill murray drank straight from the pot .
