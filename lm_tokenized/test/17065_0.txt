howard hawks was completely on top of his game when he shot this film . it is an enduring , eternal film classic . he took an already popular story , " the front page " , and fixed it by creating the female hildy . this came about during a party , at which hawks , holding forth , was going to have the party goers read " the front page " for a lark . so he hands around the scripts , and discovers there aren't enough men . a woman gets the hildy part . and the rest , movie - lovers , is cinema history . what a master stroke ! great casting ! great acting ! great direction , and photography ! great screenplay that nicely serves up big city corruption and dances around the unscrupulous abuse of 1st amendment freedoms by the press . all with taste , artistic and noble . and unbelievably funny throughout . perfect .
