this movie is the adaptation of stefan zweig's story " letter from an unknown woman " to china settling in a time period before the cultural revolution ( 1930 - 1948 ) . the director ( xu jinglei ) who is also the main actress of the movie has created a very nice visual atmosphere to reflect a heartbreaking one - sided love story . the performances of the players and the music of the movie are successful . although the tempo of the movie becomes unnecessarily slow at certain parts , the overall result is quite satisfactory . if you are interested in asian culture and the living style , you will find an additional taste in this movie .
