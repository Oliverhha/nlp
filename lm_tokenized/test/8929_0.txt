nicely done low budget horror movie . shot in manhattan and definitely creates a genuine nyc feel through long shots , local sites and color . acting is surprisingly good and not cringe producing at all . gives good introduction to all the characters and actually produces some empathy . interesting subplot of daughter returning from iraq and leads subtle romance with neighbor . the old men upstairs are a hoot . gore - and - horror - wise i found it satisfying and gripping to watch . a nice 28 - days - later vibe done on a much smaller scale . the creatures were cool . plenty of flesh - eating and blood and guts . low expectations can produce a very pleasant viewing experience . this movie did . congrats to all involved and i look forward to future projects by these folks .
