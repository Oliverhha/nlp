i saw this film at the austin film festival last week . definitely above average , but there were places where the story felt disjointed , such as why max has such an attitude about western women - - the opening scene doesn't do it for me . and a little more about why max is running into financial trouble would have been nice . overall , a very good movie with standout performances from warburton , marie matiko and eric roberts . what i liked most is that this was a non - threatening exploration of spirituality and attitudes towards marriage , as well as the genuine emotions evoked at the end of the film . it deserves a wide release and the opportunity to find an audience beyond the festival circuit .
