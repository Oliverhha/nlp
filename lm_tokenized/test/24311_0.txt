robot chicken is a stop - motion animated show , using action figures , made by seth green . each episode consists of different skits . some of them are only a few seconds while others can be five minutes . this show parodies many different things . from movies to video games to celebrities to t . v shows etc . if you can think on anything robot chicken will make a sketch about it . some sketches are completely random that make no sense . i have to say that this is my favourite animated show . i rofl every episode . this show is not for the kids as it is very crude and sometimes violent . if you are a teenager or a young adult than this show is for you . trust me it will not disappoint . 5 / 5 .
