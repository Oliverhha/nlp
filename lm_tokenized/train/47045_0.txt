the film distractingly switches between black and white and color , as well as time . it goes from the present to the past , and back a number of times through the feature . we are shown how several individuals live in the drug environment ( ending up in prison ) , getting the feeling we are to be sorry for the way they have lived their lives . the dialogue is filled with four letter words . maybe that's the language that is used in this community , but it seems to add little to the content of the story of this man's life . if there is a message in this film , it's the wrong one . drugs and prisons seem glorified and accepted as a way of life . we all have choices . the people depicted in this film , for the most part , made the wrong ones .
