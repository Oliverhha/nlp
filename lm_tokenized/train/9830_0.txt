many elements of this movie weren't bad in concept . i can hardly fault the acting , campy style , special effects , or even the plot . unfortunately , the movie fails in directing and editing , which trumps all the others . for example , the plot is basically species , but the writing was horrid , and the characters were not believable as a result . are you going to believe that a college kid is still flirting with a space alien after he's seen tentacles ? no . same for the special effects , the tentacle effects could have been great if they slowed them down by a factor of 10 or so . again , just bad directing on that . it all makes you wonder how the crew burned through $ 5 million ; they certainly didn't pay the writing and editing crew anything .
