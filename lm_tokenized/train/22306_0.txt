this show is one of the british classics . a comedy gem like only fools & horses , fawlty towers and a few select others . the show's original and never - before - attempted space setting blends with clever , rapid fire jokes to bring laughter to anyone's face . the show's style changed throughout the years , changing from a traditional sitcom ( albeit in space ) to a drama series in the later years . but throughout the changing ways and new characters and settings , the humour always remained top notch . because of this , i find it impossible to single out a " best episode " or even " best series " . some of the finest jokes i've ever heard come from red dwarf . and the fact that the americans made their own version just shows how brilliant it must be . if you haven't seen it before , quickly rush out and buy series 1 through 8 . and if you've already got them , sit down and watch . red dwarf , in one word , is ( as lister would say ) " fan - smegging - tastic ! "
