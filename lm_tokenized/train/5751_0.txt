this series of eight videos is by far my favorite japanese animation series . my other favorites are " akira " ( superb details , excellent coloring scheme , artistic aspects ) and " ghost in the shell " ( great opening scene , strong atmosphere ) but my heart is captured by " bubblegum crisis " and its cast of female characters . one of strongest features ( esp . in the first episodes ) is the soundtrack ; albeit in general japanese pop music sounds pretty childish to most listeners , the songs from " bubblegum crisis " have something special to them .
