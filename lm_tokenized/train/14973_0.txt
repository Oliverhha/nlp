an action movie is made to entertain , and should always be taken at face value . with this in mind air force one hits the mark , despite some minor flaws it still delivers tension , drama and action in abundance . the cast is strong and full of believable characters , the story is compelling and the traditional dose of sentiment is added sparingly and to good effect . harrison ford and gary oldman both put in solid performances and they are well supported by the likes of glenn close and wendy crewson . overall air force one is an safe bet for the average movie fan and should entertain most , without ever really excelling . 7 / 10
