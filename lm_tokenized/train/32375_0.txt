" . i tried to use logic to piece together things that people did and said . i obviously went into this film with wrong expectations . i thought there'd be a plot , i thought there'd be a mystery . i should have read ebert and weirs ' comments prior to watching the film and i'd realize that there is no plot and that the director didn't want you to even think for one moment that there was the possibility of an answer . it is a film that is intentionally obtuse . if you're looking for a dreamlike escapade or something to soothe you to sleep then perhaps this film is for you . ps : this film is not based on any true events any more than the blair witch project is . search the internet for more information and educate yourselves .
