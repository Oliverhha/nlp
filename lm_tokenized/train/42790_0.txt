this movie's only redeeming features are the moments when you cant help laughing out loud at the overblown clichés . such as when arn is sad and riding through the rain . or when cecila is happy and starts to dance . or every time joakim netterkvist makes his " sad puppy " face , or whenever the dramatic string orchestra starts playing . on the plus side this happens a lot . on the minus side , the rest of the time it just hurts to watch it . you feel sorry for everyone involved , especially those who paid for it . everybody , the actors , the director , the editor , seems to be nervous working on such a big budget project ( the most expensive swedish film ever ) and scared to make mistakes , and this total lack of confidence shines through everywhere . impossible though it seems , this actually makes the books look good in comparison . hell , it makes kingdom of heaven seem good . and that is quite an achievement .
