like ' pigs in a polka ' and ' the band concert ' , this cartoon short centres on a piece of music with an inventive animated theme - this time , the ' orchestra ' are workmen building the ' umpire ' state building , whether the little elevator dog or the scrawny cats knocking pegs into the ground . beautiful colour and music enhance this short , which was oscar nominated but just pipped to the post . the foreman , with baton and whistle , monitors and directs the action , including an inventive bricklayer and a lift which can dodge obstructions . wildly funny and well constructed , ' rhapsody in rivets ' is a real find , and is a merrie melodie well worth tracking down .
