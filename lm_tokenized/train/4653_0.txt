i watched this movie in the preview in my university . i must admit that when my friend told me what it was about i thought it would be one of those dull good - intentioned but poorly made movie . i was wrong . this movie is powerful and transmits a lot . doesn't pretend to feel pity , and even though the beginning might be even considered dramatic , it made me laugh out loud several times . it's the first review i write , but i definitely thought i should drop a line to defend this beautiful story that talks about what can a person do for a loved one , about re - discovering yourself , about avoiding stupid dogmas . . . and at the end it leaves you with a broad smile and a sense of tenderness that not so commonly a movie inflicts you . if you like beautiful stories , this is your movie .
