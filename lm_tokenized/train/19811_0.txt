i've recommended this film to countless friends , and the reviews are always mixed . i lived in battle creek for 3 years so perhaps it just means more to me . this film was in fact shot in ny and nc , not battle creek , but they still did a great job portraying the romance of the early 1900's . the performances are top notch and the actors in this film are give their best . it's a great film , but not a critically acclaimed movie . dana carey could've had more lines , but what i want to know is what happened to the character actor who played him as a child , this kid was really great and could've been huge . the one liners in this movie are classic . it's one of those films that you think is funny , but when you recall it at a later date , its even funnier . so get a big , buttered bowl of popcorn and enjoy it for what it is ! a hilarious tale of the wacky health craze of the early 1900's .
