babylon ad is a mess of a film . i liked other vin diesel films like chronicles of riddick ( silly but fun ) . unfortunately this film just doesn't work with the story just all over the place . the film starts off pretty normally but as it goes on the plot begins to crack and by the end is falls apart . what makes this worse is that nothing is explained and i just wonder why half the stuff that happened did happen . the acting is ok but no - one stands out , vin diesel just sounds bored throughout the film . the action scenes are run of the mill , nothing stands out and a lot are just silly and boring . i think somewhere in the film there might have been an interesting story , unfortunately somewhere along the way the makers of the film have forgotten that . overall a woeful effort .
