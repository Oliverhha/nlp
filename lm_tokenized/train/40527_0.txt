i'd heard a lot about this film - apparently the original was superb and nicely psychologically dark . this , however , was just a disappointment , positives of the movie : the two lead ' men in white ' had their nicely eerie moments , and played their parts pretty damned well . some of the forth - wall breaking was very well done , however it just wasn't done enough . the father's acting was good , and his pain was well simulated . as for the rest of the film . . . i thought the moments of drawn - out nothingness were , at times , effective . but when you look at the brevity of the film , and the amount of cinema time actually wasted , it just didn't seem to add up . naomi watts was wooden and pretty appalling , the dialogue had definitely seen better days , the supposed ' brutality ' was surprisingly disappointing . i wasn't expecting much from what i thought was going to be a standard horror movie . perhaps the main reason i was so disappointed was because at moments the screenshots , dialogue and directing seemed superb . however , it was lost in bad dialogue , mediocre acting and a poor sense of suspense . watch the original . this remake was a complete waste of time .
