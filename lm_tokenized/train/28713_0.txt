i picked up this movie because i'll watch any horror movie . i was surprised to find it was actually pretty good and fairly scary . the acting ranged from quite good to ham , and although i wished the scarecrow didn't talk it was still a pretty creepy - looking horror villain . jeff burr has proven himself to be an okay genre director . his first movie , the offspring , was great , and his stepfather and pumpkinhead sequels were better than average . even leatherface was okay , though his version of the movie was seriously tampered with before release . night of the scarecrow doesn't make me long to see his other movies , but i'd pick them up if i tripped over them .
