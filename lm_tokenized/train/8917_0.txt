this is a horrific film , made all the worse because it's all true . annabel chong is an intelligent , articulate middle class student from singapore , where her parents are so proud that she's managed to get to america . unfortunately , what she does in america is make porn films - films like " what's a nice girl like you doing in an anal movie ? " . this film is structured around her record breaking attempt to have sex with 251 men . and it is exceptionally grim . en route , we meet the characters who work in the porn industry , see annabel's friends and realise that she is very , very screwed up . this is a snapshot of humanity at its lowest . after watching it , all your liberal assumptions are challenged . a grim but essential film .
