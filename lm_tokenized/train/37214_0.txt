why did i rate it a 3 ? 1 ) because it's slow , and pretty boring . there's little effort from the production to create any suspense at all . . . 2 ) because although some of the technical aspects are rather accurate ( the dumpster diving and all the social engineering ) , most of them are truly absurd . * spoiler * : the free kevin phrase , at the atm ? gimme a break . 3 ) it's totally one - sided . it's even written by shimomura . 4 ) pretty common to all hacker movies : quite shallow . why didn't they even bother to depict the hackers ' spirit a bit more in depth ? i truly believe the whole sequence with amanda peet could have been much more useful had they explored mitnick's principles and beliefs , instead of bringing up the 1st amendment and how interesting scanning can be . wouldn't recommend it to anyone . if you really want to know the true story , check out the documentary " freedom downtime " .
