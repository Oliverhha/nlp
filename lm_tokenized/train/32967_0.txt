i hate those movie reviews that really hang it heavy on the movie in question , but after just spending the last 100 minutes of my life watching the legend of lucy keyes , i can't help but think i've been seriously ripped off and am about to do some serious hanging ! compared on the dvd cover to the magnificent nicole kidman flick " the others " , the legend of lucy keyes is nothing but a poor b grade movie that's borrowed a little bit from this film and a little bit from that film - see the barn in " the ring " and the indians in " the missing " . the special effects in " lucy " are appalling . seriously , ghost busters has better special effects in comparison to today's standards which should have made the spook in " lucy " much more believable . and what's with all the open windows - it's late autumn in this movie and bedroom windows are left open just a little to add to the supposed mood with flowing curtains and blowing tree branches . but the big questions is this - what on earth is julie delpy ( before sunset ) doing in a movie like this ? after the 2004 mini series remake of frankenstein she should have known better than to sign on for this one ! the legend of lucy keyes may very well be just the thing for some movie goers , and good luck to ' em . to each their own . but after a very brief promising few minutes at the start , for me , this movie just became more and more predictable and ridiculous .
