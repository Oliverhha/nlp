dr . magrew ( george peck ) strives to give human attributes to expertly crafted wooden puppets . emily harrison who plays jane magrew is the bright spot of the movie . but the puppets themselves are the real stars . the killer puppets do away with the bad guys . the action scenes are good , but there are too few to get excited about . even with the finale slamming the door on you ; this mild horror movie is still fun to watch . peck is less animated than the puppets . also starring are michael guerin , josh green and jason dean booher .
