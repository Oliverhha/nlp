melies had a sense of playfulness about his films , and this is one of the more amusing ones : melies takes a head - - which is melies ' head , attaches it to some bellows , and blows it up like a balloon . true , there's no deep thought involved , but there needn't be . it's less than a minute and easily sustains the one joke . melies was inventing techniques as he went along , borrowing from other media , just as d . w . griffith shortly would . there is an exuberance in seeing something done for the first time that is sorely lacking in later uses of the same editing effect . i suppose it's because melies was having fun himself .
