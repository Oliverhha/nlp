for such a low budget movie and from prc as well , the brute man is quite good . as a result of an experiment going wrong at university , hal moffat becomes disfigured and some years later , starts killing people . his victims include a grocer's delivery boy and a jeweller . he makes friends with a blind woman but nearly kills her after shopping him to the police and is arrested in her home after she set him up . the brute man is shot well in black and white and is certainly one of the better efforts from prc . i've never heard of any of the cast in this , with rondo hatton in the title role . this is worth checking out . a good way to spend an hour one afternoon or evening . rating : 3 stars out of 5 .
