this film is quite simply the best thing i have seen for a very long time . a seemingly straightforward morality tale of revenge and retribution , it builds to a climax that will hold you fixed in place and squirming as the conclusion unfolds . there are violent moments in the film , but life is sometimes violent , and what you see is never glamorized or fetishized - it is simply the way it is . i heartily recommend this film to anyone who is sick of big budget hollywood nonsense and longs for a simple story , well told and superbly acted . 10 / 10
