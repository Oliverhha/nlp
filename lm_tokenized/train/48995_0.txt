i was extremely disappointed in this film . the acting was unconvincing , and the message of the film " this could be everyone's century " is glib and sweet enough to give one cavities . the dilemma between gregory peck's and dorothy maguire's characters could have been intriguing but it is so bogged down by a too - sappy - for - words love story that it never gets the chance . i can see that back in the 1947 this film would have been daring , which is probably why it won the best picture oscar . but the historical value of the film did not save it for me . by the standards of what makes a classic , this film never manages to be more than mediocre . the only good things about this film are the scenes between philip and his secretary ( june havoc delivers her shockingly hypocritical lines perfectly ) and the fact that we get to see the handsome gregory peck a lot .
