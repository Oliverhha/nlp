it sounds like a soppy idea : that we meet the people we love time and again through the ages . but it works - for over three hours . ' la belle histoire ' switches between judea 2000 years back ( yep , the time and place of christ ) and the present day . we meet the same group of people then and now , with grand passion , bullfighting and gipsy kings thrown into the mix . being a french movie , there's a lot of pretty people here . and religious / christian metaphors . this could have become a messy stew - instead , it's a very entertaining epic for the romanticists among us . too bad it's so hard to get hold of .
