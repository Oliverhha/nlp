hotel for dogs is the story of two foster children who adopt stray dogs and find a place for them , as well as filling their need for family , in an abandoned hotel . this is not a terrible movie , but the viewer has seen it all before . there's not much originality here in its typical underdog makes good , love conquers all kind of story . it has some amusing parts , the acting is reasonable , the dogs are cute and the pacing is decent , but there's really nothing exceptional in it . it's worth a watch if you like this kind of sappy family comedy , but there's not a lot of substance and nothing that you'll remember in five years .
