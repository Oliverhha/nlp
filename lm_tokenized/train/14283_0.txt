well i like it more than most it would seem , it's central theme of hypnosis as a weapon is a very good one and it plays out as an interesting story right up to the finale . gene tierney doesn't have to do much here , she is asked to portray confusion and delivers well enough , whilst josé ferrer is more than adequate for the dastardly role of david korvo . i see a simple and effective noirish drama that although the ending is never really in doubt , the film has enough mystery about it to keep the viewer hanging in there for the final reel . the final scene may be a tad too contrived for some but it pays off well enough and as the credits rolled i was more than satisfied with what i had just watched . a very solid 7 / 10 .
