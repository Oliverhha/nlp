i must say i have just watched this film and admit to having tears in my eyes whilst watching , unusual for me . it managed to take a ' brewsters millions ' theme and turn it into something far more emotional , a drama rather that a comedy . although the film could be in danger of being over sentimental and corny i did not find it so , i believe it delivered the messages in a simple and understated way that made them believable . this is a heart warming story that i would rather watch any day than some of the big budget , special effects laden blockbusters that leave you devoid of any real emotion at the end of the film . i would thoroughly recommend to those who want just a little depth to stories in the cinema .
