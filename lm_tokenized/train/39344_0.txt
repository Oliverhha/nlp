this story could have been good but the movie falls terribly flat . the script was lacking and the acting was just not up to par . dennis quaid usually does a good job . he is the reason i watched it . but , i am guessing the script itself was so bad that the acting followed suit . there was no flow in the story . it went from one very basic scene to another . very little was slowly revealed but rather just bluntly stated . i watched it with two other people and the response to it was all the same . wow , that was bad ! the story itself could have made a great movie but the attempt to make this movie good , failed miserably . re watch 7 & silence of the lambs & you will be better off !
