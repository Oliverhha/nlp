if you are a history buff , like a murder mystery and into " what if " detective plots - you will love this movie . so what if a prostitute is killed in war torn warsaw during the final stages of ww ii ? a german military police officer cares , and even with wars chaos begins a search for the murderer that goes into post war germany . great acting and twists make this above average . the german period uniforms look real and the irony of a single death standing out among the millions of lives lost during the war is striking as two former enemy detectives begin tracking the killer together . their investigation ends with a bang . peter o'toole is the aristocratic german general the title is named after , he looks the part and plays it to perfection . with a great supporting cast this a must see wwii drama .
