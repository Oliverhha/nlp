although i would hesitate to call it " film noir , " strange illusion is a tightly woven , intriguing mystery . for a poverty row production , the writers and ulmer paced the film well and kept it interesting . the acting , although amateurish at times , doesn't distract from a believable story . my only real complaint about the film is the music - - too much of it and too loud . brett's penchant for teenage girls is a refreshingly realistic perversion for a film of the ' 40s . it also stands in stark contrast to the " gee whiz " scenes ( as mentioned in a previous comment ) , which seem like they were lifted straight out of " leave it to beaver . " i rate it 7 / 10 .
