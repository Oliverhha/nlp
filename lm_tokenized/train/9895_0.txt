greedy is a film you may have passed on showtime , or glanced at in the tv guide and thought , " never heard of it " . to be honest , i can't recall its stint in the theaters either . however , on the recommendation of my father , i gave it a chance . the interaction between all of the characters is terrific . phil hartman gives comic brilliance , mixed with wit and sarcasm in his usual way . this movie has a lot of laugh out loud moments for people who are into clever comedy with a slightly twisted mind . it is cemented in my comedy top 5 with stripes , planes trains and automobiles , ferris buellers day off , and ridgemont . highly recommended ! 9 / 10
