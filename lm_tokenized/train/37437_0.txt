this was an attempt to return to a genre that officially died in the late 90s - the slasher . introduce teenage characters that no one cares about in any facet , make them doing " bad " things and getting killed for doing it . i thought about clicking the spoiler button , because that pretty much sums up the movie . while this probably would have made a decent video game or short story , it's definitely didn't make the trip from screenplay to screen without introducing every slasher cliché imaginable . it's not worth your money for the dvd , nor was it really worth the rental fees . if you've watched any of the retro slashers , skip this one .
