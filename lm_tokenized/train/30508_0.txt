billy chung proves that he has the makings of a good director , even if he can't quite get rid of all the sleaze that tends to appear in his films . devil face's plot is a rehash of familiar hong kong films in the hitman genre , with nothing of note added . the leads are awkward , the story itself is rather blase , and the only saving grace is chung's stylish direction . unfortunately the movie doesn't leave enough room in its storyline for the audience to be involved , and the sleazy elements of the film really overwhelms what could have been a half decent story . even a minor appearance by personal favorite suet lam doesn't help matters . maybe he knows something we don't , which is why his character doesn't last long . 4 out of 10 .
