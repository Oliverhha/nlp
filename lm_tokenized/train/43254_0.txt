a swarm of ants from south america decide to over run alaska . cheesy and silly from start to end . poor animation on the ants , to begin with . sometimes you see some actual footage of about three or four real ants : scary . attacks are the most contrived sequences you'll ever witness . one dummy climbs into a giant ant hill . another guy fires a pistol at the ant hill . some other clod tries to attack them with a tractor . still another dimwit fires a rifle at the swarm as they try to surround her . nobody ever seems to think of running away ; the ants move at about 1 / 1000th the speed of a human . one guy is in a truck driving in circles while fire bombs go off on either side of the truck , no matter where he is at any given moment . but i love the " let's blow up the dam " sequence best . there's no reasonable explanation for why this would help , or any assurance that it would work . they just decide to blow up the dam . a helicopter picks up a guy after an 2 - second earthquake knocks him off his feet , and he rolls down a hill to land atop some dynamite he just lit . and on it goes . good for a laugh at how stupid it all is .
