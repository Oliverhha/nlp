this unpretentious movie offers some joys , i watched it because i wanted to see mrs . mirren and mrs . julie walters , as i think they are gorgeous . it is a piece of exploitation . its acknowledged premise is that this is an earthly naturalistic comedy , unashamed and unassuming . beyond this , the reality of the movie is quite different . is it morbid , perverted ? of course it is , though it strives not to be . it pretends to be a joyful naturalistic movie , but it bets on the very unhealthy joys it offers to its fans ( or is it too much to believe this comedy has fans ? ) . it is indecent and deliberately perverted . on the other hand , it is wholly indifferent as cinema , the directing is banal .
