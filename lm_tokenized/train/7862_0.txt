nothing spectacular in disney's latest attempt to turn one of their theme park rides into a movie . i pray that " it's a small world " is never filmed , but i digress . eddie murphy is good in the leading role , but his wife looked way too young for him . special effects were pretty good , and almost made up for the flimsy plot . but did anyone else notice that this movie was set in louisiana ( the ride at disneyland is in the part of the park called the french quarter , and the cars had louisiana license plates ) and not one character had a louisiana accent ? not even the guy who'd lived all his life in the mansion ? mindless entertainment , nothing more nor less . 4 / 10
