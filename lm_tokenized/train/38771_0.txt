it is amazing how such an action packed movie can be oh so boring . i have never before felt so restless during a movie , shifting in my chair , looking at my watch , wishing i could fast forward . it felt like three hours . i could not believe the movie was not over when the main character died , he came back to life again to bore us even more . the whole movie didn't make any sense . who was the bad guy ? why could the girl stop bullets ? what was with the father ? how is it that her mother was a computer ? just a huge mess of unfinished ideas , characters that no one cares about and badly shot action .
