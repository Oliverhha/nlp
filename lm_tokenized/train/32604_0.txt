honestly , i had a lot of expectations for this movie , because of my fondness for meryl streep and uma thurman , and i was pretty disappointed . the movie dragged and seemed to go nowhere ! i have never seen ben younger's other film , but if prime is any indication of his style , i know i should probably stay away from that one , too ! the character development seemed forced and insincere . i did not feel emotionally attached to any of these characters . throughout the movie , uma's character , rafi , keeps telling her therapist how she needs " to put all of herself out there . " i kept waiting for some secret or hidden part of her character to be revealed , but , alas , nothing like that ever happened . i could never really tell where the characters were going , what they were going to do and how they felt . for other movies , that might sound exciting and unpredictable , but for prime , it just felt like nothing was ever happening . if i had to do it all over again , i would pass on prime .
