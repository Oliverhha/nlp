if you're looking for a scary movie with a simple plot this is the one to get . it isn't nearly as scary as it probably was in the 70s but you'll jump a few times . the plot is as simple as it gets . a group of friends and a younger brother who tags along all go on a trip . they run out of gas and find their way to abandoned house . little do they know they'll be killed off one by one . the characters are pretty one dimensional . i did like how you learn more about the younger brother and his brother's relationship . that helped me actually care about the characters . in conclusion , this is a great teen flick . the plot is easy to follow , the characters aren't complicated , and it's as scary as heck !
