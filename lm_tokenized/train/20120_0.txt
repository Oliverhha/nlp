every so often a film comes along that is misunderstood by critics and ignored by the public , but is subsequently rediscovered and reappraised . i sincerely hope that " for the boys " will join those ranks . it is an uncommonly sincere , insightful and touching film , and the only things to be held against it amount to quibbles . true , the old age make up is dreadful , and the last five minutes seriously weaken the impact of the film , but the sum total is moving and perceptive . bette midler , giving the performance of her career to date , was robbed of the oscar in my opinion . it is a brave and sincere effort on her part , and such a pity that it was not met with greater recognition . james caan plays the shallow , slightly dim eddie sparks almost too well . there are times when he truly frightened me . the performances on the whole are restrained ; when the occasion for histrionics comes , both stars rise to it . thoroughly recommended . i sincerely hope this film finds its audience one day .
