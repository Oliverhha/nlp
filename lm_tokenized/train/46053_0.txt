masterpiece as it attacks an oil refinery , flies over a crowded city spewing acidic mist , and later attempting to drown godzilla in a pool of mud . the city scenes might be it's most gruesome aspect , quite evidently killing people in a gruesome manner and is quite shocking to see , especially as the other aspects of the movie are so child -
