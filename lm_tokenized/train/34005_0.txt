oh dear . this really is a sad indictment of the franchise film . i suppose it was inevitable , even unsuccessful films get sequels these days , particularly those of the horror genre . the only real good thing to come out of this was to spur william peter blatty into helming what would become the third exorcist film . despite the films best intentions , it makes little sense in relation to the original classic . the exorcist ii contains a few interesting scenes and shots but ultimately relies on the none too impressive climax to engage the viewer . the pace and direction is often slow and disjointed and i must confess to laughing out loud at a couple of unintentionally humorous passages of dialogue . i might say for exorcist devotees only , but then anybody who truly appreciates the original will want to steer clear of this sequel . for morbid curiosity only .
