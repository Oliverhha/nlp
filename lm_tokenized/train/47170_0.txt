warning : spoilers ahead in 5x2 , ozon's daring narrative approach consists of 5 isolated moments of a failed romance , played backward . the structure is largely artificial it seems , leaving too many blanks for viewers to fill in , and i didn't manage to enter the whole story . in addition to experimental choices , i found that marion and gilles were , except for a few scenes , uninteresting or even despicable characters ( especially gilles , the unconsiderate , sex - driven husband ) . on the other hand , ozon's accurate direction , combined with soft lighting and scarcity of sound , create a unique atmosphere , like in his previous movies .
