my favourite part of this sequel is when kiara & kovu are playing together . imagine my dismay when their cruel parents separated them . if that wasn't bad enough , suddenly they're grown up and have lost all their charm & personality . the rest of the film seems like a rehash of lion king & pocahontas . apart from " he lives in you " , the soundtrack is terrible . i missed the hyenas . kovu's siblings weren't half as funny . zazu wasn't funny at all . i hated simba . whenever disney characters reach adulthood these days they become either obnoxious or just plain boring . ( in older films like bambi & the fox & the hound they're not only young for longer , they also grow up to be likeable . ) disney are losing their magic touch . 4 / 10
