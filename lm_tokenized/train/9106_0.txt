many people already wrote the plots of " lust , caution " . i don't repeat again , i just have a few words here . . . i have read many reviews from the professional film critics . some are extremely insulting , and those people didn't understand the film at all . i felt sorry for ang lee and all people who worked hard for the film . " lust , caution " is a pure chinese drama with the bloody china - japan war and the chinese cultural context of 1940s : slow pace , old shanghai , mahjong , the traditional song , hatred , love , loneliness , and the souls of students and traitors . do not forget the cultural differences : western people play the card games ; chinese people play the mahjong games . i don't know either , i always see the body languages of the players . i personally dislike the raw sexual scenes , however , the film is still a masterpiece !
