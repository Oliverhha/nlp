this film will go down as a truly important and remembered film in scifi . it is along the order of close encounters of the third kind and e . t . the extra terrestrial . people who only like action / adventure flicks won't have enough brain cells to get it , but true scifi fans ( the types who can read books too ) will love this film . it's very powerful and emotional . it had some minor problems as any film does . there were some weak acting moments and a poor edit that should have been cut tighter , but in general it was excellent . in particular , 6yr old newcomer rhiannon leigh wryn as the lead character emma wilder did a wonderful job . unbelievable that a 6 year old child could be that effective in a role . i highly recommend this well written and directed film . it's one to remember .
