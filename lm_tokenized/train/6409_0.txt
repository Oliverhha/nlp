" a clockwork orange " is too strong for some people to accept and is still too powerful today . . . it is an extremely disturbing meditation on the violent nature of man . . . kubrick emphasizes the human element by paradoxically dehumanizing the characters . . . viewers see images they may not have wanted to see , but will never be able to forget . . . he presents violence in a confusing and increasing fashion that makes it alarmingly attractive . . . he invites us to join alex in raping and pillaging into london urban jungle to show that violence and power are invariably attractive . . . he makes good use of the camera , showing , in strange ways , the furious underworld of alex . . . kubrick is the driving force behind " a clockwork orange .
