this is a very funny series but please don't confuse it with a history of wwi . david lloyd george ( the british prime minister ) and douglas haig ( the general commanding the british army ) hated each other and after the war dlg got his memoirs into print very quickly - this led to the myth that the british army was led by bungling officers and sacrificed for nothing . this show plays on that myth - in an extremely funny way . alas - it also helps rob the british army of its finest victory . despite that i love this show - they visit so many of the controversial sides of this conflict in what is at times a side achingly funny way . the end is especially poignant .
