usa network pictures captured the true story of vlad the impaler . this movie was as suggested by my summary a haunting tale of justice and love . the story of dracul , was masterfully brought to life , with rudolf martin bringing just the right edge to the vlad ( dracula ) . jane march was radiant and haunting as lidia ( vlad's wife ) . the cinematography was stunning , the battle scenes short and frenzied . seeing the way in which this film was done , it is no wonder that the dark prince is still a national hero in romania , whose return is anxiously awaited . i can only hope that usa pictures has the common sense to release this film to video and dvd as no dracula collection can be complete without it .
