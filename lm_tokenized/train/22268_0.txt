i'm a big robert mitchum fan but even i couldn't sit through this one . everyone acts as if they're asleep . they show the entire range of emotion from grim to grim . after the first half i gave up and fast - forwarded to the unsatisfying conclusion . this is the slowest moving plot you'll ever see in your life and as near as i could tell none of any of the character's motivations was revealed until the final scene . since all these stars have done great work elsewhere i guess the blame lies with the director and the script . just give this movie a pass and go watch any other mitchum film .
