the forbin project has just come out on dvd , anybody who has not already seen it has 24 hours to do so before they must hang their head in shame . the movie is 35 years old now but it is still the most intelligent and accurate portrayal of artificial intelligence ever put on film . in my opinion this is one of the best movies ever made , without a doubt it is the most underrated movie ever made . be warned however this film will scare you ; there is no blood or gore and the special effects are primitive by modern standards , but if the forbin project does not scare your brain then you have not understood it . this is one of the very rare occasions when the movie is better , much much better , than the book . when i read the book years ago i remember thinking the premise was great and with a few changes it could be really great , but as it is the book is mediocre at best . with genius you wouldn't expect b grade moviemakers to have they kept all the brilliant parts and eliminated all the stupid parts . this movie has stood the test of time extraordinarily well .
