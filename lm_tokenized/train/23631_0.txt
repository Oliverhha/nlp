i love this film and i can't understand why it hasn't been more popular . try and get hold of a copy if you can . the cast is amazing - joe mantegna , john malkovic , linda fiorentino , jamie lee curtis ( in her best ever role ) , and kevin bacon all giving restrained but emotional performances . the soundtrack is wonderful too . an eclectic mix of songs that work perfectly . it took me a long time to track down the song from the closing credits but it was worth it . it's one of those films that you can watch if you're feeling a bit down and it will lift your mood . especially recommended if you've had a pointless argument with a loved one !
