this is vintage mike myers ( has there ever been a bad mike myers movie ? ) . mike myer's characters , charlie mckenzie and charlie's eccentric scottish father , carry this movie which would otherwise be pretty ordinary . the best moments in the movie occur in the mckenzie household where charlie's father steals the show . his brutal bagging of william ( head ) is among the funniest movie moments i have seen . the songs ( women women , " if you want my body " ) provide material for many drunken sing - a - longs . i thought stevie wright was also very funny , but the ending was pretty lame .
