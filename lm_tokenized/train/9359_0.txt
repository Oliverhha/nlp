practically the best remake of all time . vincent price's movie was good but this one adds another level to it . jeff goldblum is wonderful as seth brundle / brundlefly he really shows the true madness of the character while allowing the emotional content to shine through the gaps . this film is beautiful and even though the basic genre is horror this film plays out one of the greatest love stories played out in film history . in short ' the fly ' is well worth taking the time to sit down and watch be it now or in ten years this film will always be a classic .
