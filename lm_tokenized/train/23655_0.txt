tod browning is much better known for his " dracula " ( 1931 ) and , to a lesser extent , the cult classic " freaks " ( 1932 ) than for this oddity from the limelight of the silent era . from the opening scenes , in which an armless man ( chaney , natch ) undresses a young joan crawford during a circus act by pitching daggers with his feet , this is a characteristically dark , warped picture from browning that draws a great deal of power from chaney doing what he does best : playing a tormented freak secretly endeavoring to gain the heart of a beautiful woman . " the unknown " isn't perfect , but it most certainly is memorable , distinguishing itself as one of the weirdest films of all time .
