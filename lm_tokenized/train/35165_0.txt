there aren't too many slasher movies i enjoy . striking a balance between too childish and too wrapped up in your own irony is a tough gig and i think freak out just sails wide of the bullseye . that said , it's a very respectable effort for something created with such a small budget over such a large time frame and hints that there is some real writing and editing talent waiting for a better - funded opportunity to present itself . i would have liked to have seen more tits and the odd clunge shot thrown in , if only in the bonus features as this would have given the film a higher mark when it comes to production values .
