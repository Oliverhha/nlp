flåklypa grand prix is a wonderful piece of art . this movie is , to put it in simple words , perfect . i saw the first time as a kid , and i can still watch it over and over again without any loss in pleasure . it is a children's movie , in a way , but i'm over 30 and i still love it . the scenery is absolutely beautiful , with the mountains of flåklypa and everything . the puppets are amazingly alive in spite of the relatively small number of moving parts . the technological marvels of reodor felgen , including but by far not limited to the monstrous car il tempo gigante , evidence a very skilled craftmanship and an eye for details . the car competition towards the end really feels in your own stomach , too . the story line is quite simple and not really original , but that's the way it should be in a children's movie ( even for kids above 30 ) . flåklypa grand prix is my absolute favorite of all movies ever made in the whole world !
