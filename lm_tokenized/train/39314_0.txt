i had great hopes for this movie . . . none of which were fulfilled . my family and i enjoyed the first two santa clause movies , but this was a major let down . sad , too , because it had great potential . i enjoy martin short , and think he was well cast in the role of jack frost . however , the writing and editing were so disjointed , and it seemed like there were too many subplots going on for any one story line or character to get any substantial development . unless you have kids who are dying to see this , i would skip it altogether or wait for it to come out on dvd . i definitely wouldn't spend another $ 20 to go see it .
