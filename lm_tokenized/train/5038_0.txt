with the sexual revolution of the ' 60s , there of course would have to be a movie about sex . " bob & carol & ted & alice " was it . having visited a therapy group , bob and carol sanders ( robert culp and natalie wood ) have become more open about their sexuality , and are identifying more with the counterculture in general . their repressed friends , ted and alice henderson ( elliott gould and dyan cannon ) can't seem to get into it . but while in a hotel room in las vegas , after they've all revealed some secrets , they decide to have an orgy . you've probably seen the picture of the four of them in bed together . when this movie came out , many critics thought that it had a cop - out ending . in an interview , director paul mazursky explained that he felt that they only could go as far as they eventually went . anyway , you can't judge an entire movie by one individual scene ; it's what the movie's saying overall that matters . as for the characters , bob seems sort of wooden , carol is hot ( as natalie wood always was ) , ted is a dork , and alice is a little eccentric . overall , it's a pretty good movie .
