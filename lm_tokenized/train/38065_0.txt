a friend loaned this dvd to me , and i prepared to watch it not with any expectations of seeing a great or even good cinematic product , but merely hoping for a mild amount of entertainment . i was completely disappointed . this film possesses no positive attributes that i can think of . everything from the costumes to the sets to the script was banal , brainless , and obviously churned out of the hollywood schlock machine simply to make a buck on the names of the film's hapless stars . three - quarters of the way through the movie i took to skipping ' chapters ' in a vain attempt to reach a scene worth watching . ultimately , i abandoned the effort . this is the kind of movie that makes one wish desperately for commercial breaks , so that one can have some kind of relief .
