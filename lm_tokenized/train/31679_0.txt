i never read the books that this movie is based on , but it did remind me a bit of ms . piggle wiggle , and mary poppins . i'm 25 , and my fiancé is 31 . yes , i did drag him to see it , but he admittedly enjoyed it . just a very sweet , feel good kind of movie . i loved the way nanny mcphee got prettier and even younger looking as the children got more more behaved . it's like she started out as ugly on the outside as the children's behavior was . i laughed a lot and the end gave me goose bumps . i don't want to have 7 children , but wouldn't it be nice for everybody if there really was a nanny mcphee for those in need . i highly recommend this movie . especially for those with kids .
