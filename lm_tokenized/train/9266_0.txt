the story tells us about a man who owns nothing . beside his few friends , he is homeless and has got no work , often nothing to eat but that does not worry him . he is only worried that " everything is forbidden nowadays " . he is a professional car burglar who only steals to have a ride . instead of selling the car he always bring it back after cruising the city in the night . if he ever has got something in his hands he does not know other than to share . he shares his bread , his room , his car that he steals but he cannot share his love . when the film finishes you see the explanation of the film crew : " this film is real because we made it up " . he shows us the man with nothing but a big heart , perhaps too big for this life . .
