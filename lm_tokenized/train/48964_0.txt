i'm a huge zappa fan , but this is easily one of the worst movies i've ever seen , right next to star wars the phantom menace . frank and company should have taken a cue from the beatles ' magical mystery tour and realized that you just can't shoot random weirdness and expect to have a watchable movie . the music's just ok , too . this version of the mothers with flo and eddie was never one of my favorites . still , the film does kind of suck you in and dares you to watch it . some of the really old and cheeseball special effects , reverse film tricks , etc . come off as disturbing , but all in all , this is quite a snooze .
