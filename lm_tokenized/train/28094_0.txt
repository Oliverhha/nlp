this film is entertaining - certainly the beautiful and haunting song of isseu niang is worth the price of admission . everyone will enjoy this film as they watch it , and then will think seriously about its consequences . but the film is also about coming of age as the world of tradition gives way grudgingly to the world of the modern . the imagery of the teenagers ' stealing ' electric power from the mosque for their party , the traditional silhouette puppet theater of the wolof nation in which african and european themes are confused , and the ' kings ' ( fans of american pop music ) and the ' ins ' ( fans of french pop music ) vie against each other all form a theme that goes beyond the frontiers of senegal and beyond the coasts of africa . " twist à popenguine " offers an amusing view of cultural conflict and generational conflict , but also the gradual and sad loss of a heritage .
