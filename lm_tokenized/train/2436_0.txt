i saw this movie when it first debuted and i have never forgotten the vivid performances and how i cried . i was watching at someone else's house and couldn't hold back the tears . we kept saying " emmys all around " at each commercial break ! mare winningham is a phenomenal actress . the writing was superb . the scene with her mother , beautifully played by phyllis frelich , when her mother calls her selfish is one of the best scenes , bar none , that i have ever seen in my life . i cry just thinking about it . if you have an opportunity to see this film , don't miss it . you won't be sorry .
