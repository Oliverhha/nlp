i recently bought this movie without knowing or hearing anything about it . yes , i sometimes do that . and i can gladly say the choice i made was a good one exchanging my 13 dollars for a round plastic disc . through the movie it was pretty obvious to me that they wanted to save the big reveal for the end . i don't know if this is what they really intended to but i saw it coming all along . though one might find it pretty easy to pick out where this movie is going , there are still surprises for those who think they have it figured out . this movie also made me feel squeamish at times . i don't really get like that when watching movies . but this movie sure as well did just that . overall a good watch for someone who is into independent movies and want to see some real , raw , gritty imagery .
