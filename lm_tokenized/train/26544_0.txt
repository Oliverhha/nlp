i have seen this film a number of times , most memorably at the nederlands film museum , vondelpark , amsterdam , to live accompaniment . when seen with such accompaniment , the film is a delight to watch , with the film and music and live sound effects making a real evenings entertainment . a classic silent movie with all fun of the fairground . a fairy tale which is enriched by the style and presentation of the times . fairbanks plays an excellent thief full of mischief and daring .
