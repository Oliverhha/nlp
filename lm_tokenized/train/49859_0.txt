i tried to watch this movie on vhs years ago , but found it too boring to get through . i decided to give it another chance in a real theater , because i'd heard that it's such a classic . it definitely improved on the big screen ( well , not that big ) , but the good things about the movie are undercut by the unsatisfactory story . the film starts out like a biting social comedy , on the order of " la ronde " , but strangely changes tone partway through , turning much darker . but because of the light opening , the characters are not three - dimensional enough to support a serious story . the actors , especially darrieux , are good enough that they almost make it work , but they needed a better script . the abrupt , unexplained ending was especially frustrating to me . the film is beautifully shot , but the camera - work was a little too hyper for me in places - - a dance sequence made me literally dizzy . ( probably most people wouldn't have a problem with that . ) also , the rich , somewhat claustrophobic production design showed a few too many 1950's influences for my taste . on the positive side , i admired the way ophuls created ( on the surface ) a believable world of the rich , and yet had an awareness of the poorer people that surrounded it . that was something i did not expect .
