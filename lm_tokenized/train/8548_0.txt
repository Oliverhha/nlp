i say seven but really i mean seven and a half . this movie is great . it is a dark comedy that follows the fall of five stupid people and how they manage to destroy themselves morally , emotionally , and physically . nothing happens that the government cares about . no breaches in national security or plans to attack anything , but the belief that something big is going on destroys the people involved . the best part is that these idiots manage to surprise and confuse the cia . that a simple nonevent between five people in washington could completely catch the cia off guard . overall i would say that it is a funny movie that is not for those who don't like to lose characters unexpectedly and without consequence .
