i caught this documentary back in a repertory theatre back in 1992 . i haven't seen it since but i never forgot it . it's basically talks with lesbian writers who talk about writing and having published books about lesbian lovers . also they talk about what it was like in lesbian bars back then . some people may think that this will only appeal to a limited audience ( i . e . gay and lesbian ) but i saw it with a straight friend and he was fascinated . the talks are lively and fun - - there's no despair or negativity . one writer speaks openly about fighting with publishers to have her books have happy endings ! interspered is a sequence in which two women meet in a bar ( no dialogue ) , go to one of their houses and has sex . i have no problem with that , but the sequence seems way too explicit for this . still this is an excellent documentary about a subject that is never covered . how about one with gay male writers ?
