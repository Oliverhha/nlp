while this film fails to capture the aura of a big - budgit film , it succeeds in finding that of a b - movie . yes the film's plot is kinda cheesy and the acting's inadvertently funny , but it has the strange attraction one might have for an ed wood film or any other kind of corny " horror " film . the story was also a but clichéd and there was one rather misplaced hot scene . it's like a tabloid story , you don't wanna look , but something pulls you to do so anyway . all in all " dead above ground " isn't perfect , but i kinda enjoyed it anyway .
