all of director abel ferrara's movies are deeply flawed , but he is willing to take you into darker places than almost any contemporary filmmaker . his best movies are the bleakest kind of cinematic art , and the funeral is one of the best . to complain about the lack of action in this gangster movie is to miss the entire point . this is a character study of people who have made choices in their lives that have left them without hope , in much the same way as ferrara's " bad lieutenant " . although it is often pretentious and a little boring , it also contains many potent , unforgettable scenes , most notably those featuring chris penn , who i frankly didn't think could act until i saw this movie . " the funeral " is an unremittingly dark film that at times achieves a terrible beauty . i'm not sure that i would recommend it , but i am extremely glad to have seen it .
