this is one of the thoroughly enjoyable monster movies that appeared amid all the camp and trash the genre churned out in the 1950s . while it is no masterpiece , there is nothing worthy of serious criticism . the movie is well - paced with plenty of action - - the viewer is not required to wait 45 minutes before anything at all happens . it is very well filmed , in a widescreen format ( the only way it should be seen ) . and the acting by an exceptionally attractive cast is above average for this kind of film . as for the dinosaurs , they may not be up to the standards of " jurassic park " or " walking with dinosaurs " , but , again , considering what the budget must have been , they are rather good ( except for a slow - moving tyrannosaurus ) . what really makes this movie worthwhile is its atmosphere . it draws the viewer into the reasonably convincing world of a tropical jungle in the middle of a polar icecap . a very likeable and exciting movie . see it in widescreen if you can .
