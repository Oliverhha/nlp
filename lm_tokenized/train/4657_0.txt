every now and then a remarkable small film is made . the recent " venus " starring peter o'toole is one . irina palm is also one . this is a story of a middle - aged woman ( maggie , played by marianne faithful ) worn down by sadness . she must get hold of a substantial amount of money . forced by necessity and desperation maggie does something that is at first utterly alien to her , but may yet become an awakening and salvation , a route to an identity she does not know she has and to new love . marianne faithful's central performance is hugely affecting in its undecorated stillness and simplicity . this film is a cut above so many bigger releases . if you like a small story well - told it is for you .
