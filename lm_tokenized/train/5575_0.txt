i wasn't expecting to like it , but this is a great show . it's funny and the plot is actually thought through and ( sort of ) consistent . it's not just serving the jokes . especially the second season has a serious touch . that aside , i gotta say it's all thanks to ' the devil ' that i kept watching . ray wise plays him brilliantly : he is sadistic , intimidating , funny and still has room left for rare moments of affection ( ? ) . much of the cast looks boring compared to him : i never really liked any of the ' good ' characters , but that makes it funnier when they're the victim of whatever the episode is about . altogether , ' reaper ' is definitely worth a watch . it's too bad it got canceled .
