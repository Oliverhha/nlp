" play it again , sam " was directed by herbert ross ( mostly known for chick flicks , such as " steel magnolias " , " the turning point " and " boys on the side " ) , but it's a typical woody allen film . woody wrote this brilliant comedy , based on his own stage play , and starred as allan felix , a ( neurotic , of course ) film critic that , after being dumped by his wife , is constantly visited by the ghost of his idol , humphrey bogart ( jerry lacy ) . bogart's ghost ( characterized as rick blaine , from " casablanca " ) gives allan advice about how to win over beautiful women , but our hero seems too clumsy to find the right lady . the only woman who seems to be ideal for allan is linda christie ( diane keaton ) , a close friend and , also , the wife of allan's best friend , dick ( tony roberts ) . i think " play it again , sam " is easily one of the best comedies ever made , almost in the same level as the classic " annie hall " . it's so good that i'm looking forward to seeing it again . if you don't laugh watching this , you're very ill . 10 out of 10 .
