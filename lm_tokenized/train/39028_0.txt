this movie hardly qualifies as a werewolf movie . you could take the werewolf out of the picture and basically be left with " clue " as different character types ( the actress , the doctor , and even the professor ! ) try to figure out who is killing the other guests within the castle . . . or in this case , who the werewolf is . i think one element pivotal to werewolf films is " the transformation " scene , and this movie robs its audience of this payoff . you don't really even need a budget to pull off some type of effect , but all this movie provides is a costume seen in close - up or in shadow , further removing the presence of the werewolf from this film . even when the werewolf's identity is revealed , we don't get so much as a nifty contact lens effect or wry - fanged smile . additionally distancing itself from the werewolf genre , the film isn't even scary . it lacks any real thrill or even gore . it's hard to stay interested as the film goes along because the characters we begin to sympathize with are each killed off in turn . . . which is fine as an element of surprise the first time it happens , but when it continually happens throughout the movie , the end result is that it distances the viewer and you're not left with much to care about . it really is , then , like a game of clue , with about the same amount of depth , where characters are gradually eliminated one by one , merely for the sake of finding out who the killer really is , and not for suspense . i will say that it's nicely shot , the acting is above par for a low - budget video release , and for what it is - - a somewhat awkward mystery - - i guess it's ok . but a werewolf movie , it's not .
