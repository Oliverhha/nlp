this film could have drove it's " point " home a lot better than presented . it should have stayed clear away from the feel of the 70's type " blaxpoitation " films . but this film was not so bad at all . it tells a story , a story of a big time 70's guy who's soul has not been put to rest . he has a vendetta , dead or not , he needed to see it to its full completion . ( ya know , similar to george w . bush and his iraq thing . ) ernest dickerson is a darn excellent cinematographer and director of photography . in the 80's i enjoyed his work on " tales from the darkside " and i can see he was headed for it here . trouble is , in feature film , audiences expect more . progression is the key , and ernest went for a 70's blaxpoitation thing here instead of just telling the tale as it should be . snoop dog actually did a good job for the material here . if nothing else , this was his audition tape for the feature film : " starsky and hutch " . the stereotypical parts for the other actors is what turned me off . ( turned me off in the 70's as well . ) which is why this film could have done without it . but it is an entertaining film . good for your adult halloween party if everyone there is over 35 to recall the movie days of " foxy brown " , " blacula " and " black cesear " .
