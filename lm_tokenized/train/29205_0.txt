one of buñuel's greatest films . scene after scene arguments are used as beautiful excuses to subvert reality and attack established and hypocritical institutions with acute humor and surrealist means . if you have a taste for surrealism and absurd humor ( i . e . monty python , marx bros . , etc . ) this movie cannot be recommended enough . one small correction : the sniper is not sentenced to death but to capital punishment which results in something altogether different from death ( and far more sarcastic ) .
