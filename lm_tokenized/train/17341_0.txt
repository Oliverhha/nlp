the movie is very good . . . if you're into this kind of thing . it's nice to see a franchise splatter film that is intense and gory without being cheesy . many of the current crop of splatter films are played intentionally cheesy for humor . which is fine , but they shouldn't all be like that . this movie has some humor in it , but it's a darker humor , and not meant to be cheesy or campy . everything that comes to mind when you hear the words " texas chainsaw massacre " is in this movie . no wussing - out in this one ! murder , torture , cannibalism , insanity and a touch of the surreal . oh yeah , and a chainsaw . no punches are pulled , so this flick is not for the squeamish . tonally it wasn't as surreal as the original , but it had a better ( read : creepier ) tone than the 2003 remake . plenty of blood and guts . r . lee ermey plays a major character instead of a bit - player like in the 2003 remake , and the film benefits from this .
