i really enjoyed bee movie . the role of barry benson was just made for seinfeld , can't think of anyone better to be cast as the voice . plenty of humor for the kids and adults alike . i thought the plot was going to be a bit thin in the middle , but the ending ties it up just nicely . would have liked to hear more from chris rock though ! anyway , a very entertaining , light hearted film ( even with a hint of a message about ecosystem preservation for the kiddies ) . one of the better animated films after a long run of disappointments ( except for ratatouille ) . i'll be getting this one on dvd without a doubt . 8 / 10
