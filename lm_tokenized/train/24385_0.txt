colbert is well out of her element here , and cannot carry this tribute to ww ii fighting nurses . the sentiment is commendable . the film is disappointing . it goes on and on , practically forever . it pulls no punches about the toughness of the action , which is laudable . but , it also gives us nothing to hold on to . george reeves and sonny tufts are unbearably stiff and feckless in the two leading male roles . walter abel is good as the tough commander . i was disappointed when i first saw this , and recently wanted to see if it had improved with time . it hasn't .
