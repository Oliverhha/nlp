what a waste of time . i got in for free , went in with very minimal expectations ( i expected a mobbed - up version of space cowboys ) , and still left the theater aghast at the pointlessness of this movie . what i got was a comedy with no laughs ( okay , one very small one ) , a plot seemingly written on a napkin ( by a drunk whose seen goodfellas too many times ) , and probably the worst performances in the careers of every person in this movie . i expected a lot more from the writer of my favorite comedy movie ( kingpin ) and sitcom ( rich jeni's platypus man ) , but i guess barry fanaro ran out of ideas a long time ago . aside from the whole nine yards , not coincidentally another supposed " mob comedy " , this is the worst movie i've seen in the last 10 years . please , i beg of you , don't see this movie , you will thank me .
