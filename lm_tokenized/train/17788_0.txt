what would you get if you mixed two parts " er " with two parts " dynasty " ? you might think that you would get something steamy yet emotionally intriguing . instead , you might end up getting an awful medical melodrama called " doctors ' wives " . i have never understood why any movie would have its most interesting character killed off in the first fifteen minutes . the one and only excusable circumstance would be if you show that character in a lot of flashbacks . that doesn't happen in this film and it suffers severely . " doctors ' wives " has the look and feel of a tv pilot . there really isn't much location shooting to speak of . most of the film takes place in a hospital or at the characters ' homes . the screenplay is much more interested in introducing a lot of characters to you rather than fleshing any of them out . as a movie , it is dull and laughable . as a tv pilot , it showed that it might have eventually become rather interesting . or then again . . . maybe not . 1 / 10
