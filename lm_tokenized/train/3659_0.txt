' . in this fashion , ' matando cabos ' results in an entertaining and hilarious urban adventure . for all of those who are just sick of all those latin american movies that just try to " portray " the reality of society with stories around prostitution , corruption , crime and injustice , you ought to see this movie . it's a good way to see that in latin america they also " just want to have fun " .
