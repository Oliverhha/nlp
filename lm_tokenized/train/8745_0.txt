i just saw " the movie hero " today at the cleveland international film festival . i was pleased . " the movie hero " is hilarious , for one , and also really makes you think about your laughter . the main character keeps talking to his " audience " ( us , in a way ) , but everyone thinks he's crazy for that . he then goes and gets a side - kick , love interest , all the stuff for his movie . the premise is unique and the way it plays with its ideas are just fun . the acting is great and makes it a wonderful , light - hearted film about finding your inner " audience " . we got to talk to the director of this movie after it was shown and he pointed out that he wrote in a week . as a writer , i was amazed . stupid movies , yeah , maybe , but something like this should take way longer . kudos to him on that . " the movie hero " is so smart about movies , too , that i even thought about the wonderful film " adaptation " during the movie . that should tell you enough .
