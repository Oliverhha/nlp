i came upon this movie purely by accident when it was being shown on cable , and found myself riveted to my seat , not wanting to miss a minute of it . the performance by joseph cross was captivating , but what was even more compelling was the story itself . i was constantly trying to figure out how it was going to end . the ending turned out to be a real surprise , but i was also impressed by the portrayal of boyhood that the audience receives in the process of getting there . it was so true to life that i found myself wondering how much of it was based on the film writer's own experiences . above all , i was impressed by the amount of respect the movie seems to have for boys , and by its willingness to recognize that 5th graders sometimes think deep thoughts and ask deep questions .
