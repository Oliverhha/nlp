chevy chase moves to the country to become a farmer . chase plays ' andy farmer ' , a newspaper columnist who starts a new life in the sticks with his wife ( madolyn smith ) . he meets with all kinds of zany experiences . . . . too bad none of them are remotely funny . chevy is great at dry humor and modestly scores with a one - liner now and then , but the film just doesn't make it as one of those good comedies that you'd like to watch every so often with a big bowl of popcorn . ok scenes include madolyn's visit to an antique shop and chevy tossing his hot coffee on a bird outside his window . . . . * snicker * . . . . that was funny !
