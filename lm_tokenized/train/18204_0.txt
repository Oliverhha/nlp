this is the type of movie that makes you think . remember how you felt at the end ( last 10 minutes ) of the movie 6th sense ? unearthed is a movie that will have you thinking the same way . full of twists and turns , this movie gets better each time you see it . excellent performances by john bernath ( voss ) and slake counts ( pinder ) make this a great movie for either horror or sci - fi fans . it does not fit neatly into a genre category and as such defies traditional movie plots that seem all too predictable . well done and well worth the watch . low budget doesn't have to mean bad movie any more . this is a film you will enjoy seeing more than once . excellent cinematography and a driving music score . blood , guns , fast cars , alien warlords , dark cityscapes and women hanging on hooks . . . . . what more could you ask for . review based on screener copy of unearthed 2005
