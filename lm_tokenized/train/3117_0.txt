this movie used to scare me immensely when i was younger . it was the first " scary movie " i saw as a kid , and i think that may effect why i love it so much today . nostalgic purposes , indeed . i think this movie is good for it's purpose . it's not meant to be some life - changing , or hysterically funny , or terrifying suspense movie . it's meant to give you some scares , some laughs , and entertainment . and it does indeed entertain . we've heard since the 50's about little green men , and in this movie , they are there . and they don't even have to come from outer space , just chinatown . the actual mogwai ( what the gremlins are before they transform ) are adorable . so at first you are surprised at how this cute little furry creature who sings a little song could produce other mogwais whom are not so nice . i won't give away how they reproduce or turn into gremlins , but it's all kind of strange , and very fantasy - like . a great movies for adults to watch with kids for their first " scary movie " . i watched it when i was three , and while it did scare me there for a while , i still loved it a lot . i would recommend maybe six years or older , and if they get too scared , tell them it's really just puppets . ( it is . ) overall , this film is entertaining , very 80's , a little scary , and pretty funny .
