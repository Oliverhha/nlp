this show was a wonderful show . first of all , andy griffith . maybe his shows are too quaint for some . . . but i loved them . andy's soft , wise and yet accessible personae is alive and well in this show . truly he made the show . but the premise was great too . a home made rocket ship to salvage space junk . what a great idea . the show worked too . i watched this show with my family ( i was in jr hs a the time ) and it proved to be a show that we all enjoyed equally . we were , as a family , very disappointed when this show was canceled . i imagine that it had more to do with budget or keeping main cast members in place than popularity . while the show did not pull down top rating honors . . . it was the show that my friends and i would always get together and talk about in school .
