as a long time aficionado of psychological horror and spooky movies , i was immensely impressed by this little aussie flick . most " scary " movies these days simply aren't , and many aussie films struggle to have the production qualities of our overseas competitors . well , this film absolutely blew my expectations away in both regards . it is a beautifully crafted film - from the subtle yet convincing acting , to the magnificent cinematography , to the haunting sound scape , this is one of the slickest budget films i've been privileged to view . as for the scares . . . wow . just . . . wow . i'm not going to talk about what happens , but the plot always keeps you guessing , and is paced superbly . it's honestly one of the best scary flicks i've seen in years , and now ranks alongside session 9 as one of my all time faves . highly recommended .
