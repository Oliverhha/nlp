a poorly written , directed , and acted fluff with a couple of laughs . kirstie alley supplies the film's only electricity . it follows a rich new york couple evading the irs , as they hide out in the home of an amish family ( a la witness ) . the humor sprouts from your basic clash of cultures fare . the forced sentimentality is dredged up as the new yorkers realize they may have something to learn from the amish . actually , a fairer treatment of the amish than most films . however , why one of them has a french accent is beyond me .
