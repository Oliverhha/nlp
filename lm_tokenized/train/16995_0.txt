this film is hardly a masterpiece , though it does have some good moments and plenty of action . really it should have been named " broken helicopter " - - by my rough count they wreck at least 3 , maybe 4 , helicopters during the course of this film . travolta is over - the - top , of course , and slater is fine in his role . howie long does a good job as the henchman . i wouldn't spend money to see this , but if it's on cable and you're in the mood for a lot of wrecked helicopters , check it out .
