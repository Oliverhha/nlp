millard mudd is a down on his luck cartoon writer whose fortunes change with the arrival of a cute little dog named lucky . murder and mayhem ensue . lucky is not a good movie - it's too wordy , the " hero " is completely unlikeable , and the pacing is off . but it's filled with more disturbing stuff than most movies : necrophilia , sex torture , and chainsaws among them . plus it really seems to hate women . sidebar : i'm a chick horror fan and get really upset when people point to horror flicks as nothing but misogyny . but this movie really doesn't like women . it kept me watching right up to the dumb ending because i had to see where it was going . has some darkly funny moments but nothing special . no animal cruelty though - the car wreck that introduces lucky excepted . best line : " perception is nine tenths of the law . "
