julio medem had directed a few movies before this one , with his " key " movies ( vacas , tierra , ardilla roja , amantes del circulo polar ) being basically the same plot rehashed in a different way . the afore - mentioned movies , in my opinion reflected limited directorial skills and zero originality . so , he " moves into the 2000s " with a movie in which you see a constant parade of genitals followed by sex . if you like naked bodies and uncensored sex , go for it by all means . however , don't try to pass it off as " transcendental " or " artistic " like medem did . there is no plot . none . you get more of a plot swirling a bowl of alphabet soup with your spoon . even najwa nimri , usually extremely convincing and talented , phones this one in . what it boils down to is this : if you want to see sex , see this movie . if you want to see a movie with sex and a plot , then keep looking .
