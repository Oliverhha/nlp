up comes off extremely potent and well organised . while the majority of the pacing in the opening half slowly kicks off in first gear , it's not until just after the halfway mark where the tempo gets frenetic and the blood splatter hits the fans . lurking within the haunting context is a cold and creepy tenor , which makes the inventive deaths incredibly blood curdling . the sweeping camera work that's dreamt up can capture some uncanny frames and lighting is kept rather under -
