in alfred hitchock's the trouble with harry , the old geezer pulls our leg a bit , offering a sweet but rather cynical black comedy about how best to dispose of a dead body found by an old sea captain . actually , none of the characters in the film give a hoot about the dead man but are only seeking to protect themselves from scandal . the film , however , is a delightful entertainment , beautifully photographed amidst the vermont fall foliage , and filled with indelible performances by shirley maclaine in her first screen role and veterans mildred natwick , edmund gwenn , mildred dunnock , and john forsyth . even though hitch portrays people at their most loathsome , there is still something so charming about them that you can't help but smile and forgive their all too human foibles .
