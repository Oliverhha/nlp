surf school is without question one of the worst movies i've ever had the misfortune to watch . it's dull , predictable , poorly conceived and painfully unfunny . the characters are paper thin and the plot lurches awkwardly from one yawn - inducing scene to the next . there is not a single original or entertaining moment in the entire 85 or so minutes . this film tries to position itself as a comedy in the vein of american pie but whilst that movie ( and it's first sequel ) succeeded at being both entertaining and engaging surf school fails spectacularly on both counts . this movie should not be watched by anyone ever . as a side note i find it interesting that the people who have left comments raving about how great this movie is have never commented on any other films . draw from that your own conclusions .
