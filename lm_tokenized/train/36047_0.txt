this confused attempt to replicate the success of turkish delight does have some oddball moments that make it slightly entertaining and of mild interest to rutger hauer fans . mainly i'm referring to the bizarro band scene where rutger hops on stage to fake - play a guitar . crazy dancing ensues . the whole thing is reminiscent of some of those wild partying moments in reefer madness . the ending comes about in a perfunctory manner and leaves you giggling rather than feeling introspective . " yeah , right , " was the first thought that came to mind . i wouldn't put this on a must - watch list for anybody , even big rutger fans . instead , pick up the verhoeven box set which contains turkish delight , katie tippel and soldier of orange . you'll feel much better about yourself in the morning .
