this movie should be used as a demonstration to media students as to why you should not use the mic built into the camera to record sound . in many scenes you cannot hear the dialog . there is a lengthy outdoor conversation scene where you can hear a near roar of crickets in some shots , and none in others . so the lesson there is to record some " room tone " background sound to use to solve exactly this kind of problem . of course so much cricket sound was recorded probably because they used the camera mic rather than a boom . so this is an example where a movie is made basically unwatchable because of such poor sound quality . also , don't use you friends as actors just because they are your friends .
