probably the single greatest teen movie ever made ! george lucas ' wonderful coming of age film that takes place one late summer night , is one that inspired several other films , including " dazed and confused " , and " can't hardly wait " . fine performances , superb directing , and wonderful writing make this one a winner that has endured over the years , and has solidified it's position as one of the greatest films of all times ! " american graffiti " is a film that can be funny without being stupid , and touching without being maudlin . this is a movie that should be seen at least once by everybody , it was a great film in 1973 , and is even better today ! a very intelligent and believable film that only seems to get better with repeated viewings , and after seeing it for the first time , you will want to see it again . " american graffiti " is a wonderful film that has stood the test of time , and will stand the test of even more time ! thirty years from now , people will still be looking back on this and remembering how much of a superb film it was , and how many fine films it inspired ! ! ! * * * * * five out of five stars !
