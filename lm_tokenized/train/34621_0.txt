this is probably the most amateurish commercial film i've ever seen . with the possible exception of lance henricksen as long john silver , acting isn't even passable . costumes look more as if they were made with high school production rental in mind , although they are generally immaculately clean even those worn by " pirates " of the lowest rank . colonial wigs look as if they might have been included with halloween costumes at wal - mart . avoid this one at all costs . it hasn't any saving grace except for a few good camera shots . the story lacks even the basic imagination of the robert louis stevenson book . if you want to see a worthwhile and fun version , get the 1950 film with the great robert newton . i'm still wondering who ever told these asylum people they are movie makers .
