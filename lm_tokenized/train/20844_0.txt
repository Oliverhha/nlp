as daniel o'rourke said , you either love little britain or you hate it . i personally love it . my favourite characters are : ray macooney , the man with the spicy food in the restaurant , the woman who works at the university , lou and andy , majorie dawes , mollie sugden's bridesmaid , sid pegg , bubbles and carol beer only to name a few . the characters i didn't like were : kenny craig , dennis waterman and dafydd thomas and the one i absolutely hated was the one where a young david walliams fancies the grandma . a great sketch show that is very popular amongst young children . i didn't like little britain abroad though , it failed to make me laugh . tom baker is brilliant at doing the narration .
