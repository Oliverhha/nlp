only howard hughes could have pulled off something as subversive as the racket . it contains ( as far as i can tell ) the first reference of cocaine use in any american film . the casting is perfect . from the broken - nosed tough to the dangerously sexy gun - moll , you'd be hard pressed to find a better team of hard - boiled noir players . the film is filled with exotic images . the woman with a face on the top of her hat . the violin with a trumpet horn attached to it . the hollywood establishment would have never made this film . like vampyr , which was made in germany with independent money in 1932 , the film is pregnant with the artistic audacity that only non - hollywood money can bring into a production . the racket is worth the search . if you like the gangster genre , you will not be disappointed .
