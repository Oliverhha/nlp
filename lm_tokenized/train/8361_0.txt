let me set the record straight right off the top . " the private eyes " is no comedy classic . but it is a fun film that will amuse adults and make kids laugh and shriek with delight . tim conway and don knotts play a couple of bumbling ( what else ? ) scotland yard detectives assigned to investigate a murder at a castle in england ( actually north carolina ) . while in the castle they stumble across loads of creepy characters and situations . for anyone who has enjoyed this type of film ( " hold that ghost " with abbott and costello , and " the ghost and mr . chicken " come to mind ) , " the private eyes " delivers the goods . of course if you sit there and try to analyze it too deeply you are apt to dislike it . just sit back with the family and have a good laugh .
