great early 70's clothing , a philosophizing folk song ( wow , man , it's like , so deep ) , unspoken drug references , and the undead ! what more could you possibly ask for ? deathmaster really is quarry's movie . his mansonesque portrayal of khorda infiltrating and taking over a commune of lost hippies is eerie . the movie's effectiveness really lies in the context that people can be so easily controlled and fall victim to cults , rather than the fact that khorda possesses their minds because he is a vampire . that quarry is made up to resemble charles manson is a not - so - subtle hint to the movie's theme . terribly dated , this little gem is really only for the 60's and 70's horror movie freaks like myself . i love this movie . i found it at a convention and watching it brought back a lot fond memories of the saturday afternoon chiller theater flicks like deathmaster , lemora , children shouldn't play with dead things , as well as a ton of bizarre british horror movies and even some old giallo that semi - independent stations like channels 5 , 9 , and 11 out of nyc used to run constantly in the late 70's through the mid eighties . if you're in to old trippy horror , this is one to own .
