after his artsy fantasy " the thief of baghdad " freaked everyone out , doug got back to basics with this sequel to his first swashbuckler , " the mark of zorro " - - and concocted a meticulously designed , take - no - chances star vehicle . the sets , cast , and screenplay are all impressive but , as always , it's the fairbanks persona - - an odd combination of pragmatist and dreamer , magician and acrobat , lover and fighter , rogue and moralist - - and his endless bag of tricks , gags , and stunts that sets the film apart from , not only 1920s action spectacles , but those of his successors : jackie chan , james bond , and indiana jones . of his later pictures , perhaps only " the gaucho " tops this one for sheer excellence in filmmaking .
