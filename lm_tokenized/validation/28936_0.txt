i remember going to see this movie on it's release in the odeon leicester square . there couldn't have been more than about 20 people there but it didn't matter because i got so wrapped in this film . it is so good because it is such a fantastic story of the courage of these pilots , the support from their wives and the desperate race to beat the russians into space . a script which never drags , is full of humour and pulls great performances from some of the young actors of the day - sheperd , glenn , quaid , hershey , ward , harris . . . . the list goes on . add to this the superb cinematography and sound . . . what sound ! i have just ventured in to the world of dvd and now have chuck yaeger breaking the sound barrier in my living room . my neighbours may never speak to me again . . . . . . . . . .
