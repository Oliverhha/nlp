this film and " limelight " could well be subtitled as " chaplin on performance art vols . 1 & 2 . " but while " limelight " focuses mostly on the interaction between the performer and the audience , this film seems to explore two different themes relating to performance : its hypocrisy ( the tryout , being told to eat the apple by the director and then being chastised for eating it by the actor ) , and humour in its deficiencies ( note the uproar when chaplin knocks the table off , exposing the hidden man feeding the animals through the hats ) . these are both characteristic of chaplin , walking around in a tramp's outfit on his own hollywood studio , also the two opposite roles in " dictator . " the production difficulties are notorious , and chaplin cleverly adds a tyrannical stage - master into the mix . but still what resonates most with me is an example of chaplin's ideas that great art and , consequently great humour , comes more out of desperation and ignorance , if accompanied with a good heart , than talent and coldly calculated intellect . that and i consistently laugh during this film more than any other chaplin . 4 out of 5 - an excellent film
