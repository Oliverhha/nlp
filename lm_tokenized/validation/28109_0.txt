this was my first asian horror movie watch . i was leary about " reading movies " but this one was very cool . you can understand the movie by the movements , so the subtitles aren't that important ( for lack of a better term ) . very good work with the cameras , using different angles , and bringing out the smallest details . not too gory , much to my disliking , but just enough to keep one interested . good movie all in all , a must see for any asian horror fan . 8 / 10
