a misguided film adaptation of the 1973 broadway smash , and indirectly a remake of ingmar bergman's smiles of a summer night . what should have been a small , character - driven musical gets swallowed up in elaborate sets and costumes , and sadly much of stephen sondheim's score was cut . the stage play and bergman film were set in sweden ; for tax purposes the movie was filmed in vienna , thus losing sweden's " perpetual sunset " which served as the story's sexual metaphor . harold prince expertly directed the stage version , but he's simply all wrong here ; stanley donen or herbert ross would have been better behind the camera . in 1990 pbs telecast the new york city opera's production of night music ( starring sally ann howes and george lee andrews ) as part of its " live from lincoln center " series . that production captured this musical's wit , sensuality and lyricism . a pity it's not available on dvd .
