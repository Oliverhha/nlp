airwolf is a classic , action adventure with a great story , good actors and of course good effects . this is what i believe could be one of the best movies ever made for television . the idea of a supersonic helicopter with 14 firepower options and bulletproof body , just seem to go straight into people's hearts . the movie keeps us watching from start to end and that with great style . airwolf is probably a drama as much as it's an action / adventure . the main character falls in love with a female cia - agent that was working with the same global problem - recovering airwolf from libyan forces . the movie is full of good humor , thanks to ernest borgnine and the characters are strong and well simulated . the helicopter really is something , and the opening scene where they are introducing the chopper , is fantastic . donald p . bellisario is known for his creativity and he has created a lot of very good tv - series / tv - movies , but airwolf is the best one of them - in my opinion . a highly recommended movie . jah
