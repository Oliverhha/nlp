when you put two people like al pacino and russell crowe , you know you have a good thing . this movie , although a bit on the lengthy side at 2 and a half hours , keeps you pretty much involved the entire way . al pacino plays great journalist looking for the big story , but gets personal when russell crowe loses his job as a researcher for a big tobacco firm , and starts to come loose at the edges because of some damaging information he is aware of . the movie provides some very original plot twists , and kept it surprisingly real . they probably could have cut about 30 mins of footage , but after seeing mike wallace freak out . . . i can understand the length . i definately recommend , but get comfy .
