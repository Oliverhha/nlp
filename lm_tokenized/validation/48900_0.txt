let me begin by saying that i enjoyed this movie . it was a light way ( despite a few dark moments ) to spend a couple of hours , but i wouldn't nominate it for any oscars , nor would i compare it to the works of shakespeare as a previous reviewer has . if you wanted to , you could speak as highly of any film . all this would accomplish is to dilute the praise deserving of the real greats ( if you're thinking " armageddon " , stop reading now ! ) let's call " best men " what it really is , a fun little flic . there's some gunplay , a car chase , a guy who gets the girl , a moment of revelation for a would - be lost friend , and a good helping of comic relief , with an ending reminiscent of richard gere in " breathless " . i enjoyed it , but just because a character is endearingly nicknamed " hamlet " , and there's a few ( well acted ) verses recited , " best men " is no shakespearean work . let's call a spade a spade but reserve the real praise for the likes of citizen kane , lawrence of arabia , or even titanic .
