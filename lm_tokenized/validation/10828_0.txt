hunters blood ( 1987 ) is a very entertaining fast paced movie about 5 men from the city who travel 2 hours to arkansas on a weekend so they can go hunting in the forest , drink beer and just have fun ! ! ! things soon take a turn for the worse tho when they find out there's a horrible redneck family who live in the area where they set up camp . these inbred rednecks are cannibalistic and will stop at nothing to kill and torture our city boys ! ! it's a great movie , with plenty of action and some really gory images along the way , the cast are all fantastic too , my favourite is mason who is played by clu gulager but i'm abit biased coz i loved him in return of the living dead ! ! ! i haven't actually seen " deliverance " but hear it's very good , well it would have to be great to be better than this , so if you like these sort of movies then i highly recommend this one . 9 / 10 .
