i'm not a fan of crossword puzzles . despite being musically talented , and in one of the professions ( it ) that the film cites as containing people who do well with puzzles , i just find them frustrating and annoying . if they're too easy , i get bored ; if they're too hard , i give up . that being said , i liked this movie very much , largely because i was drawn to the people from the film who create and work puzzles . these are not the beautiful people of the world , bill clinton aside . they're the geeks , the underdogs , the people who calculated pi on their slide rules in high school for fun , the ones who could recite every ounce of baseball trivia , but never played a single game . they are also a warm and loving community , even in competition , that support , nurture and celebrate each other . they believe in knowledge and success for its own sake ; in striving to be better than you were - - not better than others . the film does a great job of painting a portrait of that community , and treats each person with affection and compassion . it has a great structure , that surreptitiously built and drew me along to the climax without effort . the film was successful in that it touched my heart , and challenged my mind . it made me want to be part of the community - - but only if i didn't have to work the puzzles .
