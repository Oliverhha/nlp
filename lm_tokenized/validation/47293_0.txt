painfully dull euro - horror thats a retread of the karloff mummy set on a cruise ship . ages ago a voodoo priest who was having an affair with a married woman kills the husband in a fight . the woman is decapitated and the priest is put into a wooden coffin and buried . now . the coffin is dug up and put on a cruise ship . the dead priest comes back to life and begins looking for his reborn love at the same time he's chopping off the heads of some of the passengers . its slow . its dull ( with the opening bit about the affair replayed over and over and over again with an all but obscuring red tint ) and it has some of the worst paper mache heads you'll ever see flying all over the place . cinematic sleep meds .
