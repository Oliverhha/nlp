( look for fergie the poodle and the wiener - wagon scene - - - i laughed out loud ! ) suprisingly , there are some serious moments as well , and you may find yourself wiping away a tear . this is when you find your identification with the boys , and the movie becomes yet even more wonderful . all in all , this is my favorite movie because of the wonderfully warm and funny cast and the never - say - die attitude of the lewis brothers . don't miss it !
