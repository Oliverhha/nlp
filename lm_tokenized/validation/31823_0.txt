pardes is a beautiful film . shahrukh khan is stunning as usual . newcomer mahima choudhary was fantastic . usually female newcomers overact like aish is aur pyar ho gaya but then she proved in hddcs that she can act . apoorva agnihotri was very good as a villain . kishorilal ( amrish puri ) is coming back from america to india to see his friend , suraj dev ( alok nath ) . suraj dev has a daughter called ganga ( mahima chouhary ) . he likes her and he wants an indian girl for his " westernised " son , rajiv ( apoorva agnihotri ) . kishorilal has another son who is adopted and who is not so westernised , arjun ( shahrukh khan ) . rajiv and arjun go to india . arjun and ganga are immediately friends . ganga and rajiv get on well too . rajiv says that he likes her and wishes for her to go to america with them and see what life will be like after marriage . ganga goes with her aunt ( himani shivpuri ) . when ganga reaches there she gets a few surprises . . . . . . . . . . this is a must see film . i like the way ganga only felt comfortable with arjun and not her fiancee . ganga could feel that she can only to talk to arjun . whenever he was not around she would get bored and lonely . the song " i love india " is fantastic . the film deserves a 9 / 10 .
