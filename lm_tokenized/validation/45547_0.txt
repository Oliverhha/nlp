no doubt not the best from director jack arnold , but very watchable . a college professor ( arthur franz ) nicks his hand on the teeth of a prehistoric fish and turns into a murderous man / beast . parts of this movie can be very chilling and then a few moments later you want to shout " that's pretty funny " . watch this in tandem with one of arnold's better flicks tarantula and you will have a fun and thrilling evening . franz gives a great performance compared to the rest of the cast that includes : joanna moore , nancy walters , whit bissell and pretty boy troy donahue .
