though the ritz brothers are no three stooges , i'll be damned if they didn't make this one fun movie . here they play 3 inept detectives hired to solve the gorilla murders . the plot is pretty basic fare for these " old dark house " style mysteries , but it was done better than most in the way that it didn't make it's culprit too obvious . most of the cast is hilarious , not just the ritz brothers . patsy kelly , who plays the maid , is great fun in that " spunky 30's woman " kind of way . bela lugosi is also great as the ever suspicious butler . it does have some downfalls , mostly the non - comedic cast who couldn't act their way out of a paper bag . all in all , this is a goofball mystery that comes recommended by me . 7 . 5 / 10
