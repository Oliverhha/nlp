i rated this movie a 10 in the context of other action movies ; it's nowhere near a 10 when compared to classics like casablanca , but in it's genre , it accomplishes exactly what it sets out to do . clive owen stars as " smith " a guy with a past ( that's slowly revealed during the crazy gun fights ) who shows up as a knight in shining armor to a lady who is about to pop a baby and is being chased by baddies , headed up by paul giamatti . after he rescues the kid , he links back up with an old flame , dq , played by monica bellucci . the movie starts out with a bang , quite literally and doesn't settle down the entire time . there's a plot somewhere in the movie , but if you think too hard , it'll disappear in a puff of logic . that being said , the gunfights and car chases are stellar . they are lots of fun , with ridiculous things going on the entire time . just suspend your reason & logic , strap into your theater seat and enjoy the ride . michael davis spoke before the preview and said he was an action movie buff since the beginning and made the movie he wanted to make . as an action movie fan , i thank him !
