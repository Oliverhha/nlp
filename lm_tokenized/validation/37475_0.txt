this movie tries to be intelligent and witty , but totally falls short when it can't decide whether it wants to be a comedy , a satire , a murder mystery . . . etc . the characters are totally uninteresting / unlikeable , and the entire meandering plot is not only boring and predictable , but unbelievable and frustrating . there is no development with any of the multiple characters , and the brief cameos by angelica huston , steve buschemi , and john malkovich cannot carry this disaster of a movie . i was so bored by this cliché attempt to criticize art school culture , that i was seriously considering turning the movie off halfway through , but i stuck it out hoping it would all come together and actually reach a final destination . i wish i would have turned it off . watch ghost world instead .
