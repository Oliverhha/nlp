when i went to see inkheart tonight i couldn't believe my eyes. i cannot believe that this is a result of such a great and wonderful book. it is common knowledge that movies are generally not as good as the books they're based off of... but this is in a league of its own. there was no character development - at all! the story was re-written in a whole knew way, why bother calling it inkheart? let's not talk about the acting. i only understood what was going on because i read the book. a person who is not familiar with the story will not understand this movie. i am heartbroken that the author was even involved in this movie, and did allow this outcome. maybe some books should just not be made into movies.
