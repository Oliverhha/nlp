when people ask me why dil chahta hai is on my list of great hindi movies, i ask myself "what did i love the most about this movie?" well, it's not the script or the acting or the direction, locations, songs and cinematography. all that stuff was really great, but what i loved the most about this movie was how real it was. the three guys were just like any other three guys i know...but thats what makes this movie great. you feel like you know the characters. a brilliant movie with brilliant acting and direction. more movies like this, and bollywood would be producing movies that are actually worth watching. go, dch! 9/10
