shirley temple was in a few - not many - movies that just were not appealing. in most of her films, she overcomes a bad start in life (orphanages, etc.) or overcomes an evil, nasty person (usually edna mae oliver or someone similar) but is seen happy most of the time and singing and dancing here and there. it's when the negative elements of the story are overemphasized (i.e. blue bird, baby take a bow) that her films often lose appeal. that's the case in this movie. "our little girl" is simply too depressing, a negative storyline in which shirley's parents are ready for a divorce. her mother has an affair with a friend and the father is away all the time on business, ignoring the family. when temple ("molly middleton") is happy or cute, she's too cute in here, her sugary personality overdone. meanhwhile, there is only one song and no dance numbers. people buy or rent shirley temple movies to feel good, not to get depressed or weighed down with broken-family soaps. there are plenty of other movies like that.
