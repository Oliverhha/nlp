this film was so bad that i quit before it was finished. i would have watched it in a flash, just because genevieve bujold was in it. i don't know how she could ever have got mixed up in this load of trash. the plot was weak; the acting was terrible; and the script was atrocious. the background music was like a 1940s 'b' movie. the script was filled with inconsistencies. a friend who has never been to the island agrees by telephone to come for the weekend. she finds her way there - including the charter boat trip - without directions. after she arrives, she starts crying with loneliness because she needs her boyfriend - after one day in a vacation hotel with her girlfriend! everything else is as zany, inconsistent and unlikely. it seemed to be trying at bergman, but it got about as close as animal house without the humour. don't waste your money on it. with that music, it's even useless as a sedative.
