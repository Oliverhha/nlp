i love drive. it's one of the best american martial arts films of the past decade (not saying much, i know), being one of the first films to mix hong kong-style action with an american film. mark dacascos' work is top-notch, and he and kadeem hardison play well off each other. brittany murphy nearly steals the show as an ever-so-slightly deranged (but not quite bates-deranged) motel owner, and john pyper-ferguson makes a good villain. whilst definitely a no-brainer, drive is slick, funny, and bursting at the seams with ridiculously complicated action choreography. tongue-in-cheek always beats po-faced seriousness for action films.
