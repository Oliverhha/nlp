this was my favourite film i saw at the calgary international film festival this year. it was amazing. "gloomy sunday" delivers in all aspects of world cinema. the plot is intriguing and keeps your attention. it is an interesting story. that along with great performances from joachim król and erika marozsán make this film even more worth seeing. the location of budapest is beautiful, the camera work is great; the film had comedy, action, suspense and thrills all wrapped up into one. something for everyone. i am definitely looking foreword to rolf schübel upcoming film "blueprint". i'm sure we'll see more of what we got in "gloomy sunday", which is exactly what cinema needs. rolf schübel's "gloomy sunday" 9/10
