i first saw this movie after renting it years ago. i think that it must have been the long version that i saw. - i hated it! hated!!! it was like i had a violent reaction to the whole movie. i told people for years how bad of a movie it was. - my chief complaint at the time (& still) is the mr. sophistication character. all of the strip club footage is very bad & painful to watch. however, i currently have the short version, which i really like. it is a quiet & interesting movie, although it doesn't spend as much time investigating its characters as other cassavetes movies. - it certainly isn't the first cassavetes movie that someone should see, but it is worth watching every once in awhile.
