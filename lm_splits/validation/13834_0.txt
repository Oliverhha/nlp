(he's literally bruno in the scenes spliced in from "strangers on a train.") and helen hayes! she seems to think she's playing mary tyrone in a road company of "long day's journey," and she's pitching it to the back balcony: only morphine addiction and withdrawal could justify the split-second mood swings that occur within a single sentence. thanks to tcm for making my dream come true. and for showing pro-soviet films that are as wonderfully bad in their way as "my son john." apparently the thought of the soviet union turned everyone in hollywood, friend and foe, into hysterical simpletons.
