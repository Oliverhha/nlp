in story, cinematography, and acting, i see the strong influence from jean-luc godard's films in the 80s, especially arty-and-pop "amateur" crime movie first name: carmen. the behavior of edward (damian young) after being tortured is reminiscent of godard's acting; it cracks me up. however, i have to say this film is terrible for the following reasons: student film level of acting, full of meaningless dialogues (e.g. "i'm sorry" "for what?" "i don't know"), and an abrupt ending. please no more clichéd amnesia gimmick to make a plot. please no more "latest" technology gadget--i'm talking about that pathetic floppy disk joke.
