a labor of love that affirms love. i came expecting something cynical and one-sided, featuring caricatures of anti-gay religious zealots--and imagined i would be vaguely entertained by such a depiction. instead i was deeply moved by the vulnerable humanity of the characters. apparently the producers (who also happen to be the 3 lead actors) worked for years to secure financing and otherwise make this project happen. obviously they had a clear vision of what the film could be, and their rapport with director robert cary helped him achieve that vision although he joined the project relatively late. it is rare for a movie to open my sometimes jaded mind and heart. from now on, thanks to save me, i will be able to look for the humanity in gay "recovery" crusaders, rather than condemning them out of hand as bigots. judith light deserves an oscar. save me is her best work.
