with all due respect to the other reviewers here, i feel compelled to disagree completely & whole heartedly on their view of this movie. i'd also love to have some of whatever they were smoking which made this movie so stellar for them. i would point out to those considering watching it that the average is 5.3 stars and in my opinion, that is generous. the script is slow, the dialogue juvenile & tacky and, while the actors do their best with it, the performances are most definitely lacking. if you want a "realistic" feeling movie about infidelity, try "unfaithful" with diane lane and olivier martinez. well written, spectacularly acted & far closer to real than "nina takes a lover."
