the title serves as the antithesis of this movie's existence. it features a deep exploration into the depths of man's focus on racial division as a means of control. it walks the very divide that is inherent to any human; the divide we're genetically predisposed to establishing. what i witnessed was an atypical artistic melding of man and acceptance, forging a deep bond with the essence of equality; no racism, no anti-racism. i cannot adequately explain the premise without ruining the genius that is this movie, so i can't say much more than that! i can truly say that my life changed as a result of watching this film -- heck, it even proffered some hearty laughs along the way! don't let the title fool you, it's something the whole family should see! 10/10 - christopher poole
