this movie was hands down the worst i have seen from director joel schumacher. i actually really like some of his films but was surprised (in a bad way) of this one. the characters don't develop throughout the movie and the plot was just infantile. dialogue was too simple and the occasional german did not fit in with the movie nor with the characters. for the horror fans out there, i would recommend you see night of the living dead (1990). plotwise similar but much more original. and if you want some nazi bad guy story just watch indiana jones.
