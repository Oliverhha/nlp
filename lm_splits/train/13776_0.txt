i saw this when it was released and never forgot it, most of my friends also remember it clearly! it is a wonderful glimpse into the lives of lesbians in the 50s, in many ways different from now - the hidden=ness and secrecy, the butch femme culture - and in many ways so the same - who was going out with who, when, how they fell in love, broke up, wove their lives and made family of their friendships. highly recommend it as an uplifting piece of lesbian history, and a vision of what we younger lesbians may look like as we age. delightful!
