don't go into this movie expecting a modern action adventure film. it's a movie very much of it's time where the bad guys grimaced and the hero's were gentlemen. hong kong martial arts movies were and still are made in just a few days and the storyline is rushed through as the emphasis of the movie is the action, and that's not lacking here as norris stars as the adopted son of a casino owner who is under pressure from the local mafia. it's also one of the few chuck norris titles not yet on dvd and i can't understand why! he's on peak fighting form here only a few years after his karate championship successes.
