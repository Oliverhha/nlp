scary movie adds a new element to the usual *** movie formula - reality. a few scenes from the dubya administration's history are reproduced, and an embarrassing moment from mr cruise's midlife crisis are re-lived in addition to the usual spoofs of various horror and sci fi films. the dubya scenes, nicely done by leslie nielson, were the highlights of the film for me. i'm not a fan of brainless comedy, but this non-formula material made the film tolerable for me. sm4 spends a lot of time with the village, saw and war of the worlds - plus a couple of films i only vaguely recognized... blending the plots cleverly but without adding much to the comedic value in the blending technique. brokeback mountain is also parodied (though i do not understand why this was considered a scary movie). the film is funny at times, but brow-beats its parody of tom cruise's character in war of the worlds. nevertheless, it's a fun little romp and much less grotesque than some of the previous entries in the series. acting - good for what it is. directing - good for what it is. script - ok, but could have been more intellectually challenging (and funnier) cinematography - good for what it is. recommended for fans of the series, but don't expect it to be the funniest.
