for a z-grade movie, this is all right. the acting is better than in a good number of films of this type, and it certainly manages to create mild suspense, (especially in the opening scene). it was a good idea to keep it short; many z movies try to draw out the story way too much, and it turns out terribly. that being said, this is not a good movie and it deserves nothing more than one or two stars. the acting is awful; the dialog is amateurish; the camera work is atrocious; etc. (it is not good enough to fall into the "so bad it's good" category, if that is any indication). thus, i certainly wouldn't recommend this movie unless z movies are your thing.
