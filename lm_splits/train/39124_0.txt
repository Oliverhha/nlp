i don't think i have ever seen a worse ensemble cast. i have seen many a high school play with better acting. all the most basic mistakes are made. it should only be shown to young aspiring actors as a training video in what not to do. students: note the grossly ineffective vocal inflections, the myopic combinations of fake-english-lord and california slurring, the twitching rather than walking, the gesticulating wildly while raising shoulders and rocking back and forth and jerking the head. note with what genius the actor begins in the high register and marches upward every higher toward what someone in rehearsal must have underlined as the key word. ahhh and the key word turns out to always be an adjective and always "but-no-cigar" close to a word oscar surely worried over a bit more. clearly the whole budget was spent on hiring the locations and nothing was left over to hire any talent to stand in front of them... horror is definitely the right genre for this picture but i doubt it was the horror that the director intended.
