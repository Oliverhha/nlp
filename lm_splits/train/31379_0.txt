i thought this supernatural slasher wasn't too bad. there were a few effective bits, and even a few suspenseful moments. the killings with the dagger are cool, and the bit where michael is being stalked around the hospital are good. i also got a scare from the part where virgil suddenly appears at the back window of the car. there are too many boring parts however to make this a good slasher film. if they had used more shots of virgil stalking michael and added some more gore, it would have been quite good. the ending is rather cheesy. still it's better than many slasher films i have seen so if you're a die-hard slasher fan then seek this one out.
