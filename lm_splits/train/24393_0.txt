way ahead of its time. intelligently written, movingly acted, extremely compelling battle scenes for a film of its era. excellent, realistic performances by veronica lake, paulette goddard, sonny tufts, walter abel, hugh ho chang, even george reeves. i don't understand why this film isn't mentioned among the best of american-made war movies. some reviewers here have called it "propaganda," but it is hardly that in the context of the time. i was actually quite surprised by its even-handedness. (yes, there are non-pc references to "japs," as with every other american war movie of the era.) disregard any disparaging critiques posted here and see this film.
