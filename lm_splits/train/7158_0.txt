this is an underrated, decent little thriller. it probably cost peanuts compared to 'speed', and the elevator bit in this film was just as exciting as any i've ever seen. paul mcgann isn't exactly an action hero type. he's just a bit too nice to be an action hero. but how many british actors match up to someone like bruce willis, so i guess he was as good a choice as any. i think it was a good thing that he was a sensitive, slightly wimpy with the asthma, and afraid of heights, because this made him a much better romantic hero. he, boyishly attempting to ask susan lynch out, and she swearing at him, was funny and sweet. the film ending without solving any of the problems of the residents of the building (i mean that mean kid was never punished), but ending with chrissy agreeing to go out with rob, seems to imply that this was just a romantic comedy all along.
