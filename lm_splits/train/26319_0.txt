this is one of the remarkable short comedies that arbuckle turned out at the end of his contract at keystone while he was in new jersey, far from sennett's grasp: quite possibly the best. elaborate, realistic sets, varieties of characterizations -- here he plays an irascible doctor who is jealous of wife mabel's old boyfriend --and the darkest lighting in a comedy until bringing up baby combine to produce a comedy far from keystone's frenetic mold. add in arbuckle's casual gags and the result is an excellent comedy that is still highly watchable -- except, of course, when you are trying to make sense of real-life nephew al st. john's awful mugging and high-speed rowdyism. still, the man can take a fall.
