the storyline of this movie was close to the biblical story of exodus. there were certain embellishments, as to be expected. the animation was not all that great. the effects of the parting of the red sea was good, but the general drawing of the people in the story left a lot to be desired. the music was, in my opinion, the weakest link in this movie. i honestly could not tell you a single line from any song. it was very forgettable -- and rather contrived. for no apparent reason, a song would break out. nonetheless, this was a good movie. it is nice to be able to go to a movie and not be assaulted by vulgarity or sexuality thrown in just to appeal to the masses. i liked the fact that part of the believer's heritage is written about in the mainstream media. overall, this movie got an 8 from me.
