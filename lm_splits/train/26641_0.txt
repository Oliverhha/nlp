seriously, i did cry, but this wasn't an overly sappy film. i read in most of the comments that it was too sappy for some viewers. yeah, you might cry, but rightfully. "mask" is an excellent story. rocky is a teenager who is very horribly disfigured. but he keeps his spirits up and just wants to fit in with the rest of the world. cher plays his mother who is a biker chick. she loves him to death. and rocky makes wonderful friends on the way through his life. it's a wonderful movie. i thought the story was very good and the actors were great. if you enjoyed movies like "powder" or "huntchback of notre dame", i think you would like this. i would highly recommend this film. don't be afraid to cry. 9/10
