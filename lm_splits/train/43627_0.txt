... it's difficult to conceive of a way in which this film doesn't fail, be it the writing, the acting, the direction, the lead girl's lisp and eyebrows or the music. my god, especially the music. strangely banned in the uk until 2002, i picked this up in a bargain bin for £1 after thinking it looked dreadful and fancied a night of junk viewing. i wasn't disappointed, the only remaining mystery being why it was banned in the first place. far from being shocking or distressing, it's merely flaccid and irrelevant. in many ways curfew is like a "funny games" with all the wit, talent and inspiration taken out. however, the exploitative elements lack any real meaning and are showcased in a generic, derivative plot. this very 80s movie really is the bottom of the heap and completely devoid of any merit, other than the fact that it ends.
