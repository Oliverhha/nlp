i have been a robert duvall fan for many years now so when this movie showed up on my dvr i naturally watched it. the beginning was pretty much what i expected but then the tango was introduced along with the beautiful dancing of luciana pedraza. it was then that the movie made a turn and the wonderful tango dancing became the high point of the film. not only can this women dance like no other but she has a natural way of delivering her lines that was simply mesmerizing. the scene in the restaurant when duvall asks if he was younger could he have a chance with her was priceless. i can only wonder why luciana padraza has not been seen since. david glatt
