the one pet peeve i have is when the writing is bad they start throwing in bad language hoping it will get a laugh. i'm sorry but i think if you took out every "f" bomb dropped in this movie... there'd be no dialogue. the movie was disjointed... i know some movies are meant to be avant guard... but even those weird artsy movies make some sense. there were too many things going on, it seemed at times even the actors didn't know what scene they were involved in. the plot between the linda and her boss was just stupid and misplayed by the writers. and just when you think there's finally going to be something amusing... they "shock" you back into realizing the movie sucks. the whole movie was a boring yawn fest. i'm not sure what so many people saw in this film. i felt like burning the movie projector after watching! and the ending, don't get me started... definitely not worth even the matinée prices. the only thing i liked about this movie was sledge hammer star david rasche, and only because it gave me a sledge hammer flashback. i love george clooney and brad pitt, and i usually like the coen brothers... but this is one movie i wouldn't even wait till it came on dvd for. just avoid it.
