yes i love the wedding singer. in fact it ranks among my favorite movies ever. i love this not so much for the story line, although sandler and barrymore are adorable together in this but rather the hysterical return to life in the 80's. since i am a child of the 80's(and retain many a fond memory of that time period) every time i watch this movie it brings me back. this movie is best watched among a group of your closest 80's friends. it is a comedy classic and my only beef with it is it makes me miss the 80's so much! basically, i love this movie! nothing more nothing less. wedding singer is among my top 20 favorite movies ever-one of the best comedies and great music as well! the best of the eighties music(and i do count "you spin me round" as one of them!) this movie pays tribute to a great time period and retains a place in my heart as both sweet/sentimental and roll on the floor funny. watch it if you haven't, you will be hysterical. oh man do i miss the 80's!!!!
