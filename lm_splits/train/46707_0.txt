to help her college's faltering football team, beautiful joan bennett (nan) must seduce hunky football players into transferring to her school. it's the bright idea of gay friend joe e. brown (yates). will the young men discover each other? will ms. bennett find true love among them? will upton win the "big game"? "maybe it's love / eleven men and a girl" is interesting in that it features bennett and brown on their way to becoming successful in the "talkies". they aren't there yet - bennett is unspectacular and brown's shrieks are more annoying than funny; later, he would successfully refine his comic persona. the best scene is early on, when bennett and one of her football players topple their canoe, and emerge soaking wet. *** maybe it's love (1930) william a. wellman ~ joan bennett, joe e. brown, james hall
