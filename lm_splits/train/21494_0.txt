i found this an interesting movie, admittedly a bit slow at points, but if you're out for another "crime gone wrong" or "gangster crime war" film, forget it. several reviews complain about a lack of action. it's not an action film. several reviews complain about a lack of plot. it's more an examination of characters than a story. the movie would have been much better had i been able to hear more of it. the sound editing is truly abysmal. people's shoes make more noise than their raised voices do. i actually stopped and rewound a few times to try and catch what people said. it's not the fault of the actors. it's simply a bad mix job. 7/10
