this is one of those movies that as itself is a gem. for those that have seen it, and "want to know more" i highly recommend getting the book the movie was based upon. the movie is like a "cliff's notes" version of about 5 chapters of the book!!! only complaint was that i think it was a bit too vague on the missionary selection process in the beginning of the movie - but did a great job avoiding the "dogma" aspect and staying true to the story. two thumbs up (even if their my own).
