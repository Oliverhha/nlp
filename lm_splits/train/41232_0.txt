that was the first of a 3 movie deal that clive cussler sold. clive maintained final veto power of much of the deal, a lesson sorely learned from raise the titanic. i can't remember what the other 2 novels are that make up that deal. hopefully, we will see them soon. dirk pitt novels always have had the potential to give us a uniquely american action hero movie series (sort of modern indiana jones/james bond). it is a shame that british actor (rip) richard jordan was mis-cast for the role in raise the titanic. final little side-note. raise the titanic was not the first clive cussler/dirk pitt novel. it was preceded by a couple of years by "the mediterranean caper". that was the shortest of these novels, and in my humble opinion the best written. it would make a great movie even today.
