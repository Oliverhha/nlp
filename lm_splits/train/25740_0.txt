i wanted my husband to come to the scottsdale film festival to see this movie with me, thinking it was going to be a chick flick; hoping he'd see a few images of chauvinistic men seeing the light and then turning themselves around, (i love those gentle reminders). instead my mother and i went and we rode an emotional roller-coaster during this "enlightening" movie. we laughed, we cried, we loathed.... the movie was so unpredictable!! (it was a breath of fresh hollywood air.) mai ling was an inspiration. her performance was beautiful, as was she. it ended up being me who was reminded of compassion and kindness. patrick warburton was fantastic... i hated his character so much initially i wanted him to die, and by the end of the movie my heart did a 180!! well done david beaird!!! this was definitely one movie worth the price of admission.
