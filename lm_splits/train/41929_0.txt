...just some trivia... there are several scenes from denmark (scene 35) is from a small drive-by coffee-stand on their way from copenhagen to elsinore, a sign saying "stop o g tag en k o p" (stop and have a cup)- the joke is on dylan, because k o p is not a cop. you then see a beautiful crosscut from somewhere in england, where robbie and dylan goes into a labyrinth of tunnels to - (scene 51) being from the castle kronborg (of shakespeare's hamlet fame) in elsinore, denmark. we see some people standing along a road, when d.a.pennebaker makes a fast pan, cutting to another fast pan to dylan and manuel, climbing the fortress of kronborg, while a dog is barking. there is a boy and his girlfriend, sitting on a bench on the ramparts, whom dylan and manuel addresses (trying to buy some service from her, actually)- behind them you see kronborg castle...
