if you're looking for an occult lucifer-rising sort of film, this is a definite must-see. omen was good for its time, but seems a little dated. this is not a simple re-do though on an old plot. there are interesting connections made between science and faith, and the ending was well done if a little predictable. the only negative features were the father's role was a little bit wooden (but acceptable), and explanations were a little underdone for exactly how all the supernatural puzzle pieces fit together. i'd recommend it if you're into movies like the prophecy (one but not two), the omen series (i-iii), rosemary's baby, and other such apocalyptic fiction.
