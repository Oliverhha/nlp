the biggest problem with the critically slammed "car pool" is its lack of originality. the affable arnold plays a likeable two-bit carnival owner in need of money who carjacks a gaggle of kids on their way to school in a van driven by paymer. what ensues are the usual chases and sophomoric nonsense. a busy flick with lots going on, "car pool" is a regurgitation of hackneyed stunts and gags which have been done many times before. an okay diversion for the vegetating sofa spud and little more. (d) note - imho arnold's razzie is undeserved. it's hard to look good in a really bad film.
