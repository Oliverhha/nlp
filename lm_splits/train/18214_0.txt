in the kind of movie that plays on airplanes, marlon brando stars as a crooked business tycoon who loves his swedish chocolates and rolodex computer cards. what follows is a thin story about the development and clandestine procurement of a new synthetic industrial material. brando's nemesis is cop george c. scott who tries to uncover the truth. martha keller co-stars, for what that's worth. not much going on here. the most interesting scenes are expectedly whenever brando is on the screen. best line is when brando finds a dead frog in his pool and says, "i hope he's got blue cross."
