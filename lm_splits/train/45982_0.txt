high-minded, big-budget garbage. a ludicrous story of a gutsy, out-gunned crusader taking on an evil gang and the establishment that does business with it. rourke (hopelessly miscast...apparently the union ran out of actors better suited to playing 50-year-olds) somehow is directed to huff and puff in order to make us believe that he's re-fighting the viet nam war in the streets of chinatown. (i guess that this makes year of the dragon a more pretentious iteration of the theme of the rambo movies.) throw in a couple exotic oriental types, john lone, galaxies removed from the greatness of his starring role in the last emperor, and a non-acting actress who is tossed in merely to provide eye candy and to establish rourke's characters bona fides as a non-racist, since he demonstrates that he is willing to sleep with some of them. a snooze-worthy debacle. see it only to satisfy your morbid curiosity.
