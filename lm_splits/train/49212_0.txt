this is a film that may captivate those interested in catholic theology - others should stay away. there is too much chat, as a futuristic (set in 1999) priest visits a monastery in ireland that refuses to give up the latin mass. not very exciting stuff. but there is a marvellous performance from trevor howard, an almost as good one from cyril cusack, and it's nice to see young martin sheen and michael gambon. scenery and cinematography are also nice.
