i saw this film, the director's first attempt, when it debuted at the philly film festival in 2002. i found it to be very disappointing. without giving too much away, the director takes a clichéd bollywoodian approach to very strong themes of sexual abuse, revenge and violence. in the q& a period following the film, the director admitted that some of the events were based on his true experiences, he explained as well that he had wanted to avenge a close friend through the story he portrayed. it is quite clear that he was much too emotionally close to resolving his own demons when he made this film. as a result, the film is extremely naive. being passionate about cinema and world cinema in particular, i walked away incredibly frustrated.
