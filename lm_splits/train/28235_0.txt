the fact is that it was, and still is. to treat slaves as nothing more than mindless creation built for the service of the greater is to make a mockery of the sanctity of life. i can see it through the eyes of the makers because we have house-helps here... and i would say that sometimes, they weren't treated as they are supposed to be. we must never forget that any time we discriminate someone because of their race, social status, culture, economic status, or even gender, is an insult to humankind. i can see the robots here that portrays the feelings of the servants who are simply thrown out when they are no longer needed. there was a scene (spoiler alert) in the movie where astro had a conversation with one of the robots in a sea of scrap metals called the surface.
