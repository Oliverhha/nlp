an evil force called zatteen fertilizes many women from earth with his seed before being eliminated, and their children inherit and perpetuate his power. barbara collins (joanne nail) is the last woman on earth with this gift, and her daughter katy collins (paige connor) has an evil telekinetic power. a battle between good and evil forces settles on earth. "the visitor" has a very promising story and a great cast, with names such as john huston, glenn ford, shelley winters, sam peckinpah and lance henriksen. i bought this unknown vhs expecting a great movie. however, the screenplay and the direction are horrible and completely confused, and it is impossible to understand many parts of the story. the characters are also horribly developed, and in the end, this movie is a complete mess. my vote is three. title (brazil): "herdeiros da morte" ("inheritors of the death")
