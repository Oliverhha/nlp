"beneath still waters" is'nt comparable to some of the masterpieces in which yuzna has been involved as a director ("society") or producer ("re-animator"). it has some serious flaw too, like the quite predictable script (with some really terrible line in the dialogues). but it's worth watching, if only for the spirit of the operation: low-budget euro-horror made with love and care. plus very good locations and suggestive underwater shots, a right amount of gore, a perfect villain and a final orgy that cannot fail to remind "society"'s finale. the entertainment is not missing. i think it's a kind of cinema that should be supported.
