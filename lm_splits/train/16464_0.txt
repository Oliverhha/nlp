i first saw explorers when i was 6, not long after the movie came out and still remains a film that can be watched again. even though this under rated movie by joe (gremlins) dante is for kids i still love watching it and it fulfills all kids dreams who look up at the stars and wonder what the heck is out there. the only bad comment i have on the film is the 2nd half when they actually encounter the aliens. it gets a bit silly but hay i don't think we complained as kids. well worth a purchase on dvd when it comes out.
