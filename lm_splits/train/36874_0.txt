ugh, i hated "diner." a group of college twerps who don't want to grow up sit around and blather on incessantly. i believe we're supposed to think they're cute and lovable, shaking our heads in mock disapproval of their pesky antics. give me a break. nothing's more boring than watching a movie about people who want to avoid all of the responsibilities you accepted a long time ago. maybe when i'm years older and further away from my own passage into adulthood, i will find this movie nostalgic and charming. as it is, i find it unbearable. grade: d
