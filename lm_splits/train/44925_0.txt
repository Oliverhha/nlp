i've been looking for a video of this film for many years. mainly so i can give it as a gag gift to a buddy of mine that saw it with me back in 1973. the bad part was, it had been my idea to see this particular film. to me it has the potential of a good plot and even though there wasn't a big name cast, i really thought it would be okay. i can honestly say that there is not one single redeeming part of this film that would have me recommend it one line in the film is uttered by a man running through a swamp and falls. as he lays there he is looking at a frog sitting across from him and in his mind is saying, "humans are not like frogs....." my verbal comment was, "yeah, a frog wouldn't pay three dollars to come and see this stupid movie!" at which time we left. (my line got a better audience reaction than the whole film) sorry, not something i can recommend.
