bloodrayne isn't original nor is the most epic game you'll find in the market, however i like the execution of it and it's a fun ride from start to finish. you play rayne( voiced perfectly by laura baily), a vampire on revenge for her family and a secret agent for the government during the world war ii era. the game is full of superb game play and of course comes complete with a bullet time option that occurs temporarily. the one liners are funny and the design and scope of the game is dark and moody and gory. should please those who love the gothic/action genre.
