a little slow at first, but once i got into this movie i really enjoyed it. this was my first woody allen movie, and i thought he was funny for the most part. i laughed out loud on more than one occasion (especially in a scene in which he attempts to make a pass at a depressed girl in a museum). diane keaton did a very good job as the lead female character. i especially enjoyed jerry lacy's performance as "humphrey bogart". woody's fantasy conversations with "bogart" were especially enjoyable, and lead to some of the funnier lines/scenes in the movie. a good comedy, one which i would imagine especially woody allen fans would enjoy. i think it also helps if you are a bogart fan, such as myself. 8/10
