'about last night' is the story of a guy and girl who fall in and out of love, repeatedly mixed in with about an hour of raunchy, eye-crossing sex. i think the long-lasting appeal of this movie was soft porn and a popular 80s cast (demi moore and rob lowe in the leads). however, the love story would have been more sincere and convincing had lowe's character not behaved like a prepubescent boy throughout the film. demi's character actually seemed like she was looking for true love, while he just seemed to be an unsure floater. resist the temptation to see it purely for the stars, you've been warned about what you're getting yourself into.
