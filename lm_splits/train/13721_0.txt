"in the company of men" is a simple story that is told in a simple way. a lot of shots are still frames that last for about 10 minutes, which centers the dialog and gives the movie a realistic feeling. and as scary as it may seem, "in the company of men" is very realistic. there are people like chad and howard out there. even though few are really quite as devilish as chad is, many who have a certain amount of power and income behave that way. that's why this movie isn't easy to watch. aaron eckhart, stacy edwards and matt malloy do a great job. the direction is appropriate in its simplicity and the topic is an important one. whoever has worked for a company like the one in the movie one time, will recognize the precipices of the human mind. everybody should see this and learn.
