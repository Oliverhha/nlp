for those who haven't read the book (which is my case as well) you will find a very cute and sad movie, very much worth your time. it has excellent acting, a convincing plot and heart-breaking moments all over the place, and sometimes it even pulls a laugh right out of you. even though the young henry doesn't really convince you that much, the scenes are so adorable that you overlook the petty details. the plot seems to have a few holes, but might be just details that were better explained in the book and the writers/editors/director decided to leave out of the film. this was still hardly ever a problem. to sum up: a cute, sad and enjoyable film for those who like to be moved a bit. 8/10.
