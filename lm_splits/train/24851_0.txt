an excellent movie my today's standards. there are no "f" words, no visual sex, & the only nudity is a brief shot of the top portion of a woman's breasts (no nipples shown) before the camera cuts away to another scene. i would recommend this to any parent wanting a movie that they can show in a mixed audience of teens/pre-teens. if it were made today, it would probably receive a pg rating. robby benson & glynnis o'connor play their parts well. the movie manages to capture the haunting atmosphere of the song by bobbie gentry. i saw it for the first time when i was 15 in 1976 and i still love to watch it.
