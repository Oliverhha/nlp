two thousand and none is a film about an individual facing his death. this is a tragic situation, and the tragedy is present throughout the film. the main character, a paleontologist named benjamin kasparian, however, has a really admirable and stoical attitude towards dying. the contrast between kasparian's bravery and the worry of people close to him is a source of warm and humane comedy. however, the movie seems to be made to provoke thought about the value of life rather than to amuse the audience. it's really a shame that two thousand and none doesn't seem to have found it's audience. people who would love it haven't seen it and most of them never will. myself i was fortunate enough to see the movie in the finnish television, but in order to have access to a dvd version, i had to order it from italy with a name l'uomo di talbot. fortunately the original english audio track was included. anyone with a terminal condition called life should see and enjoy this film and learn from it.
