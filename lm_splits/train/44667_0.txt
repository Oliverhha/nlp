quite possibly the worst film i have ever seen. it was painful. anything that makes a good film was absent - i thought at one point that it was a big joke. and the most ludicrous thing was, there were some big names here! julia stiles (though i loathe her), freddie prinze jr, henry winkler, selma blair - even ashton kutcher, unrecognisable until halfway through (too long to be watching, i might add!!) in a bizarre role as "jim morrison of the doors" for no good reason. it was try-hard, wannabe and unsuccessful in all aspects - even the main characters appeared to be wincing and cringing at times. don't waste your time.
