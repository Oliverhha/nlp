i enjoyed the taped stage play setting and all of the characters were immediately familiar to me, having just viewed the 1942 version. i rarely miss anything that nathan lane does and thought him perfect casting as sheridan whiteside. however...it all seemed a bit forced to me. lane's delivery was good and his mugging very funny, but he was working all the time. it just didn't seem to come naturally. the "banjo" character was just an impersonation of durante and an average one at that. as with the '42 film, i feel this character was superfluous. the overall impression for me was one of a very good amateur company doing their very best to put on a show. i never forgot it was a play, whereas in the '42 version, i got more involved in the story. as for the inter-act comments from the host and hostess, i skipped those immediately (so grateful for dvd technology!). the newsreel footage and swing music were delightful between the acts, though.
