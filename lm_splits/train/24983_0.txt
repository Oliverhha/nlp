an early mike myers film, but i think better than the far more successful austin powers series, though not as good as waynes world. cameos are used to great effect in this scene. phil hartman gets only a couple of minutes screen time but his alcatraz tour guide, vicki, serves up a hilarious anecdote about machine gun kelly and his romantic endeavours. alan arkin, michael richards and charles grodin also provide the laughs in their cameos. the plot is not the best, but it does not take from the gags which the film is really all about. in case you were worried nancy travis plays quite a straight character leaving the humour to myers et al, which is for the best. sorry ms travis.
