a classic gore movie from italian director aristide massaccesi (alias joe d'amato). it's a typical 80's slasher movie, its point of advantage is a great atmosphere and really good special fx. so good that someone claimed, when the movie was on theaters, that the scene with the fetus was a snuff! the fact is that the actress in the scene is serena grandi and the scene is just a great joke! the movie was banned in england for its violence. some really scary scene, with good pathos was realized in this horror flick, together with excellent special effects. even if it has its highs and lows it's one of the best gore movies of the 80's so, if you can't stand lots of blood and gore don't look for this movie!
