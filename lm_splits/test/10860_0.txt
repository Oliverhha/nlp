ignore those who suggest that this is nothing but retread for depalma. on some levels, it surpasses his greatest films and is uniquely original. there isn't a performance in the film that's weak and the storytelling (both through script and camerawork) unfolds like a dream. bloody where it needs to be and tragic in places you don't expect, "the fury" is one of my all-time favorites. i saw it as a young kid in the theater and hope one day to see it again on the big screen.
