this movie came up tonight during a dinner conversation with friends so i googled it and ended up here. i enjoyed lurking and reading people's comments. and then i read this: "there's nothing here that i believe would disturb a child..." uh, sure. my mother, older sisters and i saw this movie in the theater in 1977, so i was 4 or 5 years old. to this day i vividly remember seeing the movie ...as well as my incessant, inconsolable sobbing at the (spoiler) previously mentioned rock crushing scene. yes, yes, it was a different time back then and probably kids today are exposed to much worse day in and day out. and yes, i was a young kid. but *no way* is this a typical g movie. watch it first and decide for yourself before considering showing it to your kids.
