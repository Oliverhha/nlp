as a rottie owner i have a lot of experience with peoples reactions to my choice of breed. "oh, he's scary!", "those are vicious dogs" and "will he bite me?" are common things i hear on a day to day basis. yet nobody has ever asked me if my dog will poison them, electrocute them or watch them getting it on! why do i bring this up? because the killer dog in this movie rarely kills anyone the way a normal dog would! for all the killing she does she could just as easily have been a toy poodle! this movie is just ridiculous, and not in the "good" way. very slow moving story, and considering the subject, very tame deaths. all of the actors are bad, even yvonne de carlo. the only bright spot here is the rottweiler greta, who has been fantastically trained to do some really amazing stunts.
