watched the original men behind the sun, and then this sequel. this was english dubbed though. cantonese version may have been way better. anyway, from the 1st 2 minutes i felt this is some awkward attempt to ride on the "success" of the first movie. story about japanese biological weapons development with some off-topic (maybe i missed something?) autopsies. some shots are clearly ripped from the first one, like the liquid nitrogen demonstrations and when the leader dude stands in front of the japan flag & his "army". if you haven't seen the original movie, you may like this, anyway i was so much disappointed i recommend watching "men behind the sun" or even "mermaid in a manhole" instead, and skip this one.
