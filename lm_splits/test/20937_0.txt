to start, this is hilarious. although some of the jokes may be grotesque and stupid, you can't help but crack up. jim carrey's rubber-like face is funny on its own, but add great jokes and it's perfect. the acting is mediocre, and the story is...well...basically just put there to carry along the jokes. this is the ultimate highlight of jim carrey's career, and his funniest. this is the funniest movie of all-time, with "planes, trains and automobiles" in a close second. 9/10
